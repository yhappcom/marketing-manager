## 1. What is established, and at which evidence level

This file consolidates already-recorded principles and source-bound findings. **Reading, source inspection, synthetic checks, historical public snapshots, and actual live operating evidence are distinct levels.** No live campaign success, AdMob revenue, declaration approval, community permission, financial-record correctness, or LogMate import/recovery readiness is established merely by this synthesis.

| Layer | Reusable judgment | Limit / evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience and distribution | Specialist fit and retained use outrank generic reach; narrow audiences create a finite acquisition ceiling. | Obtain actual segment-specific queries, participation permissions, task completions, retention and referrals; do not invent addressable audience size. |
| Cash-light marketing | Prefer Store discovery, useful specialist answers, maintained owned reference pages, reviews/referrals and only audience-fit social channels. | Labor, moderation and measurement costs are real; zero media spend is not zero operating cost. |
| Store truth | Match country/locale-specific promise to *served* binary, in-app workflow and first/repeated value. | Public listing, source branch, upload artifact and operator Console values are different evidence. Metadata experiments require sufficient eligible traffic. |
| Community | Complete accurate native answers without product links; obtain action-specific permission for mention, link, recruitment or solicitation. | One visible historical post or moderator precedent is not future blanket permission. |
| Owned references | Maintain authoritative, versioned specialist answers, not a content-volume quota. | Distinguish estimate, revised issuer source, investor-specific broker finality and unknown data. |
| Measurement | Trace acquisition source → first specialist job → repeated specialist value → retained qualified cohort → valid finalized revenue. | Attribution is not incrementality; sparse/suppressed data is not zero; CTR, installs and Store conversion are intermediate. |
| Monetization | Maximize sustainable ad revenue per **retained specialist** under hard task, latency, accessibility, consent, validity and policy guardrails. | Estimated revenue is not finalized; no visible enforcement is not proof of valid traffic; impressions are not a sufficient success metric. |
| Governance | Keep product truth in its owner repo; Marketing owns audience/claim/channel/measurement; Design Studio owns visual/interaction, Web Manager owns site/PWA infrastructure. | Reuse peer evidence without copying an implementation contract into a marketing approval. |

## 2. Common evidence and decision vocabulary

- **SOURCE**: exact source, version/date and observed scope. **SYNTHESIS**: transfer judgment grounded in sources. **MARKETING JUDGMENT**: explicit recommendation and constraints. **PROJECT DECISION**: adopted product/marketing direction, not implementation evidence.
- **PASS (scoped)**: only the named test and environment passed; explicitly list what remains untested. **OPEN/HOLD**: no production approval without required evidence. **SUPERSEDED**: retain earlier assertion and cite the corrective evidence; never erase chronology.
- **UNKNOWN / NOT ACCESSIBLE / SUPPRESSED / MISSING / ZERO** are separate states. Absence of an operator screenshot, Reddit rules page, paid-event callback or broker statement cannot be converted to an affirmative finding.
- **Independent research file** means a distinct tracked `live_data/**/*.md` study, excluding README; **research-number sequence never determines existence**. Verification requires actual file paths.

## 3. Reusable operational ledgers (schema, not fabricated data)

| Ledger | Minimum fields | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim Registry | Product/locale/channel; exact claim; source release/served hash; proof; last checked; state; next validation | No stronger claim than demonstrated product behavior. |
| Community permission | Community/thread; current rules URL/date; actor identity; exact activity (answer/mention/link/recruitment); moderator evidence; expiry; disclosure | No transfer of permission across communities or activity types. |
| Owned-reference inventory | Topic; issuer/official URL; publication/revision; retrieval time/hash; source tax/calendar year; supersession; editor/recheck date | Publishing frequency is not a KPI; source class and missingness preserved. |
| Store routing | Search intent/locale; default/CPP/CSL destination and live inventory; message/feature proof; eligible traffic and test limits | App Tags/CPP/CSL are not assumed from public listing; avoid weak sparse tests. |
| Source quality | Primary/secondary; precise URL/version; retrieved bytes and document hash where needed; conflict and negative checks | Row fingerprint is not issuer-document provenance. |
| Repeated specialist value | Anonymous qualified segment; source/referrer; first specialist job; repeat interval; retention; failure/abandonment | Activation ≠ installed, passive events ≠ repeated value. |
| Protected workflow | Entry/import/search/backup/sync/recovery task; ad placement and geometry; consent; error/fallback; completion/latency; cooldown | No revenue gain may block or destabilize the core task. |
| Revenue validity | App/platform/surface/ad-unit; consent; request/load/error; impression; paid event (currency/micros/precision/source); settlement period; finalized amount; serving-policy history | Do not treat estimated, suppressed, unattributed or invalid-traffic-related values as final yield. |

All ledgers are **templates/decision contracts** until filled with authorized production evidence. Do not place personal pilot/financial records or operator credentials into Marketing Manager.

## 4. MintTap — findings that can inform decisions

**D1 / financial and Store claims.** The inspected `yhappcom/yieldmax_tracker@1.0.29` manual portfolio and transaction-recording paths did not show brokerage order execution; this is narrower than asserting all shipped functionality. Google Play's combined `Stock trading and portfolio management` financial declaration option is the documented *recommended truthful classification* for the observed feature scope, **not an observed Console submission**. Ordinary Store distribution must be separated from finance-restricted **optional Promotional content**; do not seek eligibility by mislabeling actual investment features. [Public claim and declaration study](live_data/minttap/d1_public_play_listing_claim_audit_2026-10-10.md).

The release selector contains **43 unique currency codes including USD (42 non-USD)**. This is neither proof of live FX availability for every currency nor proof of the public “42 currencies” claim's intended denominator. The FX daily refresh path has code-level source-validation/freshness and job-coupling acceptance gaps, but no demonstrated live outage. [Selector](live_data/minttap/d1_release_currency_claim_selector_validation_2026-10-21.md) · [FX path](live_data/minttap/d1_fx_daily_refresh_dependency_2026-10-22.md).

**D2 / advertising.** Six identified banner placements occur across Home + five fixed-bottom screens; five fixed-bottom placements share one screen-used ad unit, so network totals alone do not identify surface-level effects. Three native banner-unit getters were configured, with only two directly screen-used in inspected paths; disabled web AdSense placeholders are not served native ads. Public `app-ads.txt` publication/identity checks passed in the dated audit, **not** operator AdMob readiness or revenue validity. App-local request-state caching, missed re-eligibility, navigation cooldown, unstable initial geometry and missing per-surface impression/paid-event callback instrumentation are **source-level acceptance gaps**, not observed consent breach, click fraud or revenue loss. [Surface map](live_data/minttap/d2_ad_surface_app_id_source_reconciliation_2026-10-10.md) · [UMP/test-ad matrix](live_data/minttap/d2_ump_banner_state_transition_acceptance_2026-10-21.md) · [geometry](live_data/minttap/d2_banner_failure_ux_gate_2026-10-21.md) · [public authorization](live_data/minttap/d2_public_app_ads_authorization_chain_2026-10-09.md).

**D3 / community.** Repeated YieldMax threads demonstrate ROC missingness, duplicate rows and estimated-versus-final confusion. A complete, fact-checked, link-free answer is a useful contribution **only within current community rules**. A past public MintTap post without an outbound URL and old megathread guidance do **not** authorize current product mentions, app links, recruitment or direct promotion; r/stocks restrictions do not define r/YieldMaxETFs rules. [Community pilot](live_data/minttap/community_permission_pilot_v1.md) · [ROC thread](live_data/minttap/d3_current_thread_roc_missingness_2026-10-10.md) · [permission inventory](live_data/minttap/d3_offplatform_claim_permission_inventory_2026-10-23.md) · [thread routing](live_data/minttap/d3_yieldmaxetfs_megathread_routing_evidence_2026-10-10.md).

**D4 / ROC source.** Preserve issuer 19a-1 estimates, issuer ICI/Box 3 final-source classifications, supplemental tax inserts, Form 8937 action/basis records, broker Form 1099-DIV and missing/unknown as different source states. An archived 2025 ICI workbook was examined (1,352 rows), but its existence does not prove current revision/issued-byte identity or investor-specific final tax treatment. The release `finalSourceHash` is **a normalized row fingerprint, not the issuer document hash**. CLI `--apply=false` was observed to activate the write path; dry-run default does not authorize production import. Preserve Box 3 dash-vs-zero and date/year boundary. [ROC authority](live_data/minttap/roc_authority_provenance_audit_2026-10-06.md) · [ICI fixture](live_data/minttap/d4_ici_year_boundary_fixture_2026-10-19.md) · [CLI](live_data/minttap/d4_roc_import_cli_write_safety_2026-10-24.md) · [provenance](live_data/minttap/d4_final_source_hash_provenance_boundary_2026-10-23.md).

## 6. Research admission and transfer rule

General theory remains frozen by default. Reopen research only for a material new authoritative rule, an inaccessible-to-current-framework live decision, production contradiction, or genuinely reusable operating gap. Otherwise **advance a D1–D10 gate with the strongest accessible evidence**; if an operator account cannot be reached, move to the next decision rather than generating adjacent theory. On meaningful change: update the owning independent study or peer canonical source, then [the decision register](PRODUCTION_DECISION_REGISTER.md), then the short [STATUS](STATUS.md); record evidence obtained, blocker, next admissible test and closed subgate. Preserve `SUPERSEDED` claims explicitly and do not translate research fixture PASS into production PASS.

**RELATED DOMAIN CHECK / HANDOFF:** Marketing owns claim, channel, permission and revenue/retention decision. MintTap/LogMate product owners own shipped build, code, data and acceptance; Design Studio owns ad geometry/accessibility; Web Manager owns hosted website/PWA/Files-operating constraints; Play Console/AdMob/App Store Connect operators own account-side declarations, policy history, revenue and store inventory. This synthesis changes no such owner's artifacts or approvals.
