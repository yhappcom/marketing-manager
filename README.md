# Marketing Manager

Canonical knowledge base for app marketing, launch, growth, measurement, and long-term market strategy across yhappcom products.

## Mission

Build professional marketing judgment that can be applied to real Apple App Store and Google Play products—from first-principles market understanding through positioning, launch, acquisition, activation, retention, monetization, measurement, experimentation, and portfolio-level growth strategy.

This repository is not a collection of promotion tips. It is the long-term operating memory of the Marketing Manager.

## Canonical files

- `AGENTS.md` — operating rules and evidence standards
- `LEARNING_ROADMAP.md` — beginner-to-expert curriculum
- `STATUS.md` — current stage, completed work, gaps, and next priorities
- `research/` — integrated research checkpoints and reusable marketing knowledge

## Working principle

Study deeply, report compactly, preserve reusable knowledge, and judge success by whether the research improves a real product decision.

## Relationships

- `yhappcom/design-studio` — reusable visual, type, color, layout, interaction, and web-design expertise
- `yhappcom/web-manager` — website architecture, content, SEO, web analytics, release, and operations
- product repositories — canonical source for product behavior, constraints, users, release state, and implementation

Marketing Manager owns market, customer, positioning, go-to-market, acquisition, lifecycle, growth economics, experimentation, and marketing-performance judgment. Cross-domain overlap is used for validation rather than duplicated ownership.
