## Latest validated addition — 170
Privacy/consent state is part of ad-supply identity, not a one-time onboarding checkbox. For EEA/UK/Swiss traffic, personalized-ad eligibility depends on the required certified CMP/TCF path. Google's current guidance supports TCF v2.3; v2.3 is mandatory for TC strings generated on or after 2026-03-01.

Consent information is current state: UMP guidance requires updating consent information at app launch, and applicable users need a way to revisit/withdraw choices. Consent is multidimensional (for example ad storage, personalization, ad user data and analytics storage), so a single `consented` boolean is insufficient for monetization/measurement diagnosis.

Mediation/ad-partner configuration belongs inside the same integrity boundary. AdMob's account-level fallback-message coverage is a safety net, not proof that the app-level CMP integration is healthy.

CH0–CH5: `regulatory/traffic identity → CMP/message integrity → current choice integrity → ad-request eligibility → monetization/measurement observability → sustainable decision`.

Preserve `message shown ≠ consent granted`, `past consent ≠ current state`, `consent granted ≠ every vendor/purpose eligible`, `fallback CMP ≠ healthy integration`, and `missing revenue with unresolved consent ≠ demand failure`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Native attribution improves observability but not incrementality. Sparse evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and discovery surface second. Store/platform approval or metadata is not proof of demand or product-market fit. Ad supply authorization is not revenue proof. Consent is not a one-time boolean and consent compliance does not create placement permission. A foreground event is not an eligible app-open exposure; a loaded app-open object is not necessarily fresh/showable; a navigation tap is not a natural interstitial transition; a native/banner/collapsible/reward event is not automatically a revenue or value event. Higher request/refresh/full-screen exposure volume or short-term ad yield is not sustainable growth if it delays first/restored useful value, interrupts professional work, manipulates privacy choice, or degrades core-task completion and repeated useful use. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from clearly identified, privacy-eligible, natural or genuinely voluntary states that do not materially degrade that value.
