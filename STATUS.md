## 065–067, 076–079 — Store routing, sparse experiments, and event discovery
Intent-specific Store pages are routing infrastructure, not automatic ASO wins. Sparse traffic is both acquisition inventory and learning sample. Randomization requires an evidence budget and precommitted stopping/interpretation rules. MintTap and LogMate remain premature for Store randomization.

Apple CPP and Google Play CSL support deterministic specialist-intent routing before randomization when search intent and the underlying promise are earned. Apple In-App Events add truthful time-bounded discovery/reactivation. Google Play Promotional content is not a symmetric default counterpart: it is available to all games but only eligible apps, and current Google guidance restricts certain finance/investment-related promotional content. MintTap therefore must not plan this as growth inventory; LogMate remains future-conditional on Console eligibility and real event truth.

Canonical distinction:
`CPP/CSL = stable intent routing | Apple IAE = truthful time-bounded discovery/reactivation | Google Promotional content = eligibility + category + event-truth gated | PPO = causal creative experiment when evidence budget exists`.
