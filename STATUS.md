## Latest validated addition — 243
Weak ad revenue must be localized along the delivery chain before changing density, floors or mediation: `eligible opportunity → request → source participation → load/fill → display opportunity → impression → paid event → reconciled revenue`. Google Mobile Ads response information exposes adapter/source, errors and latency; Ad Inspector can inspect test requests, waterfall/bidding execution, adapter initialization, source outcomes and supported privacy signals. Therefore aggregate revenue or eCPM alone is insufficient diagnosis.

Mediation expansion is not a default revenue tactic. A bidding source can lose normally; `no ad returned` has multiple possible causes; an added network/SDK creates privacy, maintenance, binary-size and reliability costs. Sparse niche traffic further requires controlled observation rather than short-window configuration churn. Product flows must never manufacture extra ad opportunities merely to increase auction volume.

FC0–FC5: `eligible-opportunity identity → delivery-stage identity → integration/diagnostic integrity → supply/mediation integrity → sparse-evidence/change-control integrity → sustainable-value decision`.

Preserve: `low revenue ≠ insufficient density`; `low eCPM ≠ add networks`; `no-fill ≠ broken SDK`; `losing bid ≠ bad network`; `high fill ≠ good UX`; `more requests ≠ sustainable revenue`; `test-device success ≠ production economics`; `short-window country result ≠ global configuration rule`.
