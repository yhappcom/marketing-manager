## Latest validated addition — 154
Apple Featuring Nominations is a genuine zero-media-cost earned-distribution mechanism in App Store Connect, but submission only places a launch/update/content moment before Apple's editorial team; it does not imply selection, reach or acquisition. Apple currently supports nomination metadata for platforms, relevant countries/regions and localizations, related apps, approved/published In-App Events, up to five supplemental URLs including TestFlight public links, and helpful details such as accessibility/inclusivity and unique app/team attributes.

Apple's nomination reference says plans should be finalized at least three weeks in advance. Therefore featuring nomination belongs in release planning, not post-release publicity. The scarce input is a credible material product moment: routine maintenance, cosmetic changes and marketing-only repackaging should not be inflated into nominations simply because submission has no media cost.

BR0–BR5 governs `moment identity → editorial-worthiness → truth/proof integrity → readiness/localization → submission semantics → outcome integrity`. `submitted`, `selected`, `featured/reached`, `qualified acquisition`, and `core value` remain separate evidence states. Apple editorial selection is never evidence that MintTap financial claims or LogMate aviation/regulatory claims are correct.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not usable merely because a platform exposes the feature. Surface/account eligibility and category policy come before creative production; for MintTap, current Google Play Promotional-content finance restrictions stop that path before asset creation. Native Store experiments also consume scarce organic traffic: if current traffic cannot resolve a decision-sized effect, not running the test is valid. Apple Featuring Nominations add an earned-editorial path, but submission is not selection and selection is not proof of product, financial or regulatory correctness; nominate only substantial evidenced moments and plan them at least three weeks ahead where practicable. Intent-specific pages must begin with evidenced specialist demand, preserve truthful proof and produce qualified users who reach the promised core value. Platform recommendations, featuring or AI-generated listing copy are hypothesis/distribution aids, not evidence of demand, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sparse, censored, underpowered or inconclusive evidence remains unknown/inconclusive rather than being promoted into a growth claim. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions/eCPM/short-run revenue; it requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value.
