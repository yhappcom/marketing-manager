## Latest validated addition — 190
App-ads.txt is Store-linked monetization identity infrastructure, not a one-time website file. AdMob derives app-ads.txt discovery from the developer website exposed by the production store listing, so Store metadata, developer-domain control, crawler reachability, seller/account declarations and platform verification form one supply-chain identity. File deployment or browser visibility is not equivalent to crawler discovery or AdMob verification; a verified file is not a revenue, consent, placement or traffic-quality guarantee. Mediation/seller changes trigger authorization revalidation.

DB0–DB5: `app identity → Store-domain identity → discovery/crawlability → seller authorization integrity → platform verification state → revenue/supply-chain decision`.

Preserve: `developer website field ≠ cosmetic Store metadata`, `file deployed ≠ crawler discovered`, `HTTP 200 ≠ AdMob verified`, `app-ads.txt present ≠ seller records correct`, `authorized inventory ≠ demanded/filled inventory`, `verified authorization ≠ consent/placement compliance`, `verified authorization ≠ revenue guarantee`, and `mediation change ≠ authorization remains automatically current`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, pre-registrations, pre-orders, installs, impressions, CTR, rewarded-ad acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Store conversion, web traffic and ad yield are not terminal objectives. Platform Store/Search/ad objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window. Reddit promotion is governed per community; permission does not guarantee reachability, and owning a community does not create a right to mobilize its members into third-party communities. Owned-web publishing must serve distinct specialist demand rather than manufacture query coverage. Search Console visible queries are privacy-filtered/coverage-limited evidence rather than a complete demand census. Impression-level ad values preserve precision/provenance and remain distinct from finalized publisher earnings. Ad inventory must fit genuine product states: loading, transition and screen-space semantics cannot be manufactured merely to increase impressions. Native advertising must remain visibly and behaviorally separate from specialist product content. Rewarded advertising must remain a voluntary exchange for incremental value rather than a toll on baseline utility. Ad clicks must represent genuine interest rather than spatial or temporal interception; traffic quality and serving health are part of sustainable revenue evidence. App-ads.txt establishes an authorized programmatic supply path only when Store identity, developer domain, crawler discovery and seller records remain coherent; it does not substitute for demand, fill, consent, UX quality or revenue reconciliation. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states through authorized supply paths within deliberate interruption, separation, voluntariness and traffic-quality boundaries.
