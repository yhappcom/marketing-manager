## Latest validated addition — 231
Google Search documentation updated in September 2026 exposes two distinct zero-cost audience-retention/discovery mechanisms relevant to the owned-web/community flywheel.

Search profiles aggregate a claimed creator/publisher's content across website and social identities. Google states that when readers follow a Search profile, content linked on that profile is more likely to appear for that audience in Discover. Preferred sources is separate: users select an eligible publication domain/subdomain, making its content more likely to appear in Top Stories and allowing preferred-source highlighting in supported AI Mode/AI Overview experiences. Preferred sources is globally available for Top Stories in Google Search languages; AI-feature highlighting additionally depends on the site being included in Search generative AI features in Search Console. Only domain/subdomain sites, not subdirectories, are eligible in the source-preferences tool.

Neither mechanism is a ranking guarantee or a reason to manufacture content. Google also states that AI Search does not require special AI schema/files; normal Search eligibility, indexing, snippet eligibility and people-first SEO fundamentals remain applicable.

EQ0–EQ5: `surface identity → eligibility/claim integrity → audience-choice integrity → content-source integrity → measurement integrity → sustainable specialist-value decision`.

Preserve: `Search profile ≠ Preferred source ≠ ordinary organic Search`; `feature existence ≠ exact brand eligibility`; `follow/preference ≠ ranking guarantee`; `traffic ≠ specialist value`; `AI visibility ≠ need for AI-specific content production`.
