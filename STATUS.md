## Latest validated addition — 232
Apple Accessibility Nutrition Labels are now a material Store truth/discovery surface on Apple OS 26+ product pages. Apple explicitly states that users can include accessibility features such as VoiceOver or Larger Text in App Store search queries and that apps declaring corresponding support are considered more relevant for those searches.

This is not an ASO keyword loophole. Apple requires users to be able to complete all common tasks using a claimed accessibility feature before the developer indicates support. Device families are evaluated separately; significant accessibility bugs can invalidate a declaration. Undeclared device support remains visible as not indicated rather than disappearing. An app-specific accessibility URL can also be published.

Apple currently describes reporting as initially voluntary but says it will become required over time for new-app/update submission. The authoritative material reviewed for Research 232 does not provide a specific future enforcement date; do not invent one.

ER0–ER5: `surface identity → common-task identity → feature-verification integrity → metadata/claim integrity → discovery-interpretation integrity → qualified-value decision`.

Preserve: `accessibility implementation ≠ accessibility claim`; `partial flow support ≠ common-task support`; `label ≠ ranking guarantee`; `search relevance ≠ install`; `install ≠ accessible specialist value`. Accessibility is product quality first and zero-cost discoverability second.
