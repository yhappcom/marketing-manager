## Latest validated addition — 121
Canonical principle: **An app-open ad is legitimate only when it monetizes a wait state that already exists; it must not manufacture a wait state or tax access to core value.** Google currently recommends delaying the first app-open ad until after users have used the app several times, showing it while users would otherwise wait for loading, and not showing a late-arriving cold-start ad after main content is already available. Google also identifies frequent-open apps as the strongest fit and recommends considering other formats when that usage pattern is absent.

AM0–AM5 governs fit. AM0 covers harmful/noncompliant launch behavior. AM1 is inventory chasing without natural-wait evidence. AM2 is technically plausible but lacks product/economic evidence. AM3 requires prior successful core value, a genuine pre-existing wait, no first-start obstruction, cancellation when content is ready, no adjacent/overlapping ads, an explicit cap, U-class revenue evidence, AC privacy parity, and first-value/useful-return guardrails. AM4 adds observed incremental retained-user economics without material downstream harm. AM5 is a reusable portfolio registry.

MintTap defaults to **no app-open ad until AM3 evidence exists**. LogMate is stricter: do not use app-open during launch, flight-record entry, restore, offline recovery, import or backup, and never manufacture a splash screen to create inventory.
