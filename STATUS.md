## Latest validated addition — 125
Canonical principle: **ordinary navigation is not interstitial inventory.** A candidate must sit after a recognizably completed task at a genuine pause that already exists without advertising.

AQ3 requires completed task state, no unsaved ambiguity, a non-urgent next action, immediate continuation when no ad is available, protected-workflow exclusions, explicit cap/cooldown, test QA, privacy/age compatibility, an eligible-opportunity denominator and an owner. AQ4 requires revenue evidence to remain compatible with first value, task completion, useful return, abandonment, reputation and click-quality guardrails.

MintTap Save/navigation events are not automatically eligible. LogMate flight-save is not automatically eligible because verification, another leg, correction or duty review may immediately follow.
