## Latest validated addition — 152
Native Store experimentation is now treated as consumption of scarce specialist traffic, not as a free optimization loop. Apple PPO currently supports one test at a time, up to three treatments, randomized persistent treatment exposure, traffic allocation, duration estimation and a 90-day maximum. Apple recommends waiting for a treatment to be declared better/worse with at least 90% confidence before applying/stopping on that basis. Its five-first-download result-visibility threshold is explicitly not promoted into a sample-size rule.

Google Play Store Listing Experiments currently support one default-graphics experiment or up to five localized experiments simultaneously, up to two variants against control, target metrics, traffic allocation, MDE, confidence and native result states. Google provides estimated data/time requirements and recommends isolating asset changes when causal interpretation matters.

BP0–BP5 governs `decision identity → traffic/power feasibility → isolation/contamination → native statistical semantics → stopping discipline → downstream value`. A strategically important hypothesis may be currently untestable. Numerical conversion leadership without adequate native evidence is not a winner. Inconclusive tests are preserved as information-limit evidence rather than rerun until a favorable result appears.

BN/BO intent routing and randomized Store experiments remain analytically separate: routing asks which proof should meet which evidenced intent; experimentation asks which treatment performs better within an eligible population.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not successful because it has more variants or a higher conversion rate in isolation. Native Store experiments also consume scarce organic traffic: if current traffic cannot resolve a decision-sized effect, not running the test is valid. Intent-specific pages must begin with evidenced specialist demand, preserve truthful proof and produce qualified users who reach the promised core value. Platform recommendations or AI-generated listing copy are hypothesis/production aids, not evidence of demand or claim authority. Sparse, censored, underpowered or inconclusive evidence remains unknown/inconclusive rather than being promoted into a growth claim. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions/eCPM/short-run revenue; it requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value.
