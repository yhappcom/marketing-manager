## Latest validated addition — 196
Research 196 closes a Google Play zero-cost re-engagement gap. Promotional content is a Store-native distribution surface for eligible apps, but Google limits the intended content to fresh, timely user-facing offers, limited-time events and major updates. General service descriptions, evergreen content and routine events are not valid substitutes for real novelty.

DH0–DH5: `eligibility/surface identity → novelty/event truth → specialist-value relevance → destination continuity → distribution/measurement integrity → qualified re-engagement decision`.

Preserve: `Promotional content ≠ evergreen Store metadata`, `release occurred ≠ promotion-worthy event`, `approved event ≠ featured event`, `featuring requested ≠ featuring granted`, `Store exposure ≠ qualified acquisition`, `event click ≠ successful re-engagement`, `deep-link open ≠ promoted-value completion`, `more events ≠ more sustainable organic growth`, and `zero media spend ≠ zero production/measurement cost`.

For sparse niche apps, Promotional content is event-driven rather than calendar-driven. Featuring requests, where available, are scarce distribution opportunities and should be reserved for the highest-confidence specialist-value events. Deep links should preserve promise-to-value continuity, and outcomes should be judged by promoted-value completion and repeated core value rather than Store clicks alone.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, installs, tag count, promotional-event count, star averages, review counts, impressions, CTR, rewarded acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised specialist value. Store discovery metadata, Apple-created tags and Google Play Promotional content must remain truthful representations of supported specialist jobs, not mechanisms for manufacturing broad relevance or artificial novelty. Advertising contributes only incremental, reconciled revenue from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states through an authorized and active supply path. Product release readiness, Store-discovery integrity, re-engagement-event integrity, feedback integrity, ad-supply activation readiness and monetization-experiment readiness are separate tracks.
