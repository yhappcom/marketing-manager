AW3 requires legitimate active channel ownership, per-account platform properties, separate native-vs-Google discovery metrics, specialist-intent query review, durable-vs-trend content classification, annotations for title/caption changes, comparable cross-platform analysis and explicit downstream attribution boundaries.
