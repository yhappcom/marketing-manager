## Latest validated addition — 194
Research 194 closes a Store-feedback operating gap: sparse professional apps must not compensate for small audiences by manufacturing ratings/reviews or interpret a visible star average as direct product-quality evidence.

Apple's summary rating is territory-specific and can be reset with a new version, but written reviews persist, the old summary cannot be restored after release, and Apple recommends reset sparingly. Apple recommends rating requests after a satisfying completed action/task without interrupting activity. Developer replies notify reviewers and can legitimately re-engage users after a verified fix.

Google Play prohibits fraudulent or incentivized ratings/reviews. Developer replies are a support/product-feedback surface. A Google-confirmed In-App Review API incident reduced rating/review volume from 2026-04-21 through 2026-05-06, demonstrating that abrupt review-volume changes can be platform-caused rather than product-caused.

DF0–DF5: `signal identity → solicitation integrity → metric-semantic integrity → feedback-to-product integrity → response/recovery integrity → growth decision`.

Preserve: `rating request ≠ prompt shown`, `prompt shown ≠ rating submitted`, `summary rating ≠ product quality`, `visible reviews ≠ complete user sentiment`, `rating-count change ≠ product-caused change`, `review response ≠ marketing channel`, `rating reset ≠ review reset`, and `higher star average ≠ sustainable growth`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, installs, star averages, review counts, impressions, CTR, rewarded acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised specialist value, with trustworthy feedback captured and resolved without manipulating Store signals. Advertising contributes only incremental, reconciled revenue from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states through an authorized and active supply path. Monetization experiments must use legitimate traffic, preserve user-level assignment and distinguish observed outcomes from platform-scaled estimates and post-deployment realized revenue. Product release readiness, feedback integrity, ad-supply activation readiness and monetization-experiment readiness are separate tracks.
