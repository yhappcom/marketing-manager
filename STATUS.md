## Latest validated addition — 226
Store-page routing cannot be judged safely from a generic `conversion rate`. Google Play's current Listing Conversion Analysis defines CTR from store-listing visitors who click Install/Open/Pre-register and says CTR replaces the legacy Conversion Rate metric on that surface, while current monthly Store Performance exports still expose Store Listing Acquisitions and an install-based Store Listing Conversion Rate. These metrics have different numerator events and must never be merged into one conversion series.

Google also warns that daily unique Store Listing Visitors cannot simply be summed to reproduce weekly/monthly unique cohorts, and organic total rows overlap with search/browse components. Apple CPP analytics have a separate sparse-data constraint: page-level metrics appear only after at least five first-time downloads. Apple also distinguishes product-page attribution from Store Sheet, In-App Event and App Page acquisition surfaces. Therefore unavailable CPP analytics are not zero demand, and total App Store downloads are not assumed to equal product-page-attributed downloads.

EL0–EL5: `surface identity → event identity → denominator identity → time/cohort integrity → sparse/privacy integrity → qualified-value decision`.

Preserve: `Google Play CTR ≠ install/acquisition conversion rate`; `Install click ≠ completed acquisition`; `Store listing acquisition ≠ every Play install`; `daily unique visitors summed ≠ monthly unique visitors`; `organic total + search + browse ≠ valid additive total when total contains the components`; `Apple CPP analytics unavailable ≠ zero demand`; `App Store total downloads ≠ sum of product-page-attributed downloads`; `higher Store metric ≠ higher qualified specialist value`; and `metric label similarity ≠ semantic equivalence`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store CTR, Store conversion, generic retention, peer percentile, impressions, ad CTR, dashboard eCPM, ARPV, consent rate or estimated impression revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value through a reliable experience, with monetization that respects current privacy choice and does not corrupt that value. Store metrics are valid only under their exact event, denominator, cohort and privacy semantics. Search-intent routing improves relevance only when backed by real demand and truthful utility; custom-page capacity is not a growth objective. Attribution is not causality; sparse/aggregated/private/unavailable or threshold-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero.
