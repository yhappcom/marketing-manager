## Latest validated addition — 207
Reddit's current 2026 guidance supports a permission-first, contribution-first model for growing an owned community. Useful seed content, precise community descriptions, organic relevant mentions, moderator permission for promotion in related communities, and occasional welcomed reposts/crossposts are compatible with growth. Repetitive link insertion, mass invitations/messages and other unwanted repetitive behavior are spam risks. Platform permissibility never overrides a specific subreddit's rules.

For niche apps, an owned subreddit should therefore optimize for specialist utility density rather than promotional frequency. `r/MintTapforYieldMax` is treated as a specialist trust/support/research/retention surface first and an acquisition surface only secondarily. Subscriber count, karma and clicks are not primary success metrics.

DS0–DS5: `community job identity → metadata/seed-value integrity → external-community permission integrity → contribution-before-promotion integrity → disclosure/role integrity → qualified community-value decision`.

Preserve: `owned subreddit ≠ owned audience`, `platform allows promotion ≠ subreddit permits promotion`, `subscriber count ≠ community health`, `karma ≠ qualified acquisition`, `link click ≠ specialist value`, `crosspost technically possible ≠ crosspost welcome`, `product-owner participation ≠ spam by itself`, `frequent posting ≠ useful community`, and `community growth ≠ app growth`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, karma, submissions, nominations, installs, campaign/UTM-token count, CSL/CPP/deep-link count, experiment count, event-card count, tag count, star averages, impressions, CTR or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value. Attribution improves evidence but does not convert platform credit into causal truth; sparse, aggregated or privacy-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero. Segmented Store pages and owned app links improve continuity only when intent, promise, destination, fallback and maintenance remain aligned. Search-distributed social evidence adds a free demand-sensing layer, while owned communities add a specialist trust/support/research layer; neither substitutes for qualified downstream app value.
