## Latest validated addition — 158
Apple and Google both expose native, zero-media-cost Store attribution that should be used before adding third-party attribution complexity. Apple App Store Connect campaign links can associate a campaign token with impressions, product-page views, downloads, usage, sales and subscriptions. Apple's minimum metric visibility threshold of five in the selected date range is a reporting threshold, not statistical sufficiency.

Google Play Conversion analysis supports UTM source/campaign and other Store-listing breakdowns, while Store-performance exports preserve traffic source, search term, UTM source/campaign, visitors, acquisitions and conversion. Google Play organic is the sum of organic search and browse, so the aggregate and components must not be added together. Store-listing reports also do not represent every Play distribution surface.

BV0–BV5: `source eligibility → stable identity → platform-native instrumentation → measurement-boundary integrity → causal discipline → qualified downstream value`. A tagged link establishes an attributable route, not incremental causality. Every deliberate external Store CTA should have a stable source/content identity unless community rules, platform limitations or user experience make tagging inappropriate; exceptions are recorded rather than reconstructed later.

For MintTap, BV supplies source evidence while BN/BO supply Store destination routing. BI–BK community permission/disclosure and BE financial-claim provenance remain upstream. For LogMate, the same contract applies to qualified pilot-community, technical-content and owned-media routes without turning attribution into evidence for aviation-regulatory claims.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not usable merely because the platform exposes it. Eligibility, truthful classification and product fit precede creative production. Native attribution improves observability but does not establish incrementality. Sparse or threshold-suppressed evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and a discovery/trust surface second. Platform approval, featuring, recommendations, AI copy, accessibility labels/tags, category membership, event engagement or tagged-link conversion are distribution/context signals—not proof of demand, domain correctness, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural states that do not materially degrade that value.
