## Latest validated addition — 177
Google Play supports a publisher-defined unique URL for a Custom Store Listing (CSL), using a unique `listing` parameter. This provides a zero-cost way to preserve the identity of an owned/community/referral route while serving a more relevant Play Store presentation. It is semantically distinct from Search-keyword CSL targeting and must not be treated as a documented deferred/in-app deep link.

CO0–CO5: `route-purpose identity → audience/promise match → CSL identity integrity → link-distribution integrity → Store-evidence integrity → downstream-value validation`.

Preserve `unique CSL URL ≠ search-keyword CSL`, `unique CSL URL ≠ deferred deep link`, `specific Store presentation ≠ specific in-app destination`, `link click ≠ Store visit`, `Store visit ≠ acquisition`, `acquisition ≠ first useful value`, `Not attributed ≠ zero`, and `attributable route ≠ incremental lift`.

The operating principle is route continuity without false precision: use a unique CSL URL only when an existing zero-cost route has a differentiated, evidence-backed intent and truthful Store proof. Do not proliferate route variants solely for tracking.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Store conversion is not the terminal objective. A Store metric label is not a stable semantic contract, a peer percentile is not an industry target, and a Store keyword assignment is not a ranking guarantee. Apple CPP and Google Play CSL objects retain platform-native semantics even when normalized to a common intent layer. A Google Play unique CSL URL identifies a Store presentation route; it is not proof of a deferred in-app destination. A visible CPP is not proof that its deep link is eligible, approved, reached, or valuable. Sparse/suppressed or not-attributed evidence is inconclusive, not zero. Native attribution improves observability but not incrementality. Store/platform approval is not proof of demand or product-market fit. Consent does not create placement permission. Higher requests, impressions/user, CTR, refresh or full-screen exposure are not sustainable growth if they degrade traffic quality, privacy choice, core-task completion, first/restored value, or repeated useful use. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach the product value promised by the acquisition surface and repeat it, while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from clearly identified, privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states.
