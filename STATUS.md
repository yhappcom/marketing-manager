## Latest validated addition — 171
Frequency permission is separate from placement eligibility. AdMob supports app- and ad-unit-level frequency caps for interstitial, rewarded and app-open impressions per user over a chosen period. A natural transition or privacy-eligible request therefore does not imply unlimited repeated exposure.

Invalid traffic includes artificially inflated impressions/clicks, including publisher self-interaction, repeated user activity, encouraged or accidental clicks and automated/deceptive traffic. Development must use test ads/test devices. Exposure pressure is therefore both a product-value variable and a traffic-quality variable.

CI0–CI5: `surface eligibility → frequency identity → exposure integrity → traffic-quality integrity → revenue/value reconciliation → sustainable decision`.

Preserve `eligible state ≠ unlimited exposure permission`, `more impressions/user ≠ more sustainable revenue/user`, `high CTR ≠ healthy monetization`, `frequency cap configured ≠ product-appropriate frequency`, and `policy compliance ≠ immunity from traffic-quality review`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Native attribution improves observability but not incrementality. Sparse evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and discovery surface second. Store/platform approval or metadata is not proof of demand or product-market fit. Ad supply authorization is not revenue proof. Consent is not a one-time boolean and consent compliance does not create placement permission. A foreground event is not an eligible app-open exposure; a navigation tap is not a natural interstitial transition; an eligible transition is not unlimited exposure permission. Higher requests, impressions/user, CTR, refresh or full-screen exposure are not sustainable growth if they increase traffic-quality risk, delay first/restored useful value, interrupt professional work, manipulate privacy choice, or degrade core-task completion and repeated useful use. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from clearly identified, privacy-eligible, natural or genuinely voluntary states at exposure pressure that preserves traffic quality and product value.
