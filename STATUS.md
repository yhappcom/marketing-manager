## Latest validated addition — 134
Canonical principle: **the metric contract—not a familiar dashboard label—is canonical.** Google Play's 2026 reporting change breaks any assumption that Android Store `conversion` is one stable metric or an Apple-equivalent CVR.

Google Play's current Help documentation states that from June 2026 its primary Store-listing performance metrics are based on unique clicks, and from July 2026 Store-listing performance reports focus on intent rather than completed acquisitions. CTR is Store-listing visitors who click Install/Open/Pre-register divided by Store-listing visitors and replaces the legacy Conversion Rate on those content-performance surfaces. The default interaction view can include both never-installed users and returning users who previously uninstalled.

Completed Store-listing acquisitions remain available in Grow overview, Statistics and exports. Current downloadable Store-performance reports retain acquisition-oriented visitor/acquisition/conversion definitions. Consequently Play Console now contains coexisting click-intent and completed-acquisition metric families. Historical acquisition CVR, current listing CTR, completed Store acquisition, first open and downstream product value must remain separate.

AZ3 requires exact metric name, reporting surface, event, numerator/denominator, new/returning and user/device population, traffic scope, listing/destination, observation window and semantic version. It forbids treating an Install click as a completed acquisition, an acquisition as first value, or Apple/Google rates as interchangeable. AZ4 links the native Store chain to product-defined first value/useful return.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. A tailored Store page is not successful because its selected audience shows a higher conversion number. Store metrics are decision-worthy only when their native event, numerator/denominator, reporting surface, population, source composition, first-time/returning semantics, privacy-threshold state and metric-version are understood, and when the resulting acquisition preserves truthful specialist intent through first value and useful return. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. A Google Play Install click is intent, not a completed acquisition; completed acquisition is not activation. Apple App Store conversion, Google Play post-2026 CTR and legacy/acquisition conversion are not interchangeable. Deterministic intent routing and randomized creative experiments answer different questions. Native social reach and Google search discovery are separate evidence surfaces; neither substitutes for downstream specialist utility. Third-party community permission, owned-community governance and sustainable service operations remain prerequisites. A policy-compliant ad format is not automatically a product-fit ad format. Optimize qualified retained specialist utility, community trust and reconciled compatible revenue—not top-of-funnel volume or raw impression count.
