## Latest validated addition — 122
Canonical principle: **Rewarded advertising is legitimate only when it exchanges attention for a genuinely optional in-app benefit; it must never become a toll on core product value.** Google currently requires ordinary rewarded ads to be individually and affirmatively opted into, with the required action and reward disclosed beforehand, the promised reward delivered, and refusal/skipping leaving normal app use intact. Direct monetary rewards are prohibited; eligible rewards must be in-app and non-transferable. Rewarded interstitial is a distinct subtype: it may begin at a natural transition without prior opt-in, but requires an intro screen with a clear opt-out and must not be treated as equivalent to user-requested rewarded inventory.

AN0–AN5 sharpens earlier A0–A5. AN3 requires substantive optionality, truthful exchange disclosure, reliable reward delivery, protected core access, bounded frequency, F/E/U/AC/AD compatibility, and first-value/useful-return guardrails. AN4 requires observed incremental retained-user economics without material downstream harm. Refusal is a normal successful outcome, not a failed conversion.

MintTap must not put portfolio viewing, transaction/history, distribution/ROC, reverse-split continuity, tax adjustment or recovery behind rewarded ads. LogMate is stricter: flight entry, totals/search, import/export, backup/restore, offline access and record integrity are protected. If no genuine optional benefit exists without subtracting from core value, the correct strategy is no rewarded inventory.
