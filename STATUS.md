## Latest validated addition — 159
Native Store attribution can silently change meaning across browser, redirect and reporting boundaries. Apple distinguishes App Referrer, Web Referrer and Campaign: on iOS a website click opened from a non-Safari browser such as Chrome can be attributed to that browser as an App Referrer, while Safari redirect chains can make the last URL the Web Referrer. Apple Campaign rows also require more than one day and at least five attributed App Units before appearing.

Google Play Acquisition reporting similarly has coverage limits: UTM-tracked Store-app visits and untagged third-party referrers are distinguishable, while over-the-air and play.google.com web impressions are not tracked on the Acquisition reports page. Android's Play Install Referrer API adds an install-time provenance layer (referrer URL, click/install timestamps, install-time app version); referrer information is available for 90 days and remains unchanged unless the app is reinstalled.

BW0–BW5: `human-source identity → route capture → native semantics → coverage/suppression → install boundary → reconciliation/downstream value`. The company attribution registry must preserve `declared_human_source`, `platform_observed_source`, and `route_mechanics` separately. Missing/suppressed evidence is not zero, and browser/referrer disagreement is not automatically tracking failure.

For MintTap, live link auditing must now include redirect/shortener/browser route mechanics before channel conclusions are drawn. Android Play Install Referrer support should be inspected before adding new instrumentation. For LogMate, the same route contract should be built before pilot-community launch traffic scales.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not usable merely because the platform exposes it. Eligibility, truthful classification and product fit precede creative production. Native attribution improves observability but does not establish incrementality. Attribution must preserve the intended human source separately from the platform-observed source and route mechanics; redirects, browsers, suppression thresholds and unsupported reporting surfaces can change what is visible. Sparse or threshold-suppressed evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and a discovery/trust surface second. Platform approval, featuring, recommendations, AI copy, accessibility labels/tags, category membership, event engagement or tagged-link conversion are distribution/context signals—not proof of demand, domain correctness, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural states that do not materially degrade that value.
