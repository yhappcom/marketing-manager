## Latest validated addition — 153
Google Play Promotional content is a distinct organic discovery/re-entry surface, but it is not a default launch channel. Current Google guidance limits the feature to eligible apps/Premium growth tools and requires genuinely new, noteworthy, user-facing moments rather than evergreen service descriptions.

Most importantly for the current portfolio, Google's current Promotional content quality guidance says Promotional content is not allowed for certain financial products/services and explicitly identifies investment/financial advice, investment resources/management, stocks, funds, shares and financial utility services among the restricted themes/terms. MintTap's YieldMax ETF/fund/distribution/ROC tracking proposition materially overlaps that boundary. Therefore **Google Play Promotional content is not an approved MintTap zero-cost channel under current policy evidence**; do not spend creative effort trying to word around the restriction.

The restriction is surface-specific and is not generalized to otherwise-permitted Play listing, CSL, experiment, review or external acquisition mechanisms. LogMate does not share the identified finance restriction and remains a future candidate, but only after live Premium-growth-tool eligibility and a genuinely substantial implemented user-facing release are verified.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not usable merely because a platform exposes the feature. Surface/account eligibility and category policy come before creative production; for MintTap, current Google Play Promotional-content finance restrictions stop that path before asset creation. Native Store experiments also consume scarce organic traffic: if current traffic cannot resolve a decision-sized effect, not running the test is valid. Intent-specific pages must begin with evidenced specialist demand, preserve truthful proof and produce qualified users who reach the promised core value. Platform recommendations, featuring or AI-generated listing copy are hypothesis/distribution aids, not evidence of demand, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sparse, censored, underpowered or inconclusive evidence remains unknown/inconclusive rather than being promoted into a growth claim. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions/eCPM/short-run revenue; it requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value.
