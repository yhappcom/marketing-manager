## Latest validated addition — 191
App-ads.txt seller authorization and AdMob app readiness are separate controls. Preserve: `Store live ≠ AdMob Ready`, `Store linked ≠ app verified`, `app-ads.txt verified ≠ app approved`, `account verified ≠ app Ready`, `Ready ≠ every request filled`, `SDK integrated ≠ monetization activated`, `limited serving ≠ normal monetization baseline`, and `Ready ≠ sustainable revenue`.

DC0–DC5: `release identity → Store-link identity → verification prerequisites → readiness-review state → serving-state integrity → monetization decision`.

Monetization experiments must carry readiness/serving state. If readiness or serving changes during an experiment, split analysis at the transition; do not average constrained and normal-supply periods. Weak yield cannot be attributed to placement/format/geography/cohort until upstream supply activation constraints are ruled out.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, installs, impressions, CTR, rewarded acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised specialist value. Advertising contributes only incremental, reconciled revenue from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states through both an authorized seller path and an approved/active supply path. Product release readiness and ad-supply activation readiness are separate tracks; unresolved ad supply must not be misread as product-market evidence.
