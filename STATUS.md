Third-party community permission, owned-community governance and sustainable service operations are prerequisites, not optimizable funnel losses. A policy-compliant ad format is not automatically a product-fit ad format. Optimize qualified retained specialist utility, community trust and reconciled compatible revenue—not top-of-funnel volume or raw impression count.
