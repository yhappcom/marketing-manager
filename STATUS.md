## Latest validated addition — 242
Ad-funded specialist apps should optimize eligible inventory using impression-level revenue evidence rather than clicks, raw impressions or ad density. Google Mobile Ads exposes paid-event callbacks carrying value, currency and precision; precision may be unknown, estimated, publisher-provided or precise and therefore must remain attached to the measurement. Paid-event telemetry is analytical evidence, not automatically settled cash, and aggregate values require reconciliation against authoritative AdMob reporting.

Format follows layout and user state: anchored adaptive banners fit persistent fixed-edge inventory; inline adaptive banners fit appropriate scrollable content. Banner proximity to interactive/navigation controls creates accidental-click risk. Interstitials must not unexpectedly interrupt focused tasks or obstruct core navigation/content. Google's stated interstitial frequency ceiling is a compliance ceiling, not a monetization target.

FB0–FB5: `surface/value identity → format/layout integrity → paid-event measurement integrity → precision/reconciliation integrity → UX/policy guardrail integrity → sustainable revenue decision`.

Preserve: `higher CTR ≠ better placement`; `higher eCPM ≠ higher sustainable revenue`; `more impressions ≠ better monetization`; `paid-event value ≠ settled cash`; `estimated ≠ precise`; `policy ceiling ≠ product target`; `technical fit ≠ surface eligibility`; `low niche traffic ≠ increase ad density`.
