## Latest validated addition — 181
Reddit promotion permission and Reddit distribution are separate layers. Reddit's Contributor Quality Score (CQS) assigns accounts to five tiers using multiple signals including prior account actions, network/location signals and account-security steps such as email verification; moderators can use CQS in AutoModerator. Reddit's optional Reputation Filter can automatically filter content from accounts considered potential spammers, likely to have content removed, or unestablished. Therefore a contribution may satisfy written community rules yet still be filtered before normal visibility.

CS0–CS5: `account identity integrity → contribution legitimacy → reputation-state awareness → reachability verification → recovery integrity → downstream-value validation`.

Preserve: `subreddit permitted ≠ automatically reachable`, `submitted ≠ visible`, `visible ≠ endorsed`, `karma ≠ CQS`, `high karma ≠ immunity from filtering`, `filtered ≠ permission to repost from another account`, and `CQS optimization ≠ a marketing objective`.

Operating principle: do not game opaque trust classifiers. Use stable, truthfully affiliated identities; earn durable participation legitimacy through genuinely useful contributions; record submitted/visible/filtered/removed/locked/unknown states; and treat filtering/removal as a rules/reputation diagnosis rather than a trigger for account rotation or repeated reposting.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, submissions, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Store conversion is not the terminal objective. Platform Store objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window. Reddit promotion is governed per community; permission does not guarantee reachability because reputation and contributor-quality controls can filter contributions. Durable participation permission and reachability are assets, and repeated behavior that damages them is negative growth. Sparse, suppressed, filtered, inconclusive or unattributed evidence is not zero. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states.
