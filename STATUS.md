## Latest validated addition — 127
Canonical principle: **zero-cost distribution is not unmeasurable distribution. Preserve channel identity through the Store boundary whenever the platform permits it, then judge the channel by downstream utility rather than install count.**

Apple App Store Connect campaign links can associate campaign tokens with impressions, product-page views, downloads, usage, sales and subscriptions, but dashboard metrics are privacy-thresholded (minimum 5) and detailed small cohorts may be suppressed/combined. Google Play's official Install Referrer API can expose the referrer URL plus click/install timestamps and first-installed app version to the installed app. These mechanisms are not semantically equivalent and must not be forced into one attribution model.

AS3 requires stable campaign taxonomy, platform-appropriate Store-boundary attribution, a link registry, separation of clicks/Store views/downloads, downstream first-value/useful-return measurement, privacy-aware treatment of censored cohorts and no unnecessary personal identifiers in campaign/referrer data. AS4 requires evidence that a channel family produces qualified retained utility relative to its operating cost—not merely more installs.
