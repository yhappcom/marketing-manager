## Latest validated addition — 227
Reddit's current policy/guidance does not make all promotional content spam, but it also does not provide a universal promotional safe harbor. Individual communities may prohibit promotion or use their own conventions (including a 10% self-promotion convention); sitewide rules still prohibit repeated/unsolicited mass engagement, repetitive exposure-seeking posts, mass tagging/DMs and spam-facilitating automation. Therefore actual venue rules, account/reputation state, contribution type and moderation/filter outcomes must be recorded before interpreting community performance.

Reddit's reputation filter can suppress content from potentially spammy/unestablished accounts using sitewide signals, so removal/filtering/low distribution is not automatically weak product demand. Separately, Google Search currently supports creator/community semantics such as ProfilePage and DiscussionForumPosting structured data, but eligibility does not guarantee rich-result appearance or ranking. Structured data describes truthful owned knowledge; it does not manufacture authority.

EM0–EM5: `venue/permission identity → contribution identity → repetition/distribution integrity → disclosure/destination integrity → owned-knowledge durability → qualified-value decision`.

Preserve: `promotion ≠ automatically spam`; `10% convention ≠ universal permission`; `permission ≠ permission for repetitive mass promotion`; `removal/filtering ≠ weak demand`; `karma/upvotes ≠ qualified acquisition`; `community ownership ≠ app-link feed`; `AI drafting ≠ permission to automate distribution`; `schema eligibility ≠ ranking guarantee`; `markup ≠ expertise`; and `indexed pages ≠ qualified growth`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store CTR, Store conversion, generic retention, peer percentile, Reddit karma/upvotes, indexed-page count, impressions, ad CTR, dashboard eCPM, ARPV, consent rate or estimated impression revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires permitted, useful specialist contributions and truthful owned/Store destinations that lead qualified users to repeat promised value through a reliable experience, with monetization that respects current privacy choice and does not corrupt that value. Community moderation/filtering state must be separated from demand. Store metrics are valid only under their exact event, denominator, cohort and privacy semantics. Search-intent routing improves relevance only when backed by real demand and truthful utility; custom-page capacity is not a growth objective. Attribution is not causality; sparse/aggregated/private/unavailable or threshold-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero.
