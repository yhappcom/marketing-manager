## Design Studio alignment review — completed

The review established several operating principles that now govern Marketing Manager work:

1. **Research is subordinate to real project decisions.** File count and reading volume are not success metrics.
2. **Reading is not PASS.** Competence should progress toward application, critique, failure analysis, validation, transfer, and advisory judgment where the topic permits.
3. **Project-specific success is not a universal rule.** A winning campaign/creative/channel/message must retain its product, audience, channel, time, measurement, and lifecycle context.
4. **Cross-domain evidence should be checked before substantial work.** Relevant Product, Design Studio, and Web Manager evidence is reused, replicated, challenged, transfer-tested, or treated as a dependency.
5. **Overlap is allowed when analytically useful.** The problem is unexamined duplication, not repetition itself.
6. **Failure→revision is valuable evidence.** Marketing records should include interpretable failures and alternative explanations, not only winners.
7. **Live project mode takes priority.** When a real app needs marketing work, accumulated knowledge is applied first and only material gaps are researched.
8. **Knowledge must flow back to the owning discipline.** Marketing performance evidence involving typography, color, layout, interaction, or web presentation is returned as scoped transfer evidence rather than silently becoming design doctrine.

## Canonical role boundary after alignment

### Marketing Manager
Owns market/customer research, segmentation, targeting, positioning, value proposition, messaging, launch/GTM, channel strategy, ASO market/conversion strategy, lifecycle/growth, pricing/marketing economics, marketing analytics, attribution, incrementality, experimentation, and portfolio allocation.

### Design Studio
Owns reusable typography, color, spatial/layout, interaction, and web-design judgment and validation.

### Web Manager
Owns website architecture, technical web/SEO/operations constraints, and site implementation/operations governance.

### Product repositories
Own the truth of shipped functionality, pricing/entitlements, privacy/data behavior, and release state.

Ownership is canonical responsibility, not a restriction on adjacent learning.

## Completed substantive research

### `research/001_marketing_foundations.md`

First integrated checkpoint completed around:

- marketing as a value/exchange system rather than an advertising synonym;
- creation, communication, delivery, and exchange as distinct but connected marketing concerns;
- historical expansion from goods/transaction-centered thinking toward relationship/service/value-in-use perspectives;
- customer orientation vs broader market orientation;
- origins of the marketing mix and the compression from Borden’s broader mix to McCarthy’s 4Ps;
- 4Ps as a coverage framework rather than a complete strategy or causal model;
- distinctions among marketing, advertising, promotion, sales, product marketing, and growth;
- first app-marketing system model linking market reality → offer/value → access → communication → exchange/economics → realized outcome → measurement;
- foundation errors to avoid, including optimizing installs or platform metrics without user/business value.

This study is retained as valid Foundation evidence. It does not by itself mark Stage 1 complete.

## Current retained judgment

- Marketing is broader than promotion and advertising.
- Product experience, pricing, distribution/access, communication, exchange terms, and realized customer value interact as one market system.
- Customer feedback is evidence, not an automatic roadmap.
- Frameworks such as 4Ps are decision aids, not reality or complete strategy.
- Acquisition quality must eventually reconcile with activation, retention, business economics, and causal measurement.
- Platform metrics are proxies unless connected to the user/business outcome they are intended to represent.
- A creative or campaign performance result is context-bounded evidence, not proof of universal design or marketing superiority.
- Marketing should brief Design Studio with audience/problem/message/proof/action/channel/testing requirements rather than prescribing arbitrary visual solutions.

## Mandatory cross-domain practice going forward

For substantial work, use the `RELATED DOMAIN CHECK` defined in `AGENTS.md` and `coordination/DESIGN_STUDIO_ALIGNMENT.md` when relevant.

At minimum ask:

- is product truth verified?
- could Type, Color, Layout/Interaction, or Web Design evidence change the decision?
- could Web Manager architecture/SEO/operations evidence change the decision?
- what internal marketing evidence already exists?
- what result actually matters and what metrics are only proxies?
- what is the causal strength of the evidence?
- what context limits transfer?
