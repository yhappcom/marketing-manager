## Latest validated addition — 157
Google Play category and tags are a real zero-cost discovery surface, but they are classification metadata rather than free-form ASO keywords. Google says they help users search for and discover relevant apps, permits at most five tags, requires tags to be obviously relevant from the listing or initial app experience, and recommends changing tags only after significant content/functionality changes.

Google's current category examples explicitly include portfolio/trading under Finance, making Finance the strongest documented MintTap fit subject to live Console verification. Accessibility tags such as Screen reader-friendly, Visual assistance, Hearing assistance, Learning disability, Motor assistance and Accessible communication are capability signals; they require release evidence and must not be selected merely for reach.

BU0–BU5: `live classification identity → primary-purpose fit → tag obviousness → capability-backed special tags → change discipline → outcome semantics`. Classification is infrastructure, not a campaign. Do not rotate tags as pseudo-keywords, choose adjacent categories for presumed traffic, or attribute ranking changes to a tag without platform evidence.

Google also states that curated Play Console peer groups use review-team tagging systems that power the Play Store experience. Peer benchmarks provide contextual comparison, not causal evidence that a developer-selected tag changed ranking or conversion.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not usable merely because the platform exposes it. Eligibility, truthful classification and product fit precede creative production. Sparse evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and a discovery/trust surface second. Platform approval, featuring, recommendations, AI copy, accessibility labels/tags, category membership or event engagement are distribution/context signals—not proof of demand, domain correctness, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural states that do not materially degrade that value.
