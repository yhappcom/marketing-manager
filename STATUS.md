## Latest validated addition — 156
Apple Accessibility Nutrition Labels are now a meaningful Store truth/discovery surface, not merely compliance metadata. Apple states that users can include accessibility capabilities such as VoiceOver or Larger Text in search queries and apps that have declared matching support can be considered more relevant. Labels appear device-specifically on the product page on Apple OS 26-generation releases and later; if information is not supplied, the Accessibility section can still show that support has not been indicated.

The marketing constraint is strict: support may be declared only after the relevant Apple evaluation criteria are satisfied across **all common tasks** for that device. Isolated accessible screens are insufficient. Significant bugs that change the evaluation answer invalidate the declaration. For VoiceOver, charts/data visualizations need accessibility information through an appropriate chart API or a reasonably complete text alternative.

BT0–BT5 governs `surface/version identity → common-task inventory → feature-specific implementation proof → declaration truth/defect state → discovery/trust semantics → downstream value/maintenance`. Accessibility implementation creates the discovery asset; marketing reports validated support. A label is not an Apple endorsement, generic quality badge, evidence of financial/aviation correctness, or a known ranking multiplier.

For MintTap, App Accessibility joins the live App Store Connect audit. Candidate common tasks include portfolio/transaction workflows and understanding distribution/ROC/split information, but the exact set must come from the release. VoiceOver, Larger Text, non-color-only differentiation, contrast and dark interface are potentially material for a data-dense finance UI, but none may be declared from design intent. BE continues to govern financial correctness. LogMate should use the same release-qualified common-task matrix and must not turn accessibility support into a regulatory-compliance claim.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not usable merely because the platform exposes it. Eligibility and truthful product fit precede creative production. Sparse or underpowered evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility support is a product capability first and a discovery/trust surface second: declare only what users can actually complete across common tasks under Apple's criteria. Platform approval, featuring, recommendations, AI-generated copy, accessibility labels or event engagement are distribution/trust signals—not evidence of demand, domain correctness, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised core value, while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural states that do not materially degrade that value.
