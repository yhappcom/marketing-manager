## Latest validated addition — 162
Apple App Store pre-order and Google Play pre-registration are finite prelaunch commitment surfaces, not evergreen ASO or generic awareness channels. Apple permits pre-order only in a territory where the app has not already been released; first-ever release dates are 2–180 days ahead, while a new territory for an app already released elsewhere can be 2–365 days ahead. At release, pre-order customers are notified and the app can automatically download under Apple’s customer/device settings.

Google Play pre-registration currently has a 90-day campaign limit, permits at most two apps/games in pre-registration at once, and explicitly recommends production-near declarations/configuration plus prior testing. Pre-registered users receive launch notification and eligible devices can auto-install, but auto-install is conditional and test users have different notification semantics. Google also tells developers to drive traffic from owned/third-party promotional channels, confirming that pre-registration captures pre-existing/generated intent rather than creating demand by itself.

BZ0–BZ5: `surface/territory eligibility → release-readiness evidence → promise/listing integrity → demand-route/permission integrity → commitment-to-launch measurement boundary → launch conversion/postlaunch value`. Preserve `prelaunch attention ≠ Store commitment ≠ notification/auto-download ≠ first open ≠ useful activation ≠ repeated value`.

MintTap is already released, so this is not a recurring promotion mechanism in its existing territories. Apple pre-order could matter only for a genuinely new territory where MintTap has never shipped and where financial/legal/localization requirements are satisfied. LogMate is the stronger future candidate, but no pre-order/pre-registration should open until a production-near build, Store truth, policy readiness and credible launch date exist; Google’s 90-day window makes premature activation especially costly.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. A Store surface or Store asset is not usable merely because the platform exposes it. Eligibility, truthful classification, release truth, localization truth and destination-channel permission precede distribution. Prelaunch Store commitment is a finite launch instrument: it captures intent into a platform-retained commitment but does not create demand or prove launch-day use; notification and auto-install eligibility remain distinct from actual first open and useful activation. Localization is discovery infrastructure, not a translation checklist: professional vocabulary must be evidenced by locale, while territory, displayed localization and keyword localization remain separate dimensions. Native attribution improves observability but does not establish incrementality. Attribution must preserve the intended human source separately from the platform-observed source and route mechanics; redirects, browsers, suppression thresholds and unsupported reporting surfaces can change what is visible. Sparse or threshold-suppressed evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and a discovery/trust surface second. Platform approval, featuring, recommendations, native marketing assets, Google-owned promotion, AI copy, accessibility labels/tags, category membership, event engagement, localized metadata, prelaunch commitment or tagged-link conversion are distribution/context signals—not proof of demand, domain correctness, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural states that do not materially degrade that value.
