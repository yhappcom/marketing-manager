Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value. Advertising contributes only incremental, reconciled revenue from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states through an authorized and active supply path. Product release readiness, Store-discovery integrity, editorial/event integrity, creative-evidence integrity, feedback integrity, ad-supply readiness and monetization-experiment readiness remain separate tracks.
