## Latest validated addition — 189
Ad clicks and CTR are not monotonic monetization-success metrics. AdMob explicitly includes accidental clicks in invalid traffic; high invalid traffic can limit/disable serving and contribute to estimated-versus-finalized earnings differences. Interactive adjacency, late interstitial arrival after destination content becomes actionable, and contaminated development traffic are therefore revenue-quality problems as well as UX/policy problems. Confirmed Click is a remediation signal, not an acceptable steady-state optimization feature.

DA0–DA5: `interaction-map identity → temporal-placement integrity → traffic-quality evidence → test/live segregation → revenue-quality reconciliation → specialist-value decision`.

Preserve: `click ≠ genuine user interest`, `higher CTR ≠ better placement`, `logical trigger ≠ safe actual display timing`, `intended transition ≠ transition-time display`, `estimated click/revenue lift ≠ sustainable monetization lift`, `Confirmed Click ≠ acceptable steady-state UX`, `test interaction ≠ production evidence`, and `AdMob compliance ≠ complete Google Play ad-experience compliance`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, pre-registrations, pre-orders, installs, impressions, CTR, rewarded-ad acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Store conversion, web traffic and ad yield are not terminal objectives. Platform Store/Search/ad objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window. Reddit promotion is governed per community; permission does not guarantee reachability, and owning a community does not create a right to mobilize its members into third-party communities. Owned-web publishing must serve distinct specialist demand rather than manufacture query coverage. Search Console visible queries are privacy-filtered/coverage-limited evidence rather than a complete demand census. Impression-level ad values preserve precision/provenance and remain distinct from finalized publisher earnings. Ad inventory must fit genuine product states: loading, transition and screen-space semantics cannot be manufactured merely to increase impressions. Native advertising must remain visibly and behaviorally separate from specialist product content. Rewarded advertising must remain a voluntary exchange for incremental value rather than a toll on baseline utility. Ad clicks must represent genuine interest rather than spatial or temporal interception; traffic quality and serving health are part of sustainable revenue evidence. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states within deliberate interruption, separation, voluntariness and traffic-quality boundaries.
