## Latest validated addition — 133
Canonical principle: **routing precedes optimization, and interpretable evidence precedes a Store decision.** Sparse professional-app traffic must not be forced into decisions by treating privacy-censored, compositionally different, or differently defined metrics as comparable observations.

Apple currently attributes sales/usage/subscription activity to recorded acquisition source, but a manual redownload resets the source for subsequent attribution. Individual CPP analytics appear only after at least five first-time downloads; campaign reporting likewise has a minimum visibility condition (more than one day plus at least five attributed App Units). Apple's App Store conversion rate is Total Downloads / Unique Device Impressions and therefore includes redownloads; it is not interchangeable with first-time-install/page-view conversion. CPP analytics can extend beyond conversion into proceeds/value, and retention can be segmented by acquisition source/campaign.

AY3 requires an AX-qualified intent/destination, exact metric/denominator semantics, explicit `censored/unknown/not instrumented` states, source/territory/device composition checks, downstream first-value/useful-return linkage where available, and refusal to infer randomized causality from deterministic CPP/CSL routing. Platform disclosure thresholds are not statistical-power thresholds; no universal minimum sample size is invented.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. A tailored Store page is not successful because its selected audience shows a higher conversion number. Store metrics are decision-worthy only when their numerator/denominator, source composition, first-time/redownload semantics and privacy-threshold state are understood, and when the resulting acquisition preserves truthful specialist intent through first value and useful return. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. Deterministic intent routing and randomized creative experiments answer different questions. Native social reach and Google search discovery are separate evidence surfaces; neither substitutes for downstream specialist utility. Third-party community permission, owned-community governance and sustainable service operations remain prerequisites. A policy-compliant ad format is not automatically a product-fit ad format. Optimize qualified retained specialist utility, community trust and reconciled compatible revenue—not top-of-funnel volume or raw impression count.
