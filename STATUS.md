## Latest validated addition — 113
Canonical principle: **Support is not a footer obligation. For a sparse professional app, the Store-visible support route is part of the acquisition promise and the recovery path that protects useful return after failure.**

Apple's current App Store Connect reference requires a Support URL and states that it must lead to actual contact information so users can reach the developer about app issues, general feedback and feature requests. Marketing URL is a separate property. Apple Privacy Policy URL and optional User Privacy Choices URL are also separate trust surfaces. A generic marketing homepage must not be assumed to satisfy these different jobs.

Google Play's current verified-account documentation separates Store listing contact details from account contact information. Store settings require an email address to publish/update apps; app-support details are public on the listing, while Google account-contact details serve a different purpose. Per-app support contacts can differ from developer-account contacts.

AE0–AE5 governs support-recovery evidence. AE0 is broken/deceptive recovery. AE3 is minimum deliberate-acquisition readiness: live Store support path verified, support/marketing/privacy jobs separated, issue taxonomy mapped to specialist jobs, severe failures have escalation ownership, evidence collection is data-minimized, support is independent of rating/review solicitation, and public promises do not exceed operating capacity. AE4 closes the loop from failure family to resolution/product fix/useful-return recovery; AE5 is reusable across niche apps.

For MintTap, initial specialist failure families are portfolio/transaction persistence, distribution/ROC discrepancy, reverse-split quantity/cost-basis discrepancy, tax adjustment, exchange-rate/currency interpretation, privacy/data requests, ad interference, and version/update issues. Sensitive brokerage/account evidence is not requested by default. For LogMate, record loss/corruption, incorrect totals, import/duplicate errors, backup/restore, offline/PWA persistence/sync and export/history discrepancies are high-value support evidence; data loss outranks cosmetic issues even when crash metrics are healthy.

No evidence was established that a better support page mechanically improves Store ranking. Support is treated as recovery/trust infrastructure, not an ASO ranking factor.
