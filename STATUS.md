## Latest validated addition — 225
Apple and Google Play now both support organic-search-intent routing to differentiated Store pages. Apple CPPs can be assigned approved-version keywords so relevant searches can show a CPP instead of the default page; Google Play CSLs can target Play Search keyword bundles. This turns Store search into a zero-cost segmentation surface rather than limiting custom pages to outbound campaign URLs.

Apple currently permits up to 70 CPPs and Google Play up to 50 CSLs, but capacity is not a target. Apple promotional text remains non-ranking metadata; CPP keyword assignment is a distinct routing/discoverability mechanism. Google keyword targeting can include spelling/translation variations, so a bundle is not equivalent to one literal query. Google I/O 2026 also introduced Gemini-assisted CSL drafting from Grow keyword recommendations; generated copy remains unvalidated until reviewed against product truth, policy and specialist language.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store conversion, generic retention, peer percentile, impressions, CTR, dashboard eCPM, ARPV, consent rate or estimated impression revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value through a reliable experience, with monetization that respects current privacy choice and does not corrupt that value. Search-intent routing improves relevance only when backed by real demand and truthful utility; custom-page capacity is not a growth objective. Attribution is not causality; sparse/aggregated/private/unavailable or threshold-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero.
