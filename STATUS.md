## Latest validated addition — 180
Reddit is not a free distribution surface. Sitewide policy prohibits repeated/unsolicited mass engagement, while promotional permission is community-specific. Reddit's own moderator guidance says promotion is not inherently spam but some communities prohibit it and others use a 10% convention; that convention is not a universal Reddit entitlement or safe harbor. Moderators control local rules, and sitewide compliance does not establish subreddit permission.

CR0–CR5: `community identity → participation legitimacy → affiliation integrity → promotion/link permission → distribution integrity → value/evidence integrity`.

Preserve: `sitewide-compliant ≠ subreddit-permitted`, `promotion not inherently spam ≠ promotion welcomed`, `10% convention ≠ universal rule`, `not removed ≠ moderator endorsement`, `karma ≠ qualified acquisition`, `no explicit disclosure rule ≠ permission to imply independence`, and `AI-assisted drafting ≠ permission for automated mass engagement`.

Operating principle: use Reddit as specialist participation infrastructure, not a posting channel. Keep a community-specific rule/permission registry, disclose ownership/affiliation when material, never coordinate votes or manufacture independent consensus, and optimize for useful native contribution and downstream product value rather than links, karma, or posting volume. The owned r/MintTapforYieldMax community is support/education/product-feedback infrastructure and must not be represented as independent market consensus.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Store conversion is not the terminal objective. Platform Store objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window. Reddit promotion is governed per community and durable participation permission is an asset; repeated exposure that damages that permission is negative growth. Sparse, suppressed, inconclusive or unattributed evidence is not zero. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states.
