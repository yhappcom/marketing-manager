## Latest validated addition — 151
Google Play Custom Store Listings are now modeled as a zero-cost acquisition and re-entry routing system rather than only a paid/country localization feature. Current Play Console documentation supports up to 50 CSLs and targeting by Play Search keywords, unique listing URLs, country/region, pre-registration, lifecycle/buyer segments, custom audiences and ads traffic. A CSL can customize app name, icon, descriptions and graphic assets.

Search-keyword CSLs can use Play-known traffic keywords and selectable spelling/translation variations. Google I/O 2026 also introduced a shorter workflow from Grow keyword recommendations to Gemini-generated keyword-tailored CSLs. These recommendations/generation tools reduce production cost but do not establish demand, claim truth, intent separability or statistical sufficiency.

Unique listing URLs create a particularly relevant zero-cost route from permitted blog/community/social surfaces to message-matched Google Play proof. However, CSLs are not automatically translated; missing translations can expose the CSL default language to targeted users, so localization coverage is part of routing integrity.

BO0–BO5 governs `demand/source identity → intent/audience separability → claim/proof integrity → routing/localization integrity → evidence semantics/sparse-sample discipline → downstream value`. Acquisition CSLs and lifecycle/re-entry CSLs must remain analytically separate. Apple BN and Google BO share an evidence principle but are not treated as feature-identical systems.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not successful because it has more variants or a higher conversion rate in isolation. Intent-specific pages must begin with evidenced specialist demand, preserve truthful proof and produce qualified users who reach the promised core value. Platform recommendations or AI-generated listing copy are hypothesis/production aids, not evidence of demand or claim authority. Sparse or censored evidence remains unknown/inconclusive rather than being promoted into a growth claim. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions/eCPM/short-run revenue; it requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value.
