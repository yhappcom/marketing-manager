## Latest validated addition — 128
Canonical principle: **community permission precedes attribution. A measurable link does not create permission to place it.**

Reddit's current sitewide Spam policy prohibits repeated/unsolicited mass engagement and repetitive mass posting for exposure or financial gain. Promotional content is not inherently spam, but each community can prohibit it or impose stricter rules; Reddit's moderator guidance notes that some communities use a 10% self-promotion convention, but this is not a universal safe harbor. Community moderators ultimately decide what is unwanted in their communities. Reddit also prohibits automation for unsolicited outreach and unauthorized scraping/collection.

AT3 requires current community-rule verification, a self-contained useful answer to a real specialist question, appropriate relationship disclosure, optional links only when permitted and materially useful, no mass/repetitive/automated promotional participation, domain-appropriate evidence quality, and a maintained community registry. AT4 requires repeated trust-compatible evidence, including contribution survival/moderator feedback plus qualified downstream utility where attribution is permitted.
