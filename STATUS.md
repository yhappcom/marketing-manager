## Latest validated addition — 182
An owned Reddit community and third-party Reddit participation must remain operationally separated. Reddit's current Moderator Code of Conduct Rule 3 prohibits moderators from using a community to direct, coordinate, encourage, facilitate or enable interference in other communities. Current Reddit policy also separately prohibits coordinated voting, ban evasion and other disruption. Brand-related communities must accurately disclose official/unofficial affiliation, set reasonable expectations, and remain actively moderated.

CT0–CT5: `ownership/affiliation identity → community-purpose integrity → cross-community separation → moderation integrity → incident/recovery integrity → growth/value integrity`.

Preserve: `owned community ≠ independent endorsement`, `moderator authority ≠ external-mobilization permission`, `discussion of removal/ban ≠ permission to coordinate retaliation`, `members ≠ an amplification pool`, and `ban/filter/removal ≠ permission for account rotation`.

Operating principle: r/MintTapforYieldMax is an owned support/education/product-feedback/listening asset, not a mechanism to manufacture reach or consensus in third-party communities. Do not ask members to upvote, repost removed content, mass-contact moderators, test enforcement, or defend a filtered/banned company account elsewhere. Third-party incidents stay in the CR/CS evidence and recovery path.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, submissions, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Store conversion is not the terminal objective. Platform Store objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window. Reddit promotion is governed per community; permission does not guarantee reachability, and owning a community does not create a right to mobilize its members into third-party communities. Durable participation permission, reachability and non-interfering governance are assets. Sparse, suppressed, filtered, inconclusive or unattributed evidence is not zero. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states.
