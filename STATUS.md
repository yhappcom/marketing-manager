## Latest validated addition — 135
Canonical principle: **a randomized Store creative result is only as meaningful as its preserved experiment contract and target metric.** Current Google Play Store Listing Experiments can run on default or custom Store Listings. The developer selects a target metric—currently Unique user install clicks, Unique user open clicks, or Unique user pre-registration clicks—and Play estimates required time/event volume. Advanced controls include up to two experimental variants, audience percentage, minimum detectable effect (MDE), and confidence level.

Google defines MDE as the minimum control/variant difference needed to declare one better; smaller differences are treated as a draw. Higher confidence lowers false-positive risk but can extend the test. Native outcomes can include a better/best option, Draw, or More data needed, with recommendations where applicable. More data needed is not evidence of failure or equivalence. Experiments auto-stop after six months without automatically applying a variant.

The current help page's exact metric label/definition must be preserved rather than normalized into a generic `CVR`: for example `Unique user install clicks` is currently defined on the help page as users who installed the app while it was not installed on any other device. BA therefore requires exact target name, displayed definition, experiment/listing scope, control/treatment, audience split, variant count, MDE, confidence, dates, result state, confidence/recommendation evidence, semantic version and AY/AZ context.

BA3 is a decision-grade Store experiment. BA4 additionally requires downstream qualified acquisition/first-value/useful-return evidence so a proximal Store winner cannot be mistaken for a growth winner. BA5 preserves wins, losses and draws in a reusable experiment registry. CSL deterministic intent routing and Store Listing Experiment randomized creative assignment remain categorically separate.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. A tailored Store page is not successful because its selected audience shows a higher conversion number. A randomized Store experiment is not successful merely because Play recommends a variant: its exact target metric, definition, allocation, MDE, confidence, result state and semantic version must be retained, and downstream specialist utility must not degrade. `More data needed` is a legitimate sparse-niche outcome; do not manufacture volume by broadening beyond the real professional audience. Store metrics are decision-worthy only when their native event, numerator/denominator, reporting surface, population, source composition, first-time/returning semantics, privacy-threshold state and metric-version are understood, and when the resulting acquisition preserves truthful specialist intent through first value and useful return. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. A Google Play Store target event is not automatically completed acquisition or activation. Apple App Store conversion, Google Play post-2026 CTR and legacy/acquisition conversion are not interchangeable. Deterministic intent routing and randomized creative experiments answer different questions. Native social reach and Google search discovery are separate evidence surfaces; neither substitutes for downstream specialist utility. Third-party community permission, owned-community governance and sustainable service operations remain prerequisites. A policy-compliant ad format is not automatically a product-fit ad format. Optimize qualified retained specialist utility, community trust and reconciled compatible revenue—not top-of-funnel volume or raw impression count.
