## Progress interpretation
Do not report progress by file count. The highest-value question remains whether a target specialist can reach personal first value with acceptable effort, understand why to return, and encounter monetization only at legitimate boundaries. Organic content must be useful before amplification; distribution must be useful in the receiving channel; owned community must be useful without an install; connected advocacy must not masquerade as independent demand; reviews must remain authentic evidence; foreground monetization must consume otherwise-idle waiting; rewarded monetization must add optional surplus; banner monetization must occupy stable passive geometry rather than borrow space or clicks from active specialist interaction. Zero-cost growth and ad revenue compound by preserving specialist trust, not by maximizing posts, prompts, impressions, CTR, ads per open, or ad-dependent feature access.
