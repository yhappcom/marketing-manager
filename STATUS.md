## Latest validated addition — 108
Canonical principle: **A Store rating is a sampled reputation signal, not a product KPI. Ask only at a legitimate post-value boundary, never condition product access or benefits on feedback, and never infer population satisfaction from the prompted sample alone.**

This closes the gap between 086 service recovery and the product-value system. Apple recommends asking at an appropriate satisfaction/completed-task moment without interruption; its system prompt is rate-limited, territory ratings are distinct, and an overview-rating reset is irreversible after release, affects all countries/regions, and leaves written reviews intact. Google prohibits incentivized/manipulated ratings, weights the public rating toward recent ratings, retains a separate lifetime average, and generally delays public rating/review impact while abuse detection runs. Google Play's In-App Review completion callback does not reveal whether a dialog appeared or a review was submitted.

R0–R5 governs deliberate solicitation. R3 is the minimum threshold: neutral production-valid specialist task completed, safe noninterruptive boundary, no reward/gating/high-rating request/sentiment screening, explicit eligibility and suppression logic, direct support path for errors, and analytics that distinguish eligibility/prompt attempt from actual rating submission.

Three evidence classes remain separate: product-quality evidence (first value/useful return/failures), reputation evidence (ratings/review themes), and solicitation evidence (who was eligible and why). A successful post-task prompt cohort is selected by product logic and must not be treated as an unbiased satisfaction survey.

For MintTap, investment outcome, P/L, distribution size, refund amount or positive sentiment must never determine Store-rating eligibility. Apple rating reset defaults to **do not reset** because sparse authentic evidence has option value and old written reviews remain. For LogMate, prompt timing must follow enough real professional use to judge reliability; installation/onboarding alone is insufficient.
