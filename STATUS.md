## Latest validated addition — 236
Ratings/reviews are trust evidence, not a volume-maximization channel. Sparse niche apps must protect representative specialist sentiment and core workflow quality. Apple recommends prompting after a satisfying completed task without interruption; SKStoreReviewController can prompt up to three times per 365 days. Apple overview-rating reset is global across territories for the platform, irreversible after release, does not remove written reviews, and can weaken social proof while new ratings accumulate; default policy is therefore no reset absent a materially fixed historical problem and explicit evidence record.

Google In-App Review must be treated as an opaque platform flow: API completion does not reveal whether the dialog appeared or whether a rating/review was submitted. Google also distinguishes average rating, ratings volume, cumulative average, and the current Google Play rating, which is based on recent ratings. A confirmed Apr 21–May 6 2026 Play Store bug suppressed In-App Review dialogs and rating/review volume, demonstrating that platform incidents can confound reputation trends.

EV0–EV5: `eligible satisfaction moment → non-coercive request integrity → platform-display uncertainty → rating/review metric identity → product/platform-cause integrity → durable specialist-trust decision`.

Preserve: `request attempt ≠ prompt impression`; `flow completion ≠ rating/review submission`; `rating volume change ≠ sentiment change`; `current/displayed rating ≠ cumulative historical average`; `rating reset ≠ review reset`; `rating reset ≠ reputation repair`; `small sample ≠ representative specialist sentiment`; `platform incident ≠ product failure`.
