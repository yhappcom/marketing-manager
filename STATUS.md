## Latest validated addition — 219
Ad-funded growth cannot optimize acquisition and monetization independently. More free/organic traffic, impressions or CTR is not automatically beneficial: traffic-source quality, accidental interaction, test/production separation, anomalous traffic behavior and ad-serving state are part of revenue integrity. A click is not evidence of user intent, and an ad-serving limit is not evidence of weak product demand. Native inventory remains publisher-rendered, so choosing a native format does not outsource visual/interaction integrity to the SDK.

EE0–EE5: `traffic-source identity → interaction integrity → test/production separation → anomaly integrity → serving-state integrity → sustainable-growth decision`.

Preserve: `organic/free traffic ≠ automatically valid traffic`; `click ≠ user intent`; `CTR increase ≠ monetization improvement`; `traffic anomaly ≠ invalid-traffic proof`; `ad-serving limit ≠ product-demand failure`; `policy-compliant ≠ placement-quality`; `test activity ≠ production revenue evidence`; and `more impressions ≠ more sustainable revenue`.

## Progress interpretation

Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value through a reliable experience, with monetization that does not corrupt that value. Advertising decisions retain exact format, placement, geometry, lifecycle, traffic-quality and revenue semantics. The product does not manufacture friction, accidental interaction or sacrifice specialist readability merely to create additional ad inventory.
