## Latest validated addition — 132
Canonical principle: **routing precedes conversion optimization.** A specialist user should first reach the truthful Store surface that matches the job/intention that brought them there; only then is creative optimization meaningful.

Apple currently supports up to 70 Custom Product Pages with unique URLs, differentiated screenshots/previews/promotional text, localization, keyword routing into App Store search, and optional deep links on iOS/iPadOS 18+. Apple Product Page Optimization is a separate randomized instrument for default-page creative and is not available for CPPs. Google Play currently supports up to 50 Custom Store Listings with differentiated name/icon/descriptions/assets and targeting that includes unique URLs and Play-search keywords, among other segments. Google Store Listing Experiments are likewise distinct from deterministic CSL routing.

AX3 requires one validated specialist intent per destination, truthful current product evidence, platform-native routing, a destination registry, explicit fallback, downstream first-value/useful-return measurement, threshold-censoring discipline and retirement rules. Deterministically selected CPP/CSL audiences must not be compared as if they were randomized PPO/Store Listing Experiment treatments. Platform page-count limits are ceilings, not goals.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. A tailored Store page is not successful because it converts better within a selected audience; it must preserve a truthful specialist promise through first value and useful return. Deterministic intent routing and randomized creative experiments answer different questions. Native social reach and Google search discovery are separate evidence surfaces; neither substitutes for downstream specialist utility. Third-party community permission, owned-community governance and sustainable service operations remain prerequisites. A policy-compliant ad format is not automatically a product-fit ad format. Optimize qualified retained specialist utility, community trust and reconciled compatible revenue—not top-of-funnel volume or raw impression count.
