## Latest validated addition — 150
Apple's current Custom Product Page (CPP) model materially expands zero-cost Store routing. Apple documents up to 70 CPPs per app, each with differentiated screenshots/previews/promotional text, unique URLs, localization and optional iOS/iPadOS 18+ deep links. Critically, keywords from the latest approved app version can be assigned to a CPP so that it can appear for those App Store searches instead of the default product page. Apple advises unique keyword combinations for each CPP.

This means CPP is no longer modeled only as a paid-campaign landing mechanism. It can connect organic App Store search and permitted community/blog/social distribution to intent-specific Store proof. However, 70 pages is capacity, not a target. Sparse niche traffic makes unjustified segmentation especially costly.

BN0–BN5 governs `demand evidence → intent separability → claim/proof integrity → routing integrity → sparse-sample discipline → downstream value`. CPP and Product Page Optimization remain separate instruments: CPP routes distinct intent/audiences; PPO randomly tests creative on the default product page and is not available for CPPs. Apple's five-first-time-download threshold for CPP analytics is a reporting/privacy threshold, not statistical sufficiency.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not successful because it has more variants or a higher conversion rate in isolation. Intent-specific pages must begin with evidenced specialist demand, preserve truthful proof and produce qualified users who reach the promised core value. Sparse or censored evidence remains unknown/inconclusive rather than being promoted into a growth claim. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions/eCPM/short-run revenue; it requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value.
