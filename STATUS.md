## Latest validated addition — 240
Google Play Custom Store Listings (CSLs) are zero-cost intent/lifecycle-routing surfaces as well as paid-campaign tools. Google currently permits up to 50 CSLs and supports targeting by country/region, pre-registration, search keywords, churned/lapsed states, buyer states, ads traffic, custom audiences and unique listing URL. A CSL can customize app name, icon, descriptions and graphic assets; shared contact/privacy/category metadata remains common.

Search-keyword targeting can serve a tailored listing to users discovering the app with specified Play search terms, but this is not evidence that assigning a keyword raises ranking. Unique listing URLs can route community/owned-web/social traffic to a matching Play presentation. CSLs are not automatically translated, so segmentation without explicit localization can reduce audience fit. Country targeting also has exclusivity constraints and must not be modeled as a freely composable layer.

EZ0–EZ5: `intent/segment identity → material-difference integrity → targeting integrity → localization/promise integrity → measurement/sparsity integrity → durable-value decision`.

Preserve: `CSL count ≠ segmentation quality`; `search-keyword targeting ≠ ranking guarantee`; `unique listing URL ≠ causal proof`; `Store conversion ≠ retained specialist value`; `lifecycle targeting ≠ permission to exaggerate change`; `default CSL language ≠ automatic localization`; `Apple CPP ≠ Google CSL`; `platform page limit ≠ recommended page count`.
