## Latest validated addition — 155
Apple In-App Events (IAE) are a zero-media-cost App Store discovery and lifecycle surface, but not a generic release-announcement or evergreen ASO mechanism. Published events can appear on the product page, in search, and in editorial/personalized recommendations, and can serve new, active, and lapsed users. Apple explicitly rejects repetitive daily activities, price promotions without new content/features/goods, and general app promotion as event concepts.

IAE is therefore distinct from BN CPP and BR Featuring Nominations: CPP is persistent intent routing; IAE is a real time-bounded Store object around a genuine in-app experience; BR is an editorial pitch. An event can run up to 31 days and become discoverable up to 14 days before it starts. Deep links should reach the relevant experience, while new-user onboarding and a route back to the main app remain intact.

BS0–BS5 governs `surface eligibility → genuine temporal experience → claim/audience integrity → destination/readiness integrity → lifecycle/measurement semantics → outcome integrity`. `approved/published`, `discovered/reached`, `opened/downloaded`, `event value`, `core/restored value`, and `useful return` remain separate states. The five-first-time-download analytics threshold is a reporting/privacy threshold, not statistical sufficiency.

For MintTap, routine YieldMax distribution/ex-dividend dates, market events, recurring data refreshes, tax reminders, and generic updates are not automatically IAE candidates. Do not turn external financial calendar events into pseudo in-app events. A candidate needs a genuine in-app time-bounded experience or substantial content/feature moment and inherits BE financial-claim provenance. LogMate follows the same rule: routine flight logging, recency deadlines, regulatory dates, and recurrent pilot duties are not promotional events merely because they are time-sensitive.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface is not usable merely because the platform exposes it. Eligibility and truthful product fit precede creative production. Sparse or underpowered evidence remains inconclusive. Apple In-App Events add useful temporal discovery and re-engagement, but routine external dates or recurring tasks must not be repackaged as pseudo-events. Platform approval, featuring, recommendations, AI-generated copy or event engagement are distribution signals—not evidence of demand, domain correctness, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised core value, while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural states that do not materially degrade that value.
