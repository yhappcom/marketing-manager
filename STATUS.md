## Current operating model
Store discovery remains separated by function: `Apple CPP / Google CSL = deterministic intent routing | Apple IAE = truthful time-bounded discovery/reactivation | Google Promotional content = eligibility/category/event-truth gated | external tags = origin measurement | peer benchmark = relative diagnostic | PPO = causal creative experiment only when evidence budget exists`.

Organic/search uses 082 N0–N5; N3 requires materially distinct specialist utility and N4 adds trust-complete sourcing/authorship/scope/update discipline. Distribution uses 083 D0–D5; receiving-channel content must provide standalone value before earning a CTA. Owned communities use 084 O0–O5 and separate moderator-amplified reach from independent member preference. Connected speakers use 085 C0–C5 and cannot masquerade as independent word-of-mouth. Reviews use 086 R0–R5 and remain product/service-recovery evidence rather than sentiment-selected growth inventory.

Advertising remains governed by 069–073: `value block → candidate boundary → exposure quality → interaction geometry → content integrity → format → downstream effect`. 087 adds a specific foreground gate: `foreground → protected intent check → genuine wait boundary → F-class → exposure → actionable content → useful action/return → revenue + exit/error/compliance`. Frequency capping can reduce exposure but cannot turn a semantically invalid placement into a valid one. App Open is not a default company-template slot.

## Progress interpretation
Do not report progress by file count. The highest-value question remains whether a target specialist can reach personal first value with acceptable effort, understand why to return, and encounter monetization only at legitimate boundaries. Organic content must be useful before amplification; distribution must be useful in the receiving channel; owned community must be useful without an install; connected advocacy must not masquerade as independent demand; reviews must remain authentic evidence; and foreground monetization must consume otherwise-idle waiting rather than delay an already-actionable user. Zero-cost growth and ad revenue compound by preserving specialist trust, not by maximizing posts, prompts, impressions or ads per open.
