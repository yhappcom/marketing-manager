## Latest validated addition — 141
Canonical principle: **Impression-level ad revenue is an attribution input, not ground truth. Preserve the native precision type and reconcile revenue before using it for placement or product decisions.**

Current Google Mobile Ads documentation distinguishes UNKNOWN, ESTIMATED, PUBLISHER_PROVIDED and PRECISE paid-event values. ESTIMATED may derive from aggregated data and PUBLISHER_PROVIDED may reflect manual CPMs, so numerically equal paid events can have different evidentiary quality. Google recommends attaching the paid listener before display and forwarding paid-event data immediately. When AdMob is linked to Firebase/Analytics, `ad_impression` is logged automatically; DebugView/Realtime plus BigQuery samples can validate value/currency and expose zero/not-set anomalies.

**BG2** requires precision-preserving paid-event provenance. **BG3** validates listener timing, event transport and Analytics payload quality. **BG4** reconciles SDK/Analytics revenue against the authoritative monetization reporting surface at the finest defensible common grain while preserving currency, timezone, latency and precision mix. No universal acceptable variance threshold is invented. **BG5** permits revenue into BF optimization only when reconciliation and precision composition are understood; unreconciled revenue remains diagnostic.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. An owned blog is not successful because it publishes many pages, ranks for many ticker variants or accumulates impressions. A consequential financial claim is not trustworthy merely because it comes from an official-looking page: the source must be authoritative for that exact claim, and provisional estimates must remain provisional until final evidence exists. A tailored Store page is not successful because a selected audience shows a higher conversion number. A randomized Store experiment is not successful merely because a platform labels a treatment better. A lifecycle-targeted Store page is not successful because a lapsed or churned user clicks or reinstalls. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions, eCPM or short-run revenue; success requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core task completion, first value or useful return. **An impression-level revenue value is not decision-grade merely because the SDK emitted it; precision provenance and platform reconciliation must survive the pipeline.** Optimize qualified retained/reactivated specialist utility, evidence integrity, community trust and sustainable reconciled revenue—not content volume, top-of-funnel volume or raw ad inventory.
