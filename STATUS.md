## Latest validated addition — 247
Apple In-App Events are a selective zero-cost temporal discovery primitive, not an ASO keyword hack, release-note substitute, or marketing-created content calendar. Apple currently permits up to 10 published events and 15 approved events per app; events may last up to 31 days and become discoverable up to 14 days before starting. Event cards can surface on the product page, search and editorial/personalized discovery, support App Store start notifications, downloads, and deep-link continuity.

Apple explicitly excludes repetitive daily activities/rewards, price promotions without new content/features/goods, and general awareness promotions as good candidates. Therefore MintTap routine monthly distributions/data refreshes and LogMate routine logging/recency/import activity are not automatically events. A genuine bounded in-product experience must exist first.

FG0–FG5: `event identity → temporal integrity → specialist relevance → destination continuity → measurement integrity → reuse decision`.

Preserve: `new release ≠ In-App Event`; `routine recurring domain activity ≠ event`; `market/calendar moment ≠ in-product event`; `event impression ≠ acquisition quality`; `notification opt-in ≠ participation`; `download/open ≠ repeated specialist value`; `Store eligibility ≠ reason to manufacture an event`.
