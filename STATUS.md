## Current operating model
Store: `search intent → Q-class → Apple CPP / Google CSL → qualified acquisition → first value/useful return | Apple IAE = truthful time-bounded discovery/reactivation | Google Promotional content = eligibility/category/event-truth gated | external tags = origin measurement | peer benchmark = relative diagnostic | PPO = causal creative experiment only when evidence budget exists`.

093 governs organic search-intent routing: ranking is discovery, routing is qualification, and page conversion alone is insufficient. A keyword-specific page is intentional only at Q3+; Q4+ requires downstream intent-specific first-value/useful-return evidence. Default-first is preferred over variant proliferation.

Store trust uses 092 T0–T5. Persuasion surfaces and trust-evidence surfaces are separate: screenshots/copy may frame truthful benefits, while privacy/Data safety, developer identity/contact, support and accessibility must describe current reality. T3 cross-surface parity is the minimum state for intentional acquisition; trust metadata is not treated as an ASO ranking hack.

Organic/search uses 082 N0–N5; distribution uses 083 D0–D5; owned communities use 084 O0–O5; connected speakers use 085 C0–C5; reviews use 086 R0–R5. Localization uses 091 L0–L5: translated metadata is not treated as a qualified market until product semantics, specialist terminology, creative, first-value workflow and support boundaries reach L3.

Advertising remains governed by 069–073: `value block → candidate boundary → exposure quality → interaction geometry → content integrity → format → downstream effect`. 087 adds foreground F0–F5; 088 adds rewarded A0–A5; 089 adds banner B0–B5; 090 adds retained-utility economics E0–E5. Frequency caps, larger formats, higher CTR, higher eCPM, fill or short-term ad ARPU cannot convert an invalid placement or degraded specialist workflow into sustainable inventory. App Open, rewarded and collapsible banners are not default company-template slots.

## Progress interpretation
Do not report progress by file count. The highest-value question remains whether a target specialist can reach personal first value with acceptable effort, understand why to return, and encounter monetization only at legitimate boundaries. Store search routing must preserve the user's inferred job from query through product page to first value; a conversion uplift without downstream fit is not growth. Organic content must be useful before amplification; distribution must be useful in the receiving channel; owned community must be useful without an install; connected advocacy must not masquerade as independent demand; reviews must remain authentic evidence; localization must preserve specialist meaning rather than maximize translated coverage; Store trust surfaces must stay synchronized with shipped behavior rather than function as persuasive decoration; foreground monetization must consume otherwise-idle waiting; rewarded monetization must add optional surplus; banner monetization must occupy stable passive geometry rather than borrow space or clicks from active specialist interaction. Monetization is judged by cumulative revenue from retained useful users, not isolated eCPM, fill, CTR, impressions/session or short-term ARPU. Zero-cost growth and ad revenue compound by preserving specialist trust.
