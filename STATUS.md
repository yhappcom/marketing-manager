## Latest validated addition — 176
Apple currently permits an app deep link to be assigned to each Custom Product Page. On iOS 18/iPadOS 18 or later, Open from that CPP can route to a specific in-app destination. CPP itself is available from iOS/iPadOS 15+, so page visibility and deep-link eligibility are different states. Apple permits universal links or custom URLs, recommends universal links for a secure/integrated experience, advises avoiding unnecessary redirect/shortener layers, and requires the CPP/deep-link metadata to be approved before the deep link functions for users.

CN0–CN5: `intent identity → destination truth → capability eligibility → link integrity → review/release integrity → downstream-value validation`.

Preserve `CPP visible ≠ deep-link eligible`, `deep link configured ≠ approved/live`, `Open tap ≠ destination reached`, `destination reached ≠ first useful value`, `higher CPP conversion ≠ better Store-to-product continuity`, and `missing thresholded CPP analytics ≠ zero activity`.

The operating principle is promise continuity: a specialist Store promise should lead to the matching product value when a truthful stable destination exists, without bypassing onboarding, required setup, integrity checks, error recovery, or other prerequisites.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Store conversion is not the terminal objective. A Store metric label is not a stable semantic contract, a peer percentile is not an industry target, and a Store keyword assignment is not a ranking guarantee. Apple CPP and Google Play CSL objects retain platform-native semantics even when normalized to a common intent layer. A visible CPP is not proof that its deep link is eligible, approved, reached, or valuable. Sparse/suppressed evidence is inconclusive, not zero. Native attribution improves observability but not incrementality. Store/platform approval is not proof of demand or product-market fit. Consent does not create placement permission. Higher requests, impressions/user, CTR, refresh or full-screen exposure are not sustainable growth if they degrade traffic quality, privacy choice, core-task completion, first/restored value, or repeated useful use. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach the product value promised by the acquisition surface and repeat it, while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from clearly identified, privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states.
