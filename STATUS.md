## Latest validated addition — 164
Google Mobile Ads impression-level ad revenue (ILRD) supplies a paid-event value in micros plus currency and a precision type. Preserve `UNKNOWN`, `ESTIMATED`, `PUBLISHER_PROVIDED`, and `PRECISE` as different evidence states; a paid callback is not automatically exact realized revenue. Google recommends attaching the paid listener before showing the ad and forwarding data immediately when the callback fires to reduce missing callbacks/discrepancies.

CB0–CB5: `eligible natural state → impression identity → revenue-value/precision integrity → callback/completeness integrity → matched-boundary reconciliation → sustainable product-value decision`. Preserve `impression ≠ callback captured ≠ precise revenue ≠ reconciled account revenue ≠ sustainable incremental value`.

Interstitial revenue must be constrained to natural pauses/transitions. AdMob and Google Play guidance prohibit disruptive/recurring/unexpected implementations. Company optimization therefore targets reconciled revenue from policy-eligible natural states while preserving task completion and repeated useful value—not impressions/user, CTR, or eCPM alone.

For MintTap, Home remains ad-free. Audit whether current secondary placements have ILRD enabled/implemented and can be joined to non-sensitive stable post-task states. For LogMate, define the same event contract before monetized launch; flight entry/import/editing or any state where interruption can cause data loss/logging error is not an ad-optimization surface.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Native attribution improves observability but not incrementality. Sparse evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and discovery surface second. Store/platform approval or metadata is not proof of demand or product-market fit. Ad supply authorization is not revenue proof; an impression-level paid event is not necessarily precise or reconciled revenue; and higher short-term ad yield is not sustainable growth if it degrades core-task completion or repeated useful value. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural states that do not materially degrade that value.
