## Latest validated addition — 230
Google Play Promotional content is a Store-native discovery/re-engagement surface for fresh and timely major updates, time-limited events and qualifying offers. It can support opens/reinstalls and new downloads across Play surfaces, but it is not a universal equivalent of Apple In-App Events.

For apps, current Google documentation conditions Promotional content on Premium growth-tool eligibility. Google also requires content to be new/noteworthy and user-facing; generic service descriptions, evergreen content and routine events are ineligible. Events can be created at any time but submitted no earlier than 60 days before start, and Google says review can take up to four days.

Critical MintTap finding: current Google Play content-quality/failure guidance says Promotional content is not currently allowed for certain financial products/services and explicitly lists investing/trading tools, stocks, funds, shares, investment resources/management and financial utility services. MintTap therefore must not plan Google Play Promotional content as a current growth channel unless newer authoritative or app-specific evidence changes eligibility.

LogMate is not shown by the reviewed guidance as categorically excluded, but access is still unproven. It remains a conditional post-launch capability requiring actual Premium growth-tool access and a genuine qualifying product moment.

EP0–EP5: `platform/tool access identity → category/policy eligibility → moment eligibility → in-app truth/destination integrity → submission/distribution integrity → qualified-value decision`.

Preserve: `Apple In-App Events ≠ Google Play Promotional content`; `feature exists ≠ exact app has access`; `access ≠ category/content eligibility`; `eligibility ≠ approval`; `approval ≠ featuring/distribution`; `distribution/open ≠ first/restored specialist value`.
