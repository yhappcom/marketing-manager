## 075 — Store review service recovery and signal
`research/075_store_review_service_recovery_and_signal_gate.md`

Ratings/reviews are treated as a public service-recovery surface and sparse qualitative product signal before they are treated as an ASO score. Canonical review classes are R0 no actionable information, R1 support question, R2 reproducible friction, R3 confirmed defect/promise failure, R4 verified remediation, R5 repeated cross-user signal. Replies resolve the issue; they do not bargain for stars.

First-party platform refresh on 2026-09-17 confirms Google prohibits incentivized/manipulated reviews and advises replies to focus on the issue rather than request a higher rating. Play replies notify the reviewer, who may update their review. Apple supports public editable responses and allows an overview-rating reset at a new version, but the reset is irreversible after release, applies across countries/regions for that platform, and does not remove written reviews. Company default is therefore **do not reset Apple ratings** unless a materially different version has verified remediation and a precommitted release/reputation rationale.

Do not optimize rating-prompt timing until first value/useful return is verified. Review solicitation must not become a pre-value interruption or an incentive system.

## Progress interpretation
Do not report progress by file count. The highest-value question remains whether a target specialist can reach personal first value with acceptable effort, understand why to return, and encounter monetization only at legitimate, interaction-safe and semantically distinct boundaries. Acquisition adds another requirement: the source must recruit genuine product use rather than raw installs or ad interaction. Public reviews add a service-recovery requirement: resolve verified experience failures and feed recurring evidence back into product/claim decisions rather than managing reputation by incentives, rating pressure, or cosmetic resets. Weak activation is not repaired by increasing ad pressure, translating strings without workload parity, scaling traffic before acquisition is earned, fragmenting sparse Store traffic, stopping sparse experiments on noise, marketing roadmap/UI shell as finished workflow, treating whitespace/foreground/friction as inventory, treating accidental clicks as success, making native ads resemble professional content, or optimizing stars while the underlying failure remains.
