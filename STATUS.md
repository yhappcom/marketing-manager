## Latest validated addition — 138
Canonical principle: **The owned blog is a specialist knowledge product first and a distribution channel second. For finance/tax-adjacent MintTap content, publishing velocity and keyword coverage are not goals; each page needs a distinct user job, evidence/provenance, original non-commodity utility and editorial responsibility.**

Google's current Search guidance gives stronger trust consideration to YMYL topics that can affect financial stability. Current spam policy defines scaled content abuse independently of creation method and explicitly includes large volumes of low-value generative-AI content; doorway abuse also covers substantially similar pages created for similar queries that funnel users onward. Google's May 2026 generative-search guidance continues to prioritize valuable, unique, non-commodity content and ordinary SEO fundamentals rather than a separate GEO/AEO content factory.

**BD3** requires a self-contained page that solves the specialist question without requiring an install, authoritative sourcing/date discipline for consequential claims, clear product affiliation/responsibility, original explanation/calculation/workflow value, and no near-duplicate ticker/keyword variants. **BD4** evaluates intended specialist query discovery and qualified downstream behavior, not page count/index count/impressions alone. **BD5** updates, consolidates or retires pages as evidence changes.

MintTap must not automatically create one page per YieldMax ticker. Ticker-specific pages require materially ticker-specific evidence, calculation, history, edge case or workflow. Tax/accounting explanations must separate documented app behavior from professional tax advice and preserve authoritative provenance. LogMate inherits the same evidence discipline for aviation/regulatory claims, with jurisdiction/version dates.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. An owned blog is not successful because it publishes many pages, ranks for many ticker variants or accumulates impressions; finance-adjacent content requires evidence-led specialist utility and trust. AI assistance is not disallowed, but low-value scaled publishing is incompatible with the canonical strategy and current Google spam policy. A tailored Store page is not successful because a selected audience shows a higher conversion number. A randomized Store experiment is not successful merely because a platform labels a treatment better: its native experiment contract and downstream specialist utility must be preserved. A lifecycle-targeted Store page is not successful because a lapsed or churned user clicks or reinstalls: success requires restored core value and useful return. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. Optimize qualified retained/reactivated specialist utility, community trust and reconciled compatible revenue—not content volume, top-of-funnel volume or raw impression count.
