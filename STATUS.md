## Latest validated addition — 237
Apple App Store Connect campaign links provide first-party attribution for zero-cost channels, but attribution is a measurement contract rather than proof of causality. App Store Connect distinguishes search, browse, app referrer, web referrer and custom campaigns; campaign links can connect campaign tokens to product-page/download and downstream usage/sales/subscription evidence. First-time-download campaign credit uses a 24-hour window, later eligible campaign touches can supersede earlier ones, and manual redownload can reset the acquisition source used for subsequent sales/usage/subscription attribution.

Sparse-niche interpretation must preserve privacy thresholds. Campaign/dashboard metrics require minimum counts, individual campaign data begins after at least five first-time-download users, and detailed reports can withhold/combine very small cohorts. Missing data is therefore unknown/below-threshold rather than zero. iOS web-referrer semantics are also browser-dependent: Safari website referrals can be web referrers while non-Safari browser taps can appear as app referrers.

EW0–EW5: `decision identity → source/link identity → attribution-window integrity → sparse/privacy integrity → downstream-value integrity → qualified channel decision`.

Preserve: `campaign click ≠ download`; `attributed download ≠ causal proof`; `missing metric ≠ zero`; `web referrer ≠ all web-origin demand`; `source attribution ≠ immutable lifetime origin`; `last eligible campaign ≠ sole causal influence`; `first-time download ≠ retained specialist user`.
