## Latest validated addition — 200
Apple App Store Connect campaign links provide a first-party, privacy-aware way to distinguish marketing routes and connect campaign tokens to Store and downstream metrics. They are not causal attribution. Apple currently documents a 24-hour first-download attribution window and last-campaign credit behavior when multiple campaign links are used.

Campaign measurement is sparse-cohort constrained. The Campaigns capability is unavailable before an app has Analytics data; Apple says it may appear only after the app has been live and generating downloads for at least 24 hours. Dashboard metrics require a minimum threshold of 5 in the selected range, campaign data requires at least five individual first-time-download users, and detailed reports can withhold/combine very small cohorts for privacy.

DL0–DL5: `route identity → token integrity → attribution-window integrity → privacy/sparsity integrity → source-semantic integrity → qualified-value decision`.

Preserve: `campaign credit ≠ causal acquisition source`, `last campaign credit ≠ first discovery`, `missing metric ≠ zero`, `5-user threshold ≠ statistical sufficiency`, `web referrer ≠ all web-originated traffic`, `browser app referrer ≠ necessarily app-native promotion`, `/us/ URL ≠ US user`, `download ≠ specialist-value completion`, and `campaign-token proliferation ≠ better measurement`.

Company-wide, maintain a campaign registry joining route/surface, placement, audience job, claim, destination/default-vs-CPP, locale/territory context, provider/campaign token, source/referrer semantics, threshold state and downstream specialist/repeat value. Do not over-segment sparse niche traffic until every cohort becomes privacy-suppressed.
