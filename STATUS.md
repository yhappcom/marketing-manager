## Latest validated addition — 148
Store ratings are high-visibility trust evidence but sparse professional apps are unusually easy to distort through small, selected cohorts. Apple and Google both allow legitimate rating requests while prohibiting manipulation/incentivized feedback. The company therefore optimizes `earned review opportunity after meaningful product value`, not rating level or review volume.

BL0–BL5 separates platform legality, experienced-user eligibility, non-interruptive request governance, authenticity/support separation, issue-specific review-response routing and evidence interpretation. Eligibility must be based on objective product experience, not predicted sentiment. Do not use `Are you enjoying the app?` or equivalent positive-user gating to send only happy users to the Store. Do not reward reviews, organize insider reviews, ask reviewers to raise stars, or condition support/features on rating behavior. Platform request limits are ceilings/behavioral constraints, not company request targets.

Apple recommends rating requests at appropriate post-task moments without interruption and allows the system prompt to be requested up to three times in 365 days; its current guidance also says review responses should address feedback without marketing/spam and permits factual re-engagement when a later release fixes a reported issue. Google Play prohibits fraudulent/incentivized ratings and explicitly says developer replies should not ask for a higher rating. Google generally delays public ratings/reviews around 24 hours for abuse checks and weights the displayed Play rating toward recent ratings. Google's Reply to Reviews API exposes written reviews but not rating-only feedback, so response datasets are censored subsets rather than complete user sentiment.

For MintTap, rating requests are excluded from transaction entry/editing, ROC/tax interpretation/adjustment, data/account recovery and first-value onboarding. Financial-correctness reviews route to BE rather than generic marketing replies. For LogMate, future requests are excluded from flight entry/import, duplicate resolution, totals correction and sync/backup recovery; regulatory/logbook-correctness criticism will route to the aviation authority hierarchy.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store rating system is not successful because it maximizes stars or review count; its signal must arise from genuine sufficiently informed experience without sentiment selection, incentives or insider distortion. A brand-owned community is not an independent forum merely because discussion is user-generated. A connected founder/employee/family/advocate endorsement is not independent user evidence merely because the relationship is disclosed. Disclosure does not create permission to promote and does not cure unsupported claims. A consequential financial claim is not trustworthy merely because it comes from an official-looking page. A Store treatment is not successful merely because a platform reports a better conversion number. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions, eCPM or short-run revenue; success requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value. Promotion permission is contextual and revocable; useful participation must survive even when no product link is allowed. Community replies and Store reviews can be high-value product/content/trust evidence without being representative samples of all users.
