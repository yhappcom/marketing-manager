## Latest validated addition — 112
Canonical principle: **Age rating is product-capability disclosure and distribution eligibility, not a conversion knob. Never optimize questionnaire answers for reach; make them match the shipping product.**

Apple's current global system uses 4+, 9+, 13+, 16+, and 18+, with region-specific outcomes where required. The product page exposes the rating and can expose declared capabilities/controls. The questionnaire includes capabilities directly relevant to our apps, including advertising, UGC and messaging/chat. An unrated app cannot be published.

As of September 2026, Apple requires new-version/update submissions to declare whether the app contains social-media capabilities. A declared social-media capability can produce a Social Media descriptor and minimum 13+ treatment unless the documented under-13 handling applies. This is a release-change trigger, not a marketing tactic.

AD0–AD5 governs age-rating evidence. AD0 is material contradiction/manipulation. AD3 is minimum deliberate-acquisition readiness: shipping capabilities, questionnaire answers, regional outputs/descriptors, EULA/minimum-age policy, Store/public claims and release triggers are consistent. AD4 adds release/regional regression control; AD5 is reusable across apps.

For MintTap, advertising must be included in the audit. Future community/social, chat, UGC or unrestricted-web capabilities re-enter AD review before release. YieldMax specialization alone does not establish an Apple age threshold. For LogMate, professional-pilot positioning likewise does not justify guessing a rating.

No evidence was established that a lower age rating mechanically improves Store ranking or conversion.
