## Latest validated addition — 192
After seller authorization/readiness, mediation optimization still requires sufficient legitimate traffic. AdMob mediation A/B tests require at least 10,000 ad requests before AdMob can determine a result and can run for up to 90 days. This platform minimum is not proof of statistical power for every business/UX metric.

DD0–DD5: `eligible-traffic identity → request-volume sufficiency → configuration isolation → supply-state stability → revenue-and-UX outcome integrity → decision/reversibility`.

Preserve: `10,000 requests ≠ guaranteed statistical power`, `experiment eligibility ≠ permission to manufacture ad exposure`, `estimated monthly earnings ≠ finalized cash`, `higher eCPM ≠ sustainable product revenue`, `waterfall optimized eCPM ≠ real-time bid`, and `mediation winner ≠ product winner` without core-value guardrails.

Sparse niche apps must not increase interruption frequency, invent placements, prolong banner exposure or weaken protected workflows merely to reach an experiment threshold. If legitimate eligible traffic is insufficient, classify the test as not currently viable and wait or use observational diagnostics.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, installs, impressions, CTR, rewarded acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised specialist value. Advertising contributes only incremental, reconciled revenue from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states through an authorized and active supply path. Monetization experiments must use legitimate traffic rather than manufacture user interruption. Product release readiness, ad-supply activation readiness and monetization-experiment readiness are separate tracks.
