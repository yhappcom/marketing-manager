## Latest validated addition — 109
Canonical principle: **Reddit is no longer only a feed/community referral channel. Public posts and comments can become source material for AI-generated answers, so specialist contributions must remain useful even when the reader never clicks MintTap, LogMate or an outbound link. Optimize for answerability and source credibility, not AI-visibility hacks.**

Reddit's official AI-search documentation states that AI search synthesizes real posts/comments and provides inline citations to original sources plus related communities. Reddit Answers merged into Reddit search on 2026-05-26. Reddit's 2026-09-10 changelog adds a fresh distribution change: from mid-September an `Ask` tab beside ordinary search-result tabs is being tested with a small subset of global users across platforms. This is an experiment, not universal rollout.

AA0–AA5 governs deliberate Reddit answerability. AA3 is the minimum threshold: answer a real specialist question natively, preserve assumptions/scope/uncertainty and evidence boundaries, use stable professional terminology, provide required authoritative evidence, make the answer useful without an outbound link, disclose product affiliation where relevant, keep product mention subordinate to the answer, and respect subreddit promotion rules plus Reddit spam policy.

AI-search exposure does not relax saturation controls. Reddit's current spam policy prohibits repetitive mass posting for exposure/financial gain and spam proliferation using automation/AI. Promotional content is not inherently spam, but community-specific rules control what is acceptable. Ticker-swapped copies of the same MintTap mechanism are therefore not a legitimate AI-search strategy.

For MintTap, durable question families include reverse-split tracking, distribution mechanics and ROC bookkeeping; investment recommendations, expected returns and unverified tax conclusions remain outside the marketing claim ceiling. For LogMate, technically precise answers to real pilot/logbook workflow questions have more option value than repeated launch promotion, but marketing must never imply regulatory/legal/company acceptance beyond verified scope.

Do not use inferred `AI citation count` as a growth KPI without a trustworthy denominator. Observable evidence remains community outcome, legitimate route attribution where present, branded/direct discovery where measurable, Store acquisition, first value and K3 useful return. A zero-click answer can still be successful if it fully resolves the specialist question and builds durable category trust.

## Current operating model

Zero-cost distribution: `real specialist question → evidence → AA3+ community-native answer → {direct reader | native search | AI synthesis} → optional source/product discovery → qualified acquisition → first value → useful return`. Permission, relationship disclosure, saturation controls and community-specific rules remain mandatory. Privacy suppression and zero-click resolution are not automatically zero value; labor remains an economic cost.

Retention uses K0–K5. Platform reopen/install persistence metrics remain diagnostics, not the company retention objective. K3 requires a job-specific useful-return event at its natural cadence.

Advertising remains governed by 069–073 plus F/A/B/E/U gates. U3 is the minimum deliberate monetization threshold: legitimate placement + impression-revenue precision semantics + stable K3 denominator. Higher eCPM, CTR, fill or short-term ARPU never validates degraded specialist utility.

Store assets use W0–W5. Accessibility disclosures use Y0–Y5. Deliberate market/language expansion requires Z3 served-experience control. Rating solicitation uses R0–R5; R3 is required before deliberate prompt optimization, and Store rating is never a substitute for product-quality evidence.
