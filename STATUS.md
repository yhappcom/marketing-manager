## Latest validated addition — 187
Native styling is not permission to make paid content indistinguishable from specialist utility. Current Google Mobile Ads documentation requires ad attribution and AdChoices for native ads, routes registered asset clicks/impressions through the SDK, and warns against custom click handlers over/within the native ad view. SDK native impressions can be recorded when the first pixel becomes visible, so an impression is not evidence of meaningful attention. Native Validator provides pre-release test-ad checks for certain native placement violations and should be preserved as QA evidence rather than disabled merely to silence findings.

CY0–CY5: `specialist-job identity → paid-content identity → interaction-boundary integrity → rendering/SDK integrity → exposure-quality integrity → sustainable-value decision`.

Preserve: `native styling ≠ content impersonation`, `SDK impression ≠ meaningful attention`, `higher CTR ≠ higher-quality monetization`, `feed/list slot ≠ ad permission`, `required attribution ≠ sufficient UX separation`, `Native Validator pass ≠ product-fit approval`, and `policy-compliant placement ≠ sustainable placement`. Paid units must never impersonate product records, statistics, warnings or CTAs, and revenue gains that depend on ambiguity or accidental-click risk are invalid optimization.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, pre-registrations, pre-orders, installs, impressions, CTR or estimated ad revenue alone. Store conversion, web traffic and ad yield are not terminal objectives. Platform Store/Search/ad objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window. Reddit promotion is governed per community; permission does not guarantee reachability, and owning a community does not create a right to mobilize its members into third-party communities. Owned-web publishing must serve distinct specialist demand rather than manufacture query coverage. Search Console visible queries are privacy-filtered/coverage-limited evidence rather than a complete demand census. Impression-level ad values preserve precision/provenance and remain distinct from finalized publisher earnings. Ad inventory must fit genuine product states: loading, transition and screen-space semantics cannot be manufactured merely to increase impressions. Native advertising must remain visibly and behaviorally separate from specialist product content; an SDK impression or CTR increase does not prove useful attention or high-quality monetization. Sparse, suppressed, anonymized, filtered, inconclusive, estimated or unattributed evidence is not zero. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states within deliberate interruption and separation boundaries.
