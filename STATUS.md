## Latest validated addition — 179
Apple App Store pre-order and Google Play pre-registration are platform-managed commitment/delivery systems, not generic waitlists. Apple first-release pre-orders use a 2–180-day release window. Google Play pre-registration has a maximum 90-day campaign and pausing does not stop that clock. Platform permission therefore does not imply launch readiness.

CQ0–CQ5: `release-state identity → promise readiness → window viability → delivery/test integrity → metric identity → launch-value validation`.

Preserve: `pre-launch commitment ≠ install`, `automatic delivery ≠ first open`, `first open ≠ useful value`, `gross Apple pre-orders ≠ net pre-orders`, `Apple pre-order conversion ≠ Google pre-registration conversion`, `Google 14-day conversion ≠ pure production-launch install`, `tester ≠ ordinary pre-registrant`, and `platform permits activation ≠ release is ready`.

Operating principle: activate a Store commitment window only after release confidence, production-near testing, truthful localized Store/support/privacy surfaces, first-use/value readiness and a commitment→launch→value measurement contract exist. For LogMate, Google Play's 90-day clock makes premature activation especially costly. Pre-registration rewards are not a default tactic because they add one-time-product implementation and delivery obligations.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Store conversion is not the terminal objective. Platform Store objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration is not a generic waitlist: it creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window whose value depends on release readiness and downstream product value. Sparse, suppressed, inconclusive or unattributed evidence is not zero. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states.
