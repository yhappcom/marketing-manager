## Latest validated addition — 210
Google Play peer benchmarks are platform-native diagnostic context and must not inherit Apple peer semantics. Play uses curated groups for sensitive/business metrics and custom groups for other comparison classes. Curated membership is intentionally undisclosed, uses at least 100 apps, excludes low-performing/abandoned apps, and exposes aggregate medians/percentiles. Aggregated custom groups require 8–12 apps and are editable at most three times per month; public-data comparisons can have different rules.

DV0–DV5: `metric/surface identity → peer-class identity → geography/period/monetization integrity → aggregation/privacy/availability integrity → downstream diagnostic triangulation → intervention threshold`.

Preserve: `curated peer ≠ named competitor set`, `peer median ≠ product target`, `percentile ≠ market rank`, `benchmark unavailable ≠ poor performance`, `Store conversion gap ≠ ASO diagnosis`, `Play acquisition metric ≠ ad-platform install metric`, and `benchmark improvement ≠ sustainable revenue improvement`.

Operational consequence: use Play peer position to choose what to investigate, not what tactic to execute. Preserve exact surface, metric, peer class, geography, period and monetization/filter state. Triangulate Store conversion with source, activation, specialist-workflow completion and repeat value; triangulate crash/ANR gaps with affected device/geography cohorts. Never broaden specialist positioning or add intrusive monetization merely to move a peer percentile.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store conversion, peer percentile, experiment count, impressions, CTR or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value. Apple and Google Play benchmarks provide contextual diagnosis, not competitive truth or targets, and their percentile systems must not be merged. Attribution improves evidence but does not convert platform credit into causal truth; sparse, aggregated, private or unavailable observations remain unknown rather than zero.
