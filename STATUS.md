## Latest validated addition — 126
Canonical principle: **revenue must be attributed to an eligible user state before it can justify monetization pressure.** Aggregate eCPM, impressions or estimated revenue alone are not decision-grade evidence for a sparse professional app.

AR3 requires paid-event capture before show; value/currency/precision retention; format/unit/source context; canonical placement and eligibility-state IDs; separate opportunity/request/fill/show/paid denominators; duplicate-revenue prevention; downstream first-value/useful-return/task-continuation guardrails; privacy documentation; and reconciliation against finalized AdMob reporting. AR4 requires incremental revenue to remain compatible with retained specialist utility and the AM–AQ placement gates.

Google's impression-level values carry precision classes (UNKNOWN, ESTIMATED, PUBLISHER_PROVIDED, PRECISE), so they must not be silently treated as equally exact. iLAR is behavioral decision evidence; finalized AdMob reporting remains accounting truth.
