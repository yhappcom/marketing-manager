## Latest validated addition — 202
Google Play Custom Store Listings (CSLs) are a zero-cost route-to-message matching mechanism, not an attribution substitute. Google currently permits up to 50 CSLs and supports targeting by country/region, pre-registration, Play search keyword, user-state segments, custom audiences, ads traffic, or a unique developer-defined `listing` URL parameter. CSLs can vary app name, icon, descriptions and graphic assets while shared contact/privacy/category metadata remains common.

A unique CSL URL identifies the Store destination shown to a route; it does not establish that the route caused acquisition. Keep CSL identity separate from DM's UTM source/campaign, traffic-source semantics, sparse `Other` rows and downstream product value. CSLs also do not receive automatic translations: a default language must be selected and translations deliberately supplied.

DN0–DN5: `route identity → routing integrity → promise integrity → localization integrity → measurement integrity → scale/retirement decision`.

Preserve: `unique CSL URL ≠ attribution truth`, `CSL visit ≠ UTM-attributed visit`, `higher CSL conversion ≠ better retained specialist users`, `more CSLs ≠ better segmentation`, `up to 50 allowed ≠ 50 strategically useful`, `targeted country ≠ localized language`, and `route-message match ≠ permission to change product truth`.

For sparse niche apps, create a CSL only when a durable route has meaningfully different specialist intent, production truth supports a tailored promise, and traffic can support interpretation. Do not create pages per post/ticker/social item merely because the platform allows many listings.
