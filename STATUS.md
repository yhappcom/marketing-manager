## Latest validated addition — 208
Reddit Pro is currently a free beta suite for eligible businesses/organizations in English-speaking countries where Reddit operates. Trends can monitor keywords and surface where/how they are discussed; Smart keywords can add contextual signals such as conversation volume and related terms. Coverage is incomplete by design and excludes several classes of Reddit content, so absence in Trends is not evidence of absence of demand.

Performance measures the business account’s organic posts/comments/account separately from paid media and supports CSV export. Mod Insights remains a different evidence layer for owned-community traffic, membership/activity and moderation health. Reddit’s own 2026 community metrics also emphasize weekly visitors/contributions over lifetime subscriber counts because subscriber totals can reflect community age more than current activity.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, karma, submissions, nominations, installs, campaign/UTM-token count, CSL/CPP/deep-link count, experiment count, event-card count, tag count, star averages, impressions, CTR or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value. Attribution improves evidence but does not convert platform credit into causal truth; sparse, aggregated or privacy-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero. Segmented Store pages and owned app links improve continuity only when intent, promise, destination, fallback and maintenance remain aligned. Search-distributed social evidence and Reddit-native demand sensing add free discovery layers; owned communities add specialist trust/support/research infrastructure. None substitutes for permission, useful contribution, qualified downstream app value, or repeated specialist utility.
