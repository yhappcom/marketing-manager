## Latest validated addition — 169
App-open and ordinary interstitial ads are separate full-screen inventory classes with different semantic eligibility. Technical events such as app foreground, route changes, button taps and launch events are not monetization permissions.

Google documents app-open ads for app loading/foregrounding. On cold start, if main content becomes available before the ad is ready, do not show the late-arriving ad. Do not show app-open on the first app start; wait until users have used the app. Loaded app-open references expire after four hours.

Interstitials belong at natural pauses/completed transitions. Google Play prohibits unexpected full-screen interstitials when a user has chosen another action; an ad inserted after a button tap but before the expected action completes is specifically the wrong semantic boundary.

CG0–CG5: `state identity → task/transition completion → format eligibility → freshness/readiness → exposure/revenue observability → preserved repeated value`.

Preserve `foreground ≠ eligible waiting state`, `cold start ≠ permission`, `loaded app-open object ≠ unexpired/showable inventory`, `navigation tap ≠ natural transition`, `show attempt ≠ impression`, and `policy-eligible ≠ product-appropriate`.

For MintTap and LogMate, full-screen inventory must never become a workaround around ad-free/core-workflow decisions. First launch/onboarding, unfinished-task restoration, financial/flight data entry or correction, import/error recovery and other context-sensitive professional work remain excluded regardless of fill opportunity.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Native attribution improves observability but not incrementality. Sparse evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and discovery surface second. Store/platform approval or metadata is not proof of demand or product-market fit. Ad supply authorization is not revenue proof. A foreground event is not an eligible app-open exposure; a loaded app-open object is not necessarily fresh/showable; a navigation tap is not a natural interstitial transition; a native/banner/collapsible/reward event is not automatically a revenue or value event. Higher request/refresh/full-screen exposure volume or short-term ad yield is not sustainable growth if it delays first/restored useful value, interrupts professional work, or degrades core-task completion and repeated useful use. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from clearly identified, natural or genuinely voluntary states that do not materially degrade that value.
