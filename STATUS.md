## Latest validated addition — 186
Ad-format capability is not placement permission. Current Google guidance defines app-open ads as loading-screen inventory and says not to show them on first app start; on cold start they belong strictly to a genuine loading state, and should not appear after main content is already reached. Interstitials belong at natural task transitions/pauses, while Google Play prohibits unexpected full-screen interstitials. Anchored banners occupy persistent layout space and refresh only while visible, making occupied attention/screen time a product cost that impression counts alone do not capture.

CX0–CX5: `specialist-job identity → format-semantic fit → critical-value protection → interruption/exposure budget → revenue/value evidence join → sustainable decision`.

Preserve: `format supported ≠ placement appropriate`, `foreground event ≠ app-open opportunity`, `navigation event ≠ natural interstitial transition`, `available screen space ≠ non-critical ad space`, `banner impressions ≠ occupied-attention cost`, `frequency-cap capability ≠ target frequency`, and `higher short-term ad revenue ≠ sustainable revenue`. Never manufacture loading waits/transitions to create inventory. Protect first useful value, required setup, data entry, validation/recovery and primary specialist information from full-screen monetization by default.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, pre-registrations, pre-orders, installs, impressions or estimated ad revenue alone. Store conversion, web traffic and ad yield are not terminal objectives. Platform Store/Search/ad objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window. Reddit promotion is governed per community; permission does not guarantee reachability, and owning a community does not create a right to mobilize its members into third-party communities. Owned-web publishing must serve distinct specialist demand rather than manufacture query coverage. Search Console visible queries are privacy-filtered/coverage-limited evidence rather than a complete demand census. Impression-level ad values preserve precision/provenance and remain distinct from finalized publisher earnings. Ad inventory must fit genuine product states: loading, transition and screen-space semantics cannot be manufactured merely to increase impressions. Sparse, suppressed, anonymized, filtered, inconclusive, estimated or unattributed evidence is not zero. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states within a deliberate interruption budget.
