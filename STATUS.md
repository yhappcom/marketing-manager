## 065–067, 076–081 — Store routing, sparse experiments, event discovery, attribution, and benchmark interpretation
Intent-specific Store pages are routing infrastructure, not automatic ASO wins. Sparse traffic is both acquisition inventory and learning sample. Randomization requires an evidence budget and precommitted stopping/interpretation rules. MintTap and LogMate remain premature for Store randomization.

Apple CPP and Google Play CSL support deterministic specialist-intent routing before randomization when search intent and the underlying promise are earned. Apple In-App Events add truthful time-bounded discovery/reactivation. Google Play Promotional content is not a symmetric default counterpart: it is available to all games but only eligible apps, and current Google guidance restricts certain finance/investment-related promotional content. MintTap therefore must not plan this as growth inventory; LogMate remains future-conditional on Console eligibility and real event truth.

Zero-cost external distribution uses `source_family → intent_family → campaign_wave`, then joins Store acquisition to first value/useful return where technically available. Apple campaign reporting has minimum/privacy thresholds, so suppressed data is unknown, not zero. Google Play exposes UTM source/campaign and traffic-source breakdowns; organic parent totals must not be added to their search/browse children. Tracking never overrides community permission.

Apple peer-group benchmarks add relative funnel context for eligible conversion/retention/crash metrics, but are not specialist product-value targets or causal evidence. A strong Store percentile with weak first value is a promise-to-product mismatch risk; weak Store conversion with strong specialist first value is a message/routing investigation candidate. Benchmark unavailable is not poor performance.

Canonical distinction:
`CPP/CSL = stable intent routing | Apple IAE = truthful time-bounded discovery/reactivation | Google Promotional content = eligibility + category + event-truth gated | external campaign tags = origin measurement, not value proof | peer benchmark = relative diagnostic, not target | PPO = causal creative experiment when evidence budget exists`.

## 082 — people-first specialist content and organic search integrity
Blog/search growth now uses N0–N5 eligibility. Search demand alone is N0/N1; generic accurate summaries are at most N2. N3 requires materially distinct specialist utility; N4 adds trust-complete sourcing/authorship/scope/update discipline and is the target for finance, tax-adjacent, aviation and regulatory content. N5 additionally requires observed qualified downstream behavior.

Do not mass-produce ticker/airport/aircraft/country permutations, cosmetically refresh dates, chase unrelated trends, or use AI automation to manufacture search inventory. Every article must remain worth publishing without Google traffic or an app CTA. `SoftwareApplication` structured data is an app-page understanding/eligibility layer, not a ranking substitute; never fabricate ratings/reviews to satisfy markup.
