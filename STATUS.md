## Latest validated addition — 224
Google's current UMP guidance requires consent information to be refreshed on app launch and warns against treating locally cached consent state as canonical. Ad requests are gated by UMP `canRequestAds`; this state must be distinguished from consent-form completion, generic active use, and emitted requests. When privacy options are required, the app must expose a usable entry point so choices can be revisited. Applicable European personalized-ad serving also retains Google's certified-CMP/TCF requirements.

The operational correction is upstream-denominator integrity: revenue, request rate, fill, AU/AV and ARPU/ARPV can move because the ad-eligible population or measurement observability changed, not because auction or placement quality changed.

EJ0–EJ5: `implementation identity → consent-state freshness → request-eligibility integrity → choice/revocation integrity → denominator/observability integrity → sustainable decision`.

Preserve: `consent decision ≠ permanent consent state`; `form completed ≠ ad-request eligible`; `canRequestAds false before update ≠ refusal`; `ad-eligible active user ≠ all active users`; `request opportunity ≠ emitted request`; `consent rate ≠ growth KPI`; and `analytics decline ≠ necessarily product-use decline`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store conversion, generic retention, peer percentile, impressions, CTR, dashboard eCPM, ARPV, consent rate or estimated impression revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value through a reliable experience, with monetization that respects current privacy choice and does not corrupt that value. Attribution is not causality; sparse/aggregated/private/unavailable or threshold-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero. Advertising decisions retain consent/ad-request eligibility, exact format, placement, geometry, lifecycle, traffic quality, delivered-content/control state, auction/floor configuration, exposure distribution, frequency-cap/exposure-budget state and revenue semantics.
