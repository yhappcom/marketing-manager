## 074 — acquisition traffic-quality firewall
`research/074_organic_acquisition_traffic_quality_firewall.md`

Acquisition quality now extends through legitimate ad opportunity, rather than stopping at install or activation. Canonical source classification is Q0 unknown, Q1 manipulated/prohibited mechanism, Q2 unqualified reach, Q3 qualified acquisition/downstream unknown, Q4 qualified retained traffic, Q5 sustainable monetizable traffic.

Google first-party AdMob policy refreshed 2026-09-17 confirms invalid traffic includes artificial impressions/clicks and accidental clicks; traffic quality can affect ad serving; publishers remain responsible for traffic quality. It prohibits ordinary-ad calls such as asking users to click/view ads to support the publisher. Internal rule is stricter: community, blog, referral and social CTAs recruit **product use**, never ad interaction.

Do not infer that Reddit/social/direct/community traffic is invalid merely from source category. No reviewed first-party source supports that claim. Evaluate acquisition mechanism, specialist relevance, downstream first value/useful return, legitimate ad opportunity and policy/traffic-quality signals.

For current niche apps, a high-install community post remains Q2/Q3 until downstream evidence exists. MintTap source families are not assumed Q4/Q5. LogMate cannot reach Q4/Q5 until a production specialist first-value workflow exists.

## Progress interpretation
Do not report progress by file count. The highest-value question remains whether a target specialist can reach personal first value with acceptable effort, understand why to return, and encounter monetization only at legitimate, interaction-safe and semantically distinct boundaries. Acquisition adds another requirement: the source must recruit genuine product use rather than raw installs or ad interaction. Weak activation is not repaired by increasing ad pressure, translating strings without workload parity, scaling traffic before acquisition is earned, fragmenting sparse Store traffic, stopping sparse experiments on noise, marketing roadmap/UI shell as finished workflow, treating whitespace/foreground/friction as inventory, treating accidental clicks as success, or making native ads so visually integrated that they can be mistaken for professional app content.
