## Latest validated addition — 110
Canonical principle: **For a sparse niche app, product quality is part of zero-cost distribution infrastructure. Do not accelerate acquisition while the platform can observe avoidable user loss, instability or weak sustained value.**

Google Play explicitly evaluates quality using user metrics/in-app evaluation including user loss, DAU/MAU and experience signals such as ad load, usability, performance and feature depth. Published beta requirements for certain Play details-page quality treatments include user loss <5%, DAU/MAU >8%, sufficient users and measurement across at least 24 days in a 30-day period. These are treatment-eligibility criteria, not universal ranking thresholds.

Android vitals explicitly states core vitals affect Play visibility. Current overall bad-behavior thresholds include user-perceived crash rate 1.09% and user-perceived ANR rate 0.47%, with separate device thresholds and a rolling 28-day quality window. Memory/bitmap/code-optimization visibility effects announced for February 2027 are future-state evidence, not current ranking claims.

Apple publicly names text relevance and customer behavior, including downloads and ratings/reviews, among App Store search factors. No equivalent Apple-published crash/ANR ranking threshold was established in this research; do not invent one.

AB0–AB5 governs the release-to-growth boundary. AB0 includes known severe crash/ANR/data-loss/deceptive or unusable core paths and blocks marketing acceleration. AB3 is the minimum deliberate acquisition-scaling state: production-valid core jobs, applicable Play quality issues controlled/investigated, first value and K3 useful return defined, regressions owned, and acquisition judged downstream rather than by installs alone. Sparse/insufficient platform samples remain unknown rather than pass/fail.

For MintTap, a zero-cost post that produces installs but increases rapid uninstall, support failure or loss of useful return is not a marketing success. Calculation/data-integrity defects are trust failures even when they do not cross crash thresholds. Ad-load increases must remain subordinate to retained utility and Play quality signals.

For LogMate, AB3 should cover production-valid manual entry, persistence, totals/search, offline/backup and recovery before community launch acceleration. Flight-log data loss/corruption is an AB0 blocker regardless of healthy crash metrics.
