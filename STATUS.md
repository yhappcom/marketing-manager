## Latest validated addition — 140
Canonical principle: **Optimize incremental reconciled ad revenue subject to non-degradation of the app's core professional task. Do not optimize ad exposure itself.**

Current Google Play policy prohibits unexpected full-screen interstitials when a user has chosen to do something else. AdMob warns against repeated interstitials; its implementation ceiling must not be treated as a recommended MintTap/LogMate frequency. Rewarded ads require a clear value exchange and affirmative opt-in (or rewarded-interstitial intro/opt-out), and refusing/skipping cannot impede normal use. Banner guidance emphasizes separation from interactive elements; collapsible banners should not be requested for every anchored banner because of disruption risk. Apple 2.5.18 likewise treats clear identification, non-manipulative interaction and visible close/skip controls as compliance floors, not product-fit evidence.

**BF2** requires a natural eligible-user-state contract: exact trigger, exclusions, cap/cooldown, dismiss/opt-out semantics and AR-compatible revenue instrumentation. A state manufactured solely to create ad inventory fails BF2. **BF3** requires reconciled revenue normalized to the eligible state plus core-value guardrails such as task completion/abandonment, first-value exposure and useful return. **BF4** requires credible incremental revenue without material core-value degradation; inconclusive sparse-niche evidence remains inconclusive rather than justifying more interruption. **BF5** requires durable useful-return economics plus rollback/kill rules.

Total impressions, impressions/session, session duration, ad CTR, eCPM, fill rate and raw ad revenue are diagnostic metrics, not standalone optimization targets. MintTap and LogMate are professional utilities; brief successful sessions can be high-value sessions.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. An owned blog is not successful because it publishes many pages, ranks for many ticker variants or accumulates impressions. A consequential financial claim is not trustworthy merely because it comes from an official-looking page: the source must be authoritative for that exact claim, and provisional estimates must remain provisional until final evidence exists. A tailored Store page is not successful because a selected audience shows a higher conversion number. A randomized Store experiment is not successful merely because a platform labels a treatment better. A lifecycle-targeted Store page is not successful because a lapsed or churned user clicks or reinstalls. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. **An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions, eCPM or short-run revenue; success requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core task completion, first value or useful return.** Optimize qualified retained/reactivated specialist utility, evidence integrity, community trust and sustainable reconciled revenue—not content volume, top-of-funnel volume or raw ad inventory.
