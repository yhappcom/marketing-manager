## Stage 2 integrated decision system

1. **021 VOI/reversibility:** decide whether evidence is worth acquiring and route to ACT+MONITOR, OBSERVE/TRIANGULATE, CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT, RESEARCH FIRST, or DEFER.
2. **020 sparse inference:** separate validity, effect estimate, uncertainty and practical significance; predeclare MPME; INVALID and INCONCLUSIVE are distinct.
3. **022 stopping:** `HEALTH LOOK ≠ DECISION LOOK`; distinguish fixed horizon, genuine sequential inference and operational staged commitment; use explicit stop states.
4. **023 transfer/decay:** mechanism-first T0–T4; trigger-based evidence decay; never transport policy, regulation, factual truth or permission by analogy.
5. **024 portfolio allocation:** allocate constrained Marketing/Design/Web/Engineering/qualified-traffic capacity to decision packages at the current bottleneck, not equally by app or task count. Include maintenance tail and reuse value; avoid uncalibrated universal weighted scores.

Canonical marginal allocation question:

`If only one additional focused block of the constrained capability were available, which decision package would most improve or clarify the current limiting factor without disproportionate maintenance or trust cost?`

## Application-readiness progress

### Evidence provenance and baseline measurement — COMPLETE V1

`playbook/EVIDENCE_PROVENANCE_BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md` now defines the minimum trustworthy evidence layer.

New operating rules:

- Preserve platform-native metric definitions; derive company metrics separately.
- `UNKNOWN`, `SUPPRESSED`, `NOT_INSTRUMENTED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `INVALID`, and numeric zero are distinct states.
- Apple and Google Store funnels must retain separate native layers; do not force them into one synthetic CVR.
- Apple's current App Store Connect Conversion Rate is total downloads + pre-orders divided by unique-device impressions; it is not a generic product-page-view-to-install rate.
- Apple usage analytics are opt-in and privacy/data thresholds can suppress observations; campaign/source dimensions have their own eligibility/threshold rules.
- Google Play changed store-listing performance reporting in 2026 toward visitors/clicks/CTR and install/open/pre-register intent. Historical acquisition-rate series must carry a metric-definition break rather than being spliced into the new series.
- Attribution stops where measurement stops. Temporal correlation between Reddit/blog/social activity and downloads is not silently upgraded to channel attribution.
- Every decision-relevant datum should carry source, native definition, window, filters/population, collection method, missingness/privacy limitations, transfer class and decay trigger.
- Baselines are bounded by product/store/analytics changes and natural workflow cycles, not a universal 7/28/30-day convention.

MintTap and LogMate minimum baseline packs are specified without fabricated activation/retention values. Product-specific definitions remain live-validation items.
