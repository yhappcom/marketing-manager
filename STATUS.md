## Latest validated addition — 215
Google Mobile Ads SDK can expose impression-level paid-event values with currency and precision semantics. UNKNOWN, ESTIMATED, PUBLISHER_PROVIDED and PRECISE must remain distinct; an impression-level value is not automatically final/settled revenue. Paid-event capture should be registered before display and monitored for missing/duplicate events. Interstitials belong at natural workflow transitions, while repeated interruption and banners adjacent to interactive controls create UX/accidental-click/invalid-activity risk.

EA0–EA5: `revenue-event identity → capture integrity → placement integrity → utility guardrail → revenue reconciliation → sustainable decision`.

Preserve: `impression ≠ precise realized revenue`; `ILRD ≠ settled revenue`; `ESTIMATED ≠ PRECISE`; `higher CTR ≠ better monetization`; `policy ceiling ≠ target frequency`; `more impressions/session ≠ higher sustainable LTV`; and `eligible ad surface ≠ permission to interrupt specialist work`. Optimize reconciled revenue per qualified repeated-value user subject to utility/trust/policy constraints, not raw impressions, CTR or dashboard eCPM.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store conversion, install persistence, generic retention, peer percentile, experiment count, impressions, CTR, dashboard eCPM or impression-level estimated revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value through a reliable technical experience, with monetization that does not corrupt that value. Apple and Google Play benchmarks provide contextual diagnosis, not competitive truth or targets. Retention-like and technical-quality metrics must retain exact event, cohort and denominator semantics. Privacy/disclosure integrity must be reconstructed from shipped behavior and SDK configuration, not inferred from Store forms or vendor capability lists. Impression-level ad revenue must retain precision/currency/source semantics and be reconciled before it becomes LTV or placement-decision evidence. Attribution improves evidence but does not convert platform credit into causal truth; sparse, aggregated, private or unavailable observations remain unknown rather than zero.
