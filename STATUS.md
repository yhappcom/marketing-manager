## Latest validated addition — 147
Community permission and speaker disclosure are separate controls. FTC guidance treats employment, financial, personal and family relationships as potentially material connections and requires unexpected material connections affecting endorsement credibility to be disclosed clearly and conspicuously. Profile/about-page disclosure, presumed audience knowledge, vague shorthand and platform disclosure controls are not safe substitutes for disclosure visible with the endorsement. The FTC's review rule adds a stronger social-proof constraint: even disclosed insider reviews can be deceptive when insider activity materially distorts aggregate ratings.

BK0–BK5 therefore records actor relationship, venue permission, in-message disclosure where material, claim substantiation, anti-manipulation controls and audit evidence. For MintTap, `I built MintTap` is materially stronger transparency than an apparently independent investor account recommending the app. `No link` does not remove promotional character, and disclosure never overrides a community ban on promotion. Employees, family, moderators or advocates must not be orchestrated to manufacture independent-looking Store reviews, Reddit consensus, votes, ratings or testimonials.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A brand-owned community is not an independent forum merely because discussion is user-generated. A connected founder/employee/family/advocate endorsement is not independent user evidence merely because the relationship is disclosed. Disclosure does not create permission to promote and does not cure unsupported claims. A consequential financial claim is not trustworthy merely because it comes from an official-looking page. A Store treatment is not successful merely because a platform reports a better conversion number. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions, eCPM or short-run revenue; success requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value. Promotion permission is contextual and revocable; useful participation must survive even when no product link is allowed. Community replies can be high-value product/content/trust evidence without being acquisition evidence.
