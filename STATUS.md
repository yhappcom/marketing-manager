## Latest validated addition — 149
Research 148 governed how authentic ratings/reviews are earned and answered. Research 149 establishes what reputation becomes downstream. Apple currently states that ratings/reviews can appear in search results and are among customer-behavior inputs used by App Store search. The exact ranking weight is not published, so review volume must not be translated into deterministic rank claims.

Apple's summary rating is territory-specific. Apple also now generates LLM review summaries from individual reviews on product pages in the US and eight additional English storefronts and refreshes them over time. Therefore Store reputation is not merely a support inbox or global star average: it can become territory-specific search/conversion evidence and platform-generated summary evidence. Written-review datasets remain censored subsets of users/raters; Google's Reply to Reviews API, for example, exposes comment-bearing reviews but not rating-only feedback.

BM0–BM5 governs `authenticity inheritance → territory/version surface identity → discovery-role evidence → censored theme extraction → product/Store routing → downstream validation`. Apple rating resets are not cosmetic reputation-cleaning tools: Apple recommends sparing use, few ratings may discourage downloads, and written reviews remain after a summary-rating reset.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store rating system is not successful because it maximizes stars/review count, and a review-analysis system is not valid merely because it extracts themes from a self-selected written-review subset. Store reputation must arise from genuine informed experience, preserve territory/version context, and route recurring criticism to the responsible product/evidence system. Apple-generated review summaries are platform-generated evidence, not company-authored proof. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions/eCPM/short-run revenue; it requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value.
