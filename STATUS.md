## Latest validated addition — 185
Google Mobile Ads paid-event callbacks provide impression-level value, currency and precision, with available ad-source/mediation provenance. Precision is substantive: `UNKNOWN`, `ESTIMATED`, `PUBLISHER_PROVIDED`, and `PRECISE` must remain distinct. Google recommends registering the paid listener before display and forwarding events immediately to reduce callback loss/discrepancy.

CW0–CW5: `event identity → value/precision identity → source/mediation identity → exposure/value join integrity → report/reconciliation integrity → sustainable-value decision`.

Preserve: `paid event ≠ finalized cash`, `estimated impression value ≠ precise value`, `sum(local impression-level values) ≠ guaranteed finalized payout`, `eCPM ≠ user-level realized revenue`, `higher impressions/user ≠ better monetization`, and `frequency-cap capability ≠ optimal frequency`. AdMob reports are estimated until month-end; invalid-traffic and other finalization adjustments mean local impression-level events are a decision/measurement stream, not an accounting ledger. Monetization changes must join revenue to consent, exposure intensity, specialist core value and return behavior.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, pre-registrations, pre-orders, installs, impressions or estimated ad revenue alone. Store conversion, web traffic and ad yield are not terminal objectives. Platform Store/Search/ad objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window. Reddit promotion is governed per community; permission does not guarantee reachability, and owning a community does not create a right to mobilize its members into third-party communities. Owned-web publishing must serve distinct specialist demand rather than manufacture query coverage. Search Console visible queries are privacy-filtered/coverage-limited evidence rather than a complete demand census. Impression-level ad values preserve precision/provenance and remain distinct from finalized publisher earnings. Sparse, suppressed, anonymized, filtered, inconclusive, estimated or unattributed evidence is not zero. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states.
