## Latest validated addition — 197
Research 197 closes the Apple-side zero-cost editorial-distribution gap. App Store Connect Featuring Nominations let eligible roles submit an app launch, meaningful new content, or an app enhancement for editorial consideration, with explicit platform, territory and localization scope. Approved/published In-App Events can be attached, and up to five supplemental URLs can support the story, including documentation, art assets and TestFlight public links. Accessibility, inclusivity, priority and unique app/team details are valid supporting context.

DI0–DI5: `opportunity identity → editorial-worthiness integrity → specialist-value evidence → story/evidence integrity → territory/localization continuity → outcome/reuse decision`.

Preserve: `nomination submitted ≠ Apple editorial selection`, `selected/featured ≠ qualified acquisition`, `qualified acquisition ≠ repeated specialist value`, `release exists ≠ editorial-worthy release`, `zero media spend ≠ zero preparation cost`, `more nominations ≠ more sustainable organic distribution`, `supplemental material ≠ permission for unsupported claims`, `global availability ≠ global nomination relevance`, and `In-App Event attached ≠ featuring guaranteed`.

For sparse niche apps, nomination is an editorial pitch backed by real product evidence, not a recurring ASO task. Routine MintTap distribution/data refreshes and routine LogMate maintenance are not manufactured into featuring stories. Submitted, selected/featured, Store exposure and downstream specialist value remain separate evidence states.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, nominations, installs, tag count, promotional-event count, star averages, review counts, impressions, CTR, rewarded acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised specialist value. Store discovery metadata, Apple-created tags, Google Play Promotional content and Apple editorial nominations must remain truthful representations of supported specialist jobs, not mechanisms for manufacturing broad relevance, artificial novelty or editorial-worthiness. Advertising contributes only incremental, reconciled revenue from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states through an authorized and active supply path. Product release readiness, Store-discovery integrity, editorial/re-engagement-event integrity, feedback integrity, ad-supply activation readiness and monetization-experiment readiness are separate tracks.
