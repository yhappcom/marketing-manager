## Latest validated addition — 129
Canonical principle: **ownership of a subreddit creates governance responsibility, not an exemption from trust constraints.**

Reddit's current Moderator Code requires brand/company communities to be clearly identified as official when officially affiliated (and unofficial when not). Reddit provides a Brand Affiliate tag for commercially intended/incentivized contributions, while contributors remain responsible for required disclosures. Moderator rules also prohibit using a community to coordinate interference in other communities and require clear expectations and active moderation.

AU3 therefore requires explicit affiliation, stable rules, contextual commercial disclosure where applicable, genuine support/feedback utility, no cross-community coordination, no promotional automation/unsolicited outreach, identifiable product announcements, active moderation operations, tolerance of legitimate criticism, and Store links only where useful. AU4 requires repeated evidence that the community produces specialist/support utility and genuine participation without promotional pressure.
