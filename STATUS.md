## Latest validated addition — 174
Apple now documents an organic-search role for Custom Product Pages beyond unique campaign URLs: approved keywords from the latest approved app version can be assigned to a CPP, allowing that CPP rather than the default page to appear for those selected searches. Apple recommends unique keyword sets per CPP. CPPs remain review-gated, localizable and measurable at page level; Apple currently permits up to 70 CPPs, but capacity is a ceiling rather than a sparse-niche operating target.

This creates a zero-cost intent-routing layer: `validated search intent → keyword-routed CPP → truthful specialist proof → download → matching in-app value`. It does not create new keyword inventory, guarantee ranking/impressions, or prove incrementality.

CL0–CL5: `intent evidence → product truth → routing identity → asset coherence → Store evidence → product-value validation`.

Preserve `keyword assigned ≠ ranking gained`, `CPP visible ≠ guaranteed impression`, `higher CPP conversion ≠ incremental acquisition`, `higher CPP conversion ≠ better retained user`, `70 available CPPs ≠ reason to create 70`, and `Apple aggregate CPP lift ≠ MintTap/LogMate forecast`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. A Store metric label is not a stable semantic contract, a peer percentile is not an industry target or causal diagnosis, and a keyword assignment is not a ranking guarantee. Native attribution improves observability but not incrementality. Sparse evidence remains inconclusive and suppressed/missing observations are not zero. Accessibility is a product capability first and discovery surface second. Store/platform approval or metadata is not proof of demand or product-market fit. Ad supply authorization is not revenue proof. Consent is not a one-time boolean and consent compliance does not create placement permission. A foreground event is not an eligible app-open exposure; a navigation tap is not a natural interstitial transition; an eligible transition is not unlimited exposure permission. Higher requests, impressions/user, CTR, refresh or full-screen exposure are not sustainable growth if they increase traffic-quality risk, delay first/restored useful value, interrupt professional work, manipulate privacy choice, or degrade core-task completion and repeated useful use. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from clearly identified, privacy-eligible, natural or genuinely voluntary states at exposure pressure that preserves traffic quality and product value.
