## Latest validated addition — 167
Rewarded inventory is a voluntary value exchange, not permission to degrade the free product. Ordinary rewarded ads require affirmative per-instance opt-in plus clear disclosure of the action and reward; declining/skipping must not interfere with normal use. Rewarded interstitial is different: it may appear automatically at a natural transition, but requires a pre-ad intro screen with a functioning opt-out.

AdMob prohibits direct monetary rewards such as cash, cryptocurrency and gift cards. Permitted indirect/non-monetary rewards must remain within the publisher platform/app and be non-transferable. For MintTap, a reward must never be framed as yield, dividend, return, portfolio value or another financial benefit.

Reward-event semantics must remain separate. Under Google-served rewarded ads the earned-reward callback precedes dismissal, while mediated sources can determine callback ordering. Therefore dismissal/impression is not reward truth; grant logic must be idempotent and independently observable.

CE0–CE5: `core-access boundary → reward legitimacy → choice/disclosure integrity → event/reward integrity → revenue reconciliation → sustainable-value decision`.

Canonical chain: `eligible optional enhancement → truthful reward offer → explicit choice/valid opt-out → actual ad exposure → earned-reward callback → idempotent grant → redemption/use → reconciled revenue → preserved repeated core value`.

Preserve `reward offer ≠ opt-in ≠ impression ≠ earned reward`, `dismissal ≠ earned reward`, `earned callback ≠ successful grant`, `grant ≠ redemption`, and `policy-eligible ≠ product-appropriate`.

For MintTap and LogMate, do not invent a rewarded feature merely to create inventory. Existing core tracking/logbook utility, saved data, correction, accuracy/safety-sensitive workflows and normal continuation remain outside the reward gate. A future treatment requires a genuinely optional, low-risk secondary enhancement and CB-compatible revenue/value measurement.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. Native attribution improves observability but not incrementality. Sparse evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and discovery surface second. Store/platform approval or metadata is not proof of demand or product-market fit. Ad supply authorization is not revenue proof; a banner request is not an impression, a collapsible request is not necessarily an expanded exposure, a reward offer is not an opt-in, an impression/dismissal is not an earned reward, and an impression-level paid event is not necessarily precise or reconciled revenue. Higher request/refresh volume, repeated expanded overlays, coerced rewarded views, or short-term ad yield is not sustainable growth if it degrades core-task completion or repeated useful value. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural or genuinely voluntary states that do not materially degrade that value.
