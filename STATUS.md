## Latest validated addition — 136
Canonical principle: **Apple PPO and Google Play Store Listing Experiments are both randomized Store experiments, but they are not one interchangeable statistical contract. Preserve native experiment semantics before comparing downstream business outcomes.**

Current Apple PPO Analytics uses Bayesian statistical analysis designed for App Store product-page data. Results appear after at least five first-time downloads are associated with the test; this is a reporting threshold, not proof of adequate sample size. Current native states include Collecting Data, Performing Better, Performing Worse and Likely to be Inconclusive. Performing Better/Worse requires at least 90% confidence against the selected baseline. Apple exposes estimated conversion rate, estimated relative lift and a 90% credible interval.

A PPO can contain up to three treatments and a developer-selected treatment traffic percentage; treatment traffic is divided across treatments. Localizations can be included/excluded. More treatments can lengthen the path to a conclusion. Tests run for up to 90 days unless manually stopped; a stopped test cannot be restarted. `Likely to be Inconclusive` means current traffic/results suggest the test may not gather enough evidence within the test horizon to reach 90% confidence; it is not proof of zero lift, failure or equivalence.

Apple's pre-test duration estimate uses historical daily impressions/new downloads and a selected desired conversion improvement, but Apple states the estimate is informational and does not alter the test. Version releases or overlapping metadata/asset changes can contaminate interpretation. PPO is not available for Custom Product Pages: CPP remains deterministic intent routing; PPO remains randomized creative evidence on the ordinary product-page population.

**BB3** requires the preserved native contract: hypothesis, baseline, treatment asset delta/count, treatment traffic %, localization scope, dates, five-first-download reporting state, estimated conversion rate/lift, confidence, credible interval where exposed, exact result state, 90-day censor/inconclusive state, and version/metadata changes, interpreted under AY. **BB4** additionally requires downstream qualified acquisition, first value and useful return. **BB5** retains wins, losses, inconclusive and contaminated/stopped tests as reusable experiment memory.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. A tailored Store page is not successful because a selected audience shows a higher conversion number. A randomized Store experiment is not successful merely because a platform labels a treatment better: its native experiment contract and downstream specialist utility must be preserved. Apple's five-first-download PPO display threshold is not statistical sufficiency; `Likely to be Inconclusive` is legitimate sparse-niche evidence. Google's BA contract and Apple's Bayesian BB contract must not be flattened into one statistical model. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. Deterministic intent routing and randomized creative experiments answer different questions. Optimize qualified retained specialist utility, community trust and reconciled compatible revenue—not top-of-funnel volume or raw impression count.
