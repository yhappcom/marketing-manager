## Latest validated addition — 139
Canonical principle: **A financial claim may only be stated at the epistemic level supported by its source. For MintTap, source authority, claim type, effective date, jurisdiction and provisional/final status travel with the claim; later final evidence supersedes but does not erase earlier provisional evidence.**

YieldMax's own fund materials state that displayed ROC figures are estimates that can later be characterized differently. SEC-filed Section 19 notices likewise state that distribution-source allocations are estimates and are not provided for final tax reporting. IRS Topic 404 and Publication 550 are authoritative for general US federal nondividend-distribution/basis mechanics, but they are not evidence for Korean tax treatment. Distribution amount/rate and estimated ROC must never be presented as investment performance.

**BE3** requires claim-specific primary evidence plus date, jurisdiction and explicit evidence status (`provisional`, `final`, `historical`, `superseded`). **BE4** requires refresh/supersession triggers and consistent propagation. **BE5** reconciles later final evidence against earlier estimates while retaining the historical provenance trail. Aggregators are discovery aids, not silent canonical overwrite sources for consequential financial facts.

MintTap tax-adjustment documentation must distinguish product accounting behavior from legal/tax conclusions. Korea-specific tax claims require current Korean primary authority and, where operational withholding/refund behavior matters, evidence of that treatment; US issuer/IRS material alone is insufficient.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. An owned blog is not successful because it publishes many pages, ranks for many ticker variants or accumulates impressions. A consequential financial claim is not trustworthy merely because it comes from an official-looking page: the source must be authoritative for that exact claim, and provisional estimates must remain provisional until final evidence exists. AI assistance is not disallowed, but low-value scaled publishing is incompatible with the canonical strategy. A tailored Store page is not successful because a selected audience shows a higher conversion number. A randomized Store experiment is not successful merely because a platform labels a treatment better. A lifecycle-targeted Store page is not successful because a lapsed or churned user clicks or reinstalls. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. Optimize qualified retained/reactivated specialist utility, evidence integrity, community trust and reconciled compatible revenue—not content volume, top-of-funnel volume or raw impression count.
