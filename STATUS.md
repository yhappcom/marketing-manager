## New retained conclusions from research 019

Launch is not one announcement day. Company launch logic is now:

`evidence readiness → product/release readiness → store readiness → measurement readiness → seed owned assets → permission-checked community work → optional pre-order/pre-registration → controlled release → qualified activation observation → retention/promise diagnosis → distribution expansion → evergreen acquisition`

Key rules:
- optimize launch for qualified first cohorts, learning quality and durable distribution, not launch-day raw downloads;
- do not set a public launch date merely because the binary is technically ready;
- require audience/problem, product/value, Store, measurement and distribution readiness gates;
- pre-order/pre-registration is optional and admitted only when a reachable pre-launch audience, credible launch timing and measurable learning/intent-capture job exist;
- current Apple first-release pre-order supports a release date 2–180 days ahead; current Google Play pre-registration has a 90-day campaign window per country/region;
- pre-order/pre-registration counts are intent signals, not activation or retention;
- Apple phased release and Google Play staged rollout are **update mechanisms, not first-launch acquisition strategies**;
- activate launch channels sequentially enough to verify the live Store/product path and preserve interpretable evidence;
- diagnose weak acquisition in order: discoverability → intent → store/proof → activation → retention → durability; do not use more traffic as the default remedy for downstream failure;
- maintain promise consistency across community/social/content → Store → screenshot proof → first-use reality → monetization;
- launch-week samples are often sparse; UNKNOWN/SUPPRESSED/INCONCLUSIVE rules still apply;
- successful cash-light launch transitions into evergreen Store/search/content/community/product loops rather than remaining dependent on launch-week posting.
