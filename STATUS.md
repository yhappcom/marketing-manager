## Latest validated addition — 221
AdMob eCPM floors are auction controls, not revenue KPIs. A higher floor can raise observed eCPM while reducing eligible demand/match or serving; therefore higher eCPM does not establish higher total or sustainable revenue. Current Google documentation exposes Google-optimized High, Medium and All-prices modes plus manual/disabled floors, warns that higher floors can reduce serving/match rate, supports country-specific manual overrides, and notes that new or recently changed units/floors/traffic/placements may need more than a week before floor behavior stabilizes. Floor scope must also be preserved: do not assume an AdMob floor universally constrains third-party waterfall demand.

EG0–EG5: `configuration identity → auction-denominator integrity → geography/source integrity → stabilization & sparse-evidence integrity → specialist-utility integrity → sustainable-revenue decision`.

Preserve: `higher eCPM ≠ higher total revenue`; `higher floor ≠ better auction outcome`; `floor setting ≠ universal mediation floor`; `global floor ≠ country-specific effective floor`; `short post-change observation ≠ stable treatment effect`; and `dashboard eCPM ≠ reconciled sustainable revenue`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store conversion, generic retention, peer percentile, impressions, CTR, dashboard eCPM or estimated impression revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value through a reliable experience, with monetization that does not corrupt that value. Attribution is not causality; sparse/aggregated/private/unavailable observations remain unknown rather than zero. Advertising decisions retain exact format, placement, geometry, lifecycle, traffic-quality, delivered-content/control state, auction/floor configuration and revenue semantics. The product does not manufacture friction, accidental interaction, inappropriate creative exposure or sacrifice specialist readability merely to create additional ad inventory or improve eCPM.
