## Latest validated addition — 198
Research 198 closes the Apple-side Store-native event-discovery/reactivation gap. Apple In-App Events can appear on the product page, in search, and in editorial/personalized surfaces, and can serve acquisition, active-user engagement, or lapsed-user reactivation. Apple explicitly rejects repetitive activities, price promotions without new content/features/goods, and generic app-awareness promotions as good event candidates. Events are therefore a truthful timely-experience surface, not recurring free ad inventory.

DJ0–DJ5: `surface/eligibility identity → event truth/temporality → audience-purpose integrity → promise-to-destination continuity → measurement-semantic integrity → qualified-growth decision`.

Preserve: `release/update exists ≠ In-App Event`, `timely label ≠ genuinely time-bounded user value`, `published event ≠ featured event`, `event purpose selected ≠ audience delivered`, `event impression ≠ engagement`, `event page view ≠ app open`, `app open ≠ specialist-value completion`, `download ≠ retained specialist user`, `missing sparse event analytics ≠ zero event activity`, and `more event cards ≠ more sustainable organic discovery`.

Apple permits an event to last up to 31 days and be promoted up to 14 days before start. Deep links should route to the promised event state; universal links are preferred. Event analytics can include impressions, page views, opens, downloads, subscriptions and sales, but individual event data appears only after at least five first-time downloads. Sparse-data absence must therefore remain semantically distinct from observed zero.

MintTap must not manufacture IAEs from ordinary YieldMax distribution/data/ROC refreshes, routine tax-data maintenance or generic portfolio promotion. LogMate must not manufacture them from maintenance, parser fixes or generic pilot-logbook promotion.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, nominations, installs, event-card count, tag count, star averages, impressions, CTR, rewarded acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value. Store discovery metadata, Apple-created tags, Google Play Promotional content, Apple editorial nominations and Apple In-App Events must remain truthful representations of supported specialist jobs, not mechanisms for manufacturing broad relevance, artificial novelty or pseudo-events. Advertising contributes only incremental, reconciled revenue from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states through an authorized and active supply path. Product release readiness, Store-discovery integrity, editorial/event integrity, feedback integrity, ad-supply readiness and monetization-experiment readiness remain separate tracks.
