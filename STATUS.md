## Latest validated addition — 201
Google Play zero-cost route measurement requires a platform-specific contract rather than copying Apple Campaign Link semantics. Current Play Console Conversion analysis can break Store-listing performance down by traffic source, Store listing, country/region, language, UTM source/campaign and install state. Store-listing reports cover defined Store surfaces and explicitly do not include every Play surface such as Promotional content.

Store Performance exports expose UTM source/campaign for third-party referrals, but Google notes that sparse values can collapse to `Other` when minimum thresholds are not reached. Missing named UTM rows or `Other` therefore cannot be interpreted as zero route traffic.

Google Analytics/Firebase install-referral semantics are separate: source `google-play` can represent either a Play-listing deep link carrying a referrer or organic Play discovery. It is not proof of organic Play search.

DM0–DM5: `surface identity → route/token integrity → source-semantic integrity → sparse/privacy-threshold integrity → conversion-window/denominator integrity → qualified-value decision`.

Preserve: `UTM-tagged Store visit ≠ causal acquisition proof`, `GA/Firebase google-play ≠ necessarily organic search`, `not attributed ≠ low-quality`, `Other ≠ zero`, `missing detailed UTM row ≠ zero`, `Store acquisition ≠ first_open`, `install ≠ retained user`, `Store conversion ≠ specialist-value completion`, and `more UTM tokens ≠ better measurement`.

Company-wide, Apple DL and Google Play DM share one business acquisition registry but retain platform-native source semantics, thresholds, denominators and surfaces. Normalize route/audience job/claim/destination/value completion; never force Apple and Play attribution labels into a false one-to-one mapping.
