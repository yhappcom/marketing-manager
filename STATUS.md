## Latest validated addition — 193
Research 192 established whether a sparse-niche mediation experiment is viable. Research 193 establishes how to interpret a viable experiment without conflating assignment, platform scaling and realized business outcomes.

AdMob mediation A/B tests split app users between variants; Variant B receives 1%–50% of users and allocation is fixed after launch. This is not request-level randomization. AdMob requires at least 10,000 ad requests before it can determine a result, while scaled monthly earnings starts only after >=4 days and >=10,000 impressions. These are separate thresholds with separate semantics.

Scaled monthly earnings is a platform-normalized estimate of 28-day earnings if a variant were applied to 100% of users. With unequal allocation, Variant B metrics are scaled for comparison. It is neither observed arm revenue nor guaranteed/finalized post-rollout revenue.

DE0–DE5: `experiment identity → assignment identity → treatment isolation → metric-semantic integrity → product-value join → deployment decision`.

Preserve: `user allocation ≠ request-level randomization`, `10,000 requests ≠ 10,000 impressions`, `scaled monthly earnings ≠ observed arm revenue`, `scaled monthly earnings ≠ finalized earnings`, `platform leader ≠ guaranteed future winner`, and `mediation winner ≠ product winner`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, installs, impressions, CTR, rewarded acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised specialist value. Advertising contributes only incremental, reconciled revenue from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states through an authorized and active supply path. Monetization experiments must use legitimate traffic, preserve user-level assignment and distinguish observed outcomes from platform-scaled estimates and post-deployment realized revenue. Product release readiness, ad-supply activation readiness and monetization-experiment readiness are separate tracks.
