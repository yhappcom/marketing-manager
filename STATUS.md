## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, nominations, installs, campaign/UTM-token count, CSL/CPP/deep-link count, experiment count, event-card count, tag count, star averages, impressions, CTR or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value. Attribution improves evidence but does not convert platform credit into causal truth; sparse, aggregated or privacy-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero. Segmented Store pages and owned app links improve continuity only when intent, promise, destination, fallback and maintenance remain aligned. Search-distributed social evidence adds a free demand-sensing layer, but Google Search/Discover performance, native social performance and app acquisition remain separate measurement systems.
