## Current operating model
Store discovery mechanisms remain distinct: `Apple CPP / Google CSL = deterministic intent routing | Apple IAE = truthful time-bounded discovery/reactivation | Google Promotional content = eligibility/category/event-truth gated | external tags = origin measurement | peer benchmark = relative diagnostic | PPO = causal creative experiment only when evidence budget exists`.

Organic/search content uses 082 N0–N5. Search demand alone does not create content eligibility. N3 requires materially distinct specialist utility; N4 adds trust-complete sourcing/authorship/scope/update discipline and is the target for finance, tax-adjacent, aviation and regulatory content; N5 additionally requires qualified downstream evidence.

083 adds D0–D5 distribution eligibility. The canonical N3/N4 article is the maintained evidence object; Reddit/social/community units are native adaptations, not duplicated promotional payloads. D3 requires standalone on-platform value, D4 adds transparent provenance + subordinate CTA + channel permission, and D5 requires qualified downstream behavior without moderation/spam/trust incidents. Search canonicalization handles representative URLs; it does not justify low-value page multiplication. Reddit reposting is a mechanism, not blanket permission.

Advertising remains governed by 069–073: `value block → candidate boundary → exposure quality → interaction geometry → content integrity → format → downstream effect`. Organic/community acquisition recruits product use, never ad interaction. Reviews remain service-recovery/product signals before ASO score optimization.
