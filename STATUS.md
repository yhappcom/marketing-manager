## Latest validated addition — 175
Current Google Play Console Help documents Search keywords as a Custom Store Listing audience. Publishers can select Play Search keywords known to bring traffic, search for additional candidates, and inspect/select spelling corrections and translations contained in each keyword variation bundle. This creates a zero-paid-media Play intent-routing layer that complements—but is not semantically identical to—Apple CPP keyword routing.

Google currently permits up to 50 CSL pages. CSLs can customize app name, icon, descriptions and graphic assets, but are not automatically translated. Search-keyword CSL must remain distinct from country/region, unique-URL, ads-traffic, pre-registration, churn/lapse and other CSL routes.

CM0–CM5: `live-intent evidence → keyword-bundle identity → product-truth match → localization integrity → route/evidence identity → downstream-value validation`.

Preserve `selected keyword ≠ ranking gained`, `selected keyword ≠ guaranteed impression`, `keyword bundle ≠ seed phrase only`, `higher CSL conversion ≠ incremental acquisition or retained value`, `50 available CSLs ≠ reason to create 50`, `search CSL ≠ other CSL route`, `Google CSL keyword object ≠ Apple CPP keyword object`, and `Gemini suggestion ≠ validated claim/publish-ready copy`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma, pre-registrations, pre-orders or installs. A Store metric label is not a stable semantic contract, a peer percentile is not an industry target or causal diagnosis, and a Store keyword assignment/selection is not a ranking guarantee. Apple CPP and Google Play CSL search-keyword objects must retain their platform-native semantics even when normalized to a common intent layer. Native attribution improves observability but not incrementality. Sparse evidence remains inconclusive and suppressed/missing observations are not zero. Accessibility is a product capability first and discovery surface second. Store/platform approval or metadata is not proof of demand or product-market fit. Ad supply authorization is not revenue proof. Consent is not a one-time boolean and consent compliance does not create placement permission. A foreground event is not an eligible app-open exposure; a navigation tap is not a natural interstitial transition; an eligible transition is not unlimited exposure permission. Higher requests, impressions/user, CTR, refresh or full-screen exposure are not sustainable growth if they increase traffic-quality risk, delay first/restored useful value, interrupt professional work, manipulate privacy choice, or degrade core-task completion and repeated useful use. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from clearly identified, privacy-eligible, natural or genuinely voluntary states at exposure pressure that preserves traffic quality and product value.
