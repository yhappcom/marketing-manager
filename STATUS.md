## Latest validated addition — 244
Acquisition growth and ad monetization must be joined during traffic-quality incidents. Google makes publishers responsible for traffic quality and prohibits self/live-ad testing, artificial or incentivized interaction, automated/bot traffic, deceptive placement and other invalid activity; AdMob development/testing must use test ads/test devices. Google also recommends segmenting traffic meaningfully, including app, ad unit and country.

An ad-serving limit is an assessment/enforcement state, not proof that a particular community/social/organic channel is invalid and not evidence that more inventory is needed. Preserve the exact Policy Center state and onset, compare it with legitimate acquisition/release events, verify test-device separation, audit accidental-click placement risk, and join that evidence with FA–FC delivery/revenue evidence before changing growth or monetization.

FD0–FD5: `event identity → acquisition-change identity → test/developer integrity → placement/interaction integrity → traffic-quality evidence → sustainable decision`.

Preserve: `ad-serving limit ≠ proof a specific marketing channel is invalid`; `traffic spike ≠ invalid traffic`; `organic/social traffic ≠ automatically safe or unsafe`; `high CTR ≠ successful monetization`; `revenue drop ≠ need for more ads`; `test success with live ads ≠ acceptable QA`; `mediation availability ≠ permission to route around unresolved traffic-quality risk`.
