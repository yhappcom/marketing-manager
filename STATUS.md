## Latest validated addition — 137
Canonical principle: **Google Play Custom Store Listings are not only acquisition-intent surfaces; current Play Console supports native targeting of prior-user lifecycle/value states. Acquisition and reactivation must therefore remain separate funnels.**

Current documented CSL targeting includes churned users, lapsed users (no app open in the last 28 days), lapsed + churned users, non-buyers, one-time buyers, repeat buyers, lapsed buyers (no purchase in the last 180 days), custom audiences, plus country/region, pre-registration, search keywords, unique URL and ads traffic. Up to 50 CSLs can be created. Name, icon, descriptions and graphic assets can vary; CSLs are not automatically translated.

**BC3** requires exact native segment definition, legitimate specialist re-entry job, truthful differentiated promise, listing/localization scope, dates/semantic version, AZ-preserved Store metrics, explicit prior-user state, restored-core-value event and useful-return event. **BC4** requires qualified reactivation evidence beyond Store interaction/reacquisition. **BC5** preserves lifecycle-specific wins, losses, insufficient evidence and retired hypotheses.

Do not infer Apple parity: current Apple CPP evidence in the repository establishes URL/keyword/localization/deep-link routing, not an equivalent native churned/lapsed/buyer-state targeting contract. Preserve `platform_native_targeting_mode`.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. A tailored Store page is not successful because a selected audience shows a higher conversion number. A randomized Store experiment is not successful merely because a platform labels a treatment better: its native experiment contract and downstream specialist utility must be preserved. A lifecycle-targeted Store page is not successful because a lapsed or churned user clicks or reinstalls: success requires restored core value and useful return. Apple's five-first-download PPO display threshold is not statistical sufficiency; `Likely to be Inconclusive` is legitimate sparse-niche evidence. Google's BA contract and Apple's Bayesian BB contract must not be flattened into one statistical model. Google Play lifecycle CSL targeting must not be projected onto Apple without native evidence. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. Deterministic intent routing, lifecycle re-entry routing and randomized creative experiments answer different questions. Optimize qualified retained/reactivated specialist utility, community trust and reconciled compatible revenue—not top-of-funnel volume or raw impression count.
