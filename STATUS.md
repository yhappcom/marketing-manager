## Latest validated addition — 213
Apple default product-page promotional text is a zero-cost Store message-freshness surface: up to 170 characters, displayed above the description, and editable without submitting a new app version. It is not an ASO keyword field: Apple explicitly states promotional text does not affect search ranking. This creates a controlled way to communicate genuinely current product facts without waiting for another binary release, but also creates stale-claim risk.

DY0–DY5: `surface identity → shipped-state integrity → search-semantics integrity → freshness/expiry integrity → cross-surface continuity → qualified-value decision`.

Preserve: `editable anytime ≠ safe to change casually`; `promotional text ≠ keyword field`; `message freshness ≠ product freshness`; `upcoming claim ≠ shipped capability`; and `Store conversion change ≠ qualified growth`. Time-sensitive copy requires effective date, expiry/revalidation trigger and replacement/default state. CPP promotional text remains under the separate DP lifecycle.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store conversion, install persistence, generic retention, peer percentile, experiment count, impressions, CTR or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value through a reliable technical experience. Apple and Google Play benchmarks provide contextual diagnosis, not competitive truth or targets. Retention-like and technical-quality metrics must retain exact event, cohort and denominator semantics. Attribution improves evidence but does not convert platform credit into causal truth; sparse, aggregated, private or unavailable observations remain unknown rather than zero.
