## Latest validated addition — 160
Store creative is not confined to the Store listing. Google Play states that listing assets may be used on Google Play and other Google promotional channels; Play Console's `External marketing` control can restrict promotion across Google-owned properties. Google also distinguishes mandatory asset requirements from recommendations that can affect recommendation/promotion eligibility. Preview videos shown on Play must have ads disabled, and current guidance recommends localized/current assets and alt text for graphics.

Apple provides native marketing assets through App Store marketing tools and the App Store Connect app for launches, new versions and select featuring moments, intended for social/marketing sharing. Official Store badges and artwork remain governed assets and must not be improvised into false endorsement, ranking or recognition claims.

BX0–BX5: `live distribution-state identity → release/claim truth → platform asset integrity → destination-context integrity → route/measurement integrity → downstream value/maintenance`. Platform-native creative lowers zero-cost production burden, but does not grant permission to post in communities and does not establish endorsement or incrementality.

For MintTap, Play `External marketing` is now a required live-audit field before diagnosing organic reach. Existing screenshots/feature graphic/short description/video should be treated as potentially cross-surface assets and checked against shipping product truth and BE financial-claim provenance before broader reuse. For LogMate, the Store asset registry should be created during launch preparation so pilot-community/owned-web creative derives from release-qualified canonical assets rather than drifting copies.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface or Store asset is not usable merely because the platform exposes it. Eligibility, truthful classification, release truth and destination-channel permission precede distribution. Native attribution improves observability but does not establish incrementality. Attribution must preserve the intended human source separately from the platform-observed source and route mechanics; redirects, browsers, suppression thresholds and unsupported reporting surfaces can change what is visible. Sparse or threshold-suppressed evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and a discovery/trust surface second. Platform approval, featuring, recommendations, native marketing assets, Google-owned promotion, AI copy, accessibility labels/tags, category membership, event engagement or tagged-link conversion are distribution/context signals—not proof of demand, domain correctness, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural states that do not materially degrade that value.
