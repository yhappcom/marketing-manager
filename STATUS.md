## Latest validated addition — 204
Apple Custom Product Pages are now treated as a continuity layer rather than merely alternate creative. Apple currently supports up to 70 CPPs per app; pages can have unique URLs, localized creative, assigned App Store keywords, and deep links. Assigned keywords can cause a CPP to appear instead of the default page for matching searches. Deep links are supported for iOS/iPadOS 18+, so fallback state remains part of the contract.

For sparse niche apps, capacity must not drive segmentation. Individual CPP analytics appear only after at least five first-time downloads. Missing page-level data remains unknown, not zero. Apple-reported aggregate conversion uplift is evidence about the platform feature, not a MintTap/LogMate forecast.

DP0–DP5: `page necessity → entry identity → search/creative promise integrity → destination continuity → sparse measurement/downstream guardrail → scale/refresh/retire`.

Preserve: `CPP available ≠ CPP needed`, `70-page capacity ≠ segmentation target`, `unique CPP URL ≠ attribution truth`, `keyword assigned ≠ rank guarantee`, `keyword match ≠ qualified-user match`, `CPP conversion lift ≠ downstream-value lift`, `deep link configured ≠ destination continuity proven`, and `no visible CPP analytics ≠ zero activity`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, nominations, installs, campaign/UTM-token count, CSL/CPP count, experiment count, event-card count, tag count, star averages, impressions, CTR or estimated ad revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value. Attribution improves evidence but does not convert platform credit into causal truth; sparse, aggregated or privacy-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero. Segmented Store pages improve continuity only when intent, promise, destination and maintenance remain aligned.
