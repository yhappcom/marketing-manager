## Latest validated addition — 220
Non-intrusive monetization is not only a placement/frequency property: delivered ad content can damage specialist trust even when geometry is clean. AdMob provides app/account blocking controls and an Ad Review Center, but general-category classification is automated and not guaranteed to exclude every related ad. Mediation bidder filtering is an explicit quality-versus-demand tradeoff: stronger filtering can reject more bids and reduce revenue, while relaxing it can increase eligible demand but may admit ads below some content/editorial/technical thresholds. Therefore neither broad blocking nor relaxed filtering is a default revenue strategy.

EF0–EF5: `inventory/control identity → harm/relevance identity → narrowest-effective control → revenue-opportunity integrity → specialist-trust guardrail → sustainable decision`.

Preserve: `policy compliance ≠ product-context appropriateness`; `category block ≠ guaranteed semantic exclusion`; `sensitive ≠ restricted`; `Ad Review Center evidence ≠ complete historical inventory`; `more eligible demand ≠ better sustainable monetization`; `less blocking ≠ automatically better revenue quality`; `more blocking ≠ automatically better trust`; and `filtering state ≠ privacy/consent state`.

## Progress interpretation

Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value through a reliable experience, with monetization that does not corrupt that value. Advertising decisions retain exact format, placement, geometry, lifecycle, traffic-quality, delivered-content/control state and revenue semantics. The product does not manufacture friction, accidental interaction, inappropriate creative exposure or sacrifice specialist readability merely to create additional ad inventory.
