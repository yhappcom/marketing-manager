## Latest validated addition — 120
Canonical principle: **A peer benchmark is a diagnostic prior, not a company objective.** Apple peer benchmarks are aggregated by factors including category, business model and download-volume tier and expose directional percentile context for metrics such as conversion, retention and crash rate. Apple explicitly says benchmarks are directional rather than exact rankings and protects data with aggregation/differential privacy. Google Play likewise provides aggregated peer comparisons across Store conversion, growth/quality and other Console surfaces; curated groups use at least 100 apps and expose medians/percentiles rather than private individual metrics.

AL0–AL5 governs benchmark interpretation. AL0 is harmful percentile gaming. AL1 treats median/75th percentile as a universal KPI. AL2 records a correct descriptive comparison but has no specialist diagnosis. AL3 requires exact metric/denominator, peer basis, observation period, relevant segmentation, maturity/release context, directional-not-ranking interpretation, a specialist-job hypothesis, and precedence for first value/useful return over percentile chasing. AL4 adds an observed diagnostic/intervention loop without unsupported causality. AL5 is a reusable portfolio benchmark registry.

For MintTap, Finance-category or similar peer behavior is context, not a behavioral target for YieldMax investors. For LogMate, generic daily-retention norms must not create artificial engagement mechanics for pilots; actual duty/logging/import/backup/reporting cadence takes precedence.
