## Latest validated addition — 144
Canonical finding: **automatic Firebase/AdMob `ad_impression` collection, SDK lifecycle callbacks, and MintTap product-semantic placement identity are three different evidence layers. None may be inferred from another.** Firebase currently documents automatic `ad_impression` logging for linked AdMob + Analytics apps, so absence of a manual Flutter `logEvent('ad_impression')` is not evidence that impression measurement is absent. Conversely, existence of automatic `ad_impression` is not evidence that P1–P6, route/substate/action boundary, remount reason or BF eligible-state identity is preserved.

BH0–BH5 now requires production/release-qualified configuration, observed automatic impression delivery, canonical placement/context joinability, BigQuery/export quality and BG reconciliation before an ad placement becomes decision-grade. DebugView → Realtime → BigQuery/export is the validation ladder. Reporting latency must be allowed for before declaring a pipeline break. Avoid duplicate revenue-bearing `ad_impression` logging when AdMob automatic measurement already represents the impression.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers or installs. A consequential financial claim is not trustworthy merely because it comes from an official-looking page. A Store treatment is not successful merely because a platform reports a better conversion number. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions, eCPM or short-run revenue; success requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value. A code audit is not decision-grade unless it names the production/release ref; a route-level placement label is not a sufficient ad denominator when the user's task crosses into consequential action; and **automatic ad analytics is not placement-level decision evidence until it can be joined to the product-semantic eligible state without duplicate revenue accounting.**
