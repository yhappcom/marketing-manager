## Latest validated addition — 241
For AdMob-funded apps, the developer website is part of the monetization authorization chain, not merely a marketing surface. Google states that new apps set up in AdMob have required app-ads.txt verification since January 2025; authorization verification precedes app-readiness review, and apps cannot fully serve ads until both are complete.

AdMob derives the app-ads.txt hostname from the developer website published in the Google Play or Apple App Store listing and checks the authorization file according to root-domain/subdomain/redirect rules. Therefore Store metadata, developer-domain infrastructure and ad readiness form one operational contract. A working marketing page or an app-ads.txt file somewhere on the site is not sufficient evidence.

FA0–FA5: `monetization identity → Store-domain identity → authorization-file integrity → crawler integrity → readiness/latency integrity → sustainable-value decision`.

Preserve: `developer website exists ≠ crawler sees correct hostname`; `site loads ≠ app-ads.txt crawlable`; `file exists ≠ root/redirect contract correct`; `app-ads.txt verified ≠ app-readiness approved`; `readiness approved ≠ privacy/consent complete`; `ads can serve ≠ placement is acceptable`; `low revenue ≠ increase ad density`; `temporary unverified state ≠ configuration failure`.
