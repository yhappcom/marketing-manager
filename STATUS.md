## Latest validated addition — 161
Apple search keywords are localized App Store version metadata, not one global ASO field. Apple documents keywords as localizable and its App Store Connect API scopes search-keyword relationships to a specific App Store version localization. Apple also states that localized keywords can be used for search in countries/regions supporting that language, while the localization displayed to a user can depend on storefront language support, device language, developer-provided localizations and primary language.

This creates an important sparse-niche evidence boundary: territory, locale, displayed localization and keyword localization are not interchangeable. Localization should be market adaptation based on actual professional vocabulary, not literal translation. Apple explicitly recommends tailoring metadata/keywords to markets; promotional text does not affect search ranking and is not a substitute keyword field.

BY0–BY5: `live locale identity → audience-language evidence → functional/claim truth → metadata-field integrity → localization-route integrity → qualified outcome`. No deterministic keyword ranking weight or guaranteed lift is inferred from localization.

For MintTap, live Korean and English metadata must be audited as separate evidence surfaces before edits. Candidate distribution, ROC/reverse-split and portfolio/transaction intent clusters require locale-native vocabulary evidence rather than mechanical translation. For LogMate, Store localization must not imply localized in-app support while the product remains English-only.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not clicks, followers, karma or installs. A Store surface or Store asset is not usable merely because the platform exposes it. Eligibility, truthful classification, release truth, localization truth and destination-channel permission precede distribution. Localization is discovery infrastructure, not a translation checklist: professional vocabulary must be evidenced by locale, while territory, displayed localization and keyword localization remain separate dimensions. Native attribution improves observability but does not establish incrementality. Attribution must preserve the intended human source separately from the platform-observed source and route mechanics; redirects, browsers, suppression thresholds and unsupported reporting surfaces can change what is visible. Sparse or threshold-suppressed evidence remains inconclusive. Accessibility is a product capability first and a discovery/trust surface second. Platform approval, featuring, recommendations, native marketing assets, Google-owned promotion, AI copy, accessibility labels/tags, category membership, event engagement, localized metadata or tagged-link conversion are distribution/context signals—not proof of demand, domain correctness, regulatory authority or product-market fit. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the promised core value while advertising adds reconciled incremental revenue only from natural states that do not materially degrade that value.
