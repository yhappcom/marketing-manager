## Latest validated addition — 238
Google Play channel measurement is not an Android copy of Apple Campaign Links. Play Console separates Play-organic search/explore, installs without Store-listing visits, tracked UTM channels, Google Search, Google Ads and third-party referrers. Tracked UTM channels identify unique users visiting the Store listing in the Play Store app from UTM-tagged links; over-the-air and play.google.com web impressions are not tracked in the acquisition report, so the report is not a census of all external exposure.

Monthly acquisition exports support channel/country/organic-search/UTM dimensions and retained-installer metrics at 1/7/15/30 days. Daily Store-listing uniques cannot be summed to reconstruct weekly/monthly unique cohorts, and Play-organic total must not be added to its search/explore components. The app-side Play Install Referrer is a separate evidence object: it can expose referrer string, click/install timestamps and instant-experience state; Google states it remains available for 90 days and changes on reinstall, recommending a one-time first-run query after installation.

EX0–EX5: `decision identity → link/source identity → denominator/cohort integrity → referrer-lifecycle integrity → downstream-value integrity → qualified channel decision`.

Preserve: `UTM visit ≠ install`; `install ≠ retained specialist user`; `UTM/referrer association ≠ sole causal proof`; `Play Console channel ≠ app-side Install Referrer`; `daily uniques summed ≠ period uniques`; `organic total + search + explore ≠ valid additive total`; `missing route from acquisition report ≠ no exposure`; `referrer after reinstall ≠ immutable lifetime origin`.
