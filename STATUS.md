## Latest validated addition — 248
Google Play Promotional content is not the Android equivalent of an unrestricted Apple In-App Events channel. It is available to games and apps meeting Premium growth tools eligibility, requires genuinely new/noteworthy user-facing offers, time-limited events or major updates, and broader Play reach depends on content-quality approval.

Critical portfolio constraint: Google currently states Promotional content is not allowed for certain financial products/services and specifically identifies investing/trading, stocks, funds, shares, investment resources/management and financial utility services among restricted themes. MintTap therefore treats Google Play Promotional content as blocked by default unless current Play Console evidence and changed authoritative rules explicitly establish permission. This does not imply that MintTap itself is prohibited from Google Play.

Google's 2026 Apps Experience Program also exempts apps that do not produce material in-app content/events/promotions, with tools/utilities among its examples. LogMate therefore must not manufacture events merely to create Play discovery inventory; actual Play Console eligibility and a genuine new/noteworthy in-app experience come first.

FH0–FH5: `platform/app eligibility → category/restriction integrity → novelty integrity → in-app continuity → quality/reach integrity → qualified-value decision`.

Preserve: `Play app availability ≠ Promotional content eligibility`; `Apple In-App Events eligibility ≠ Google Promotional content eligibility`; `routine domain activity ≠ new/noteworthy promotion`; `submission accepted ≠ broad featuring`; `AEP Play Content guidance ≠ reason to manufacture events`.
