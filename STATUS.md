## Latest validated addition — 229
Apple In-App Events provide a zero-paid-media Store-native discovery/re-engagement surface for genuine time-bounded events, major updates, special content or experiences. They can reach new, active and lapsed users through product-page, search, editorial/personalized and event-detail surfaces, support App Store start notifications, and can route users through a tested deep link into the relevant in-app experience.

They are not a generic promotion loophole. Apple explicitly excludes repetitive routine activity, price-only promotion without new content/features/goods, and general app-awareness promotion as good candidates. Events can last up to 31 days and be promoted up to 14 days before start; up to 15 approved events can exist in App Store Connect and up to 10 can be published at once.

Event analytics expose impressions, event-page views, app opens, downloads, subscriptions and sales, but individual event data appears only after at least five first-time downloads. Below-threshold absence therefore remains unknown/suppressed rather than zero.

EO0–EO5: `event eligibility identity → audience-state identity → destination integrity → Store-distribution integrity → sparse-evidence integrity → qualified re-engagement decision`.

Preserve: `Store-native re-engagement ≠ reason to invent an event`; `event impression ≠ event-page engagement`; `notification opt-in ≠ return`; `download/open ≠ restored specialist value`; `missing event analytics below threshold ≠ zero`; and `Store distribution opportunity ≠ guaranteed reach/featuring`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store CTR, Store conversion, pre-orders/pre-registrations, event impressions, event-page views, notification opt-ins, generic retention, peer percentile, Reddit karma/upvotes, indexed-page count, ad impressions, ad CTR, dashboard eCPM, ARPV, consent rate or estimated impression revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires permitted, useful specialist contributions and truthful owned/Store destinations that lead qualified users to first or restored specialist value and repeat promised value through a reliable experience, with monetization that respects current privacy choice and does not corrupt that value. Community moderation/filtering state must be separated from demand. Store metrics are valid only under their exact event, denominator, cohort and privacy semantics. Prelaunch Store commitment is justified only after qualified demand and launch readiness exist; it is not a substitute for either. Store-native events are justified only by a real qualifying product moment and a matching destination; they are not a generic promotion surface. Search-intent routing improves relevance only when backed by real demand and truthful utility; custom-page capacity is not a growth objective. Attribution is not causality; sparse/aggregated/private/unavailable or threshold-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero.
