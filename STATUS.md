## Latest validated addition — 188
Rewarded advertising is valid only as a genuinely optional exchange for a clearly defined incremental benefit. Current AdMob policy requires rewarded ads to remain skippable/dismissible and normal use to remain available after declining; rewarded interstitials require an introductory screen with a functional `no`/`don't accept` choice. Promised rewards must be delivered. Google Play's opt-in rewarded-ad treatment does not create permission to degrade ordinary app use to manufacture opt-in demand.

CZ0–CZ5: `core-utility boundary → exchange clarity → reward-delivery integrity → verification/instrumentation integrity → voluntariness/substitution measurement → sustainable-value decision`.

Preserve: `rewarded ≠ mandatory`, `explicit opt-in ≠ coercive consent`, `high opt-in rate ≠ healthy monetization`, `ad completion ≠ reward delivery proof`, `client reward callback ≠ server-verified reward provenance`, `rewarded-policy exemption ≠ permission to degrade normal use`, and `incremental benefit ≠ artificially removed core utility`. AdMob SSV can add signed reward provenance and transaction identity when reward validity warrants it, but server-callback timing must not be confused with whether the user earned the reward.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not page count, rankings, clicks, followers, karma, submissions, pre-registrations, pre-orders, installs, impressions, CTR, rewarded-ad acceptance or estimated ad revenue alone. Store conversion, web traffic and ad yield are not terminal objectives. Platform Store/Search/ad objects retain native semantics even when normalized to common intent/value layers. Underpowered experiments do not become useful because a platform permits them. Pre-order/pre-registration creates a finite platform commitment/delivery window. Reddit promotion is governed per community; permission does not guarantee reachability, and owning a community does not create a right to mobilize its members into third-party communities. Owned-web publishing must serve distinct specialist demand rather than manufacture query coverage. Search Console visible queries are privacy-filtered/coverage-limited evidence rather than a complete demand census. Impression-level ad values preserve precision/provenance and remain distinct from finalized publisher earnings. Ad inventory must fit genuine product states: loading, transition and screen-space semantics cannot be manufactured merely to increase impressions. Native advertising must remain visibly and behaviorally separate from specialist product content. Rewarded advertising must remain a voluntary exchange for incremental value rather than a toll on baseline utility. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat the product value promised by acquisition surfaces while advertising contributes reconciled incremental revenue only from privacy-eligible, non-intrusive states within deliberate interruption, separation and voluntariness boundaries.
