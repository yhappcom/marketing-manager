## Latest validated addition — 246
Full-screen ad eligibility is a product/session-state decision, not merely an available-ad decision. Google positions app-open ads for loading/foreground waiting states and recommends avoiding the first app start; on cold start, if main content becomes usable before the ad is ready, do not show the ad afterward. Interstitials must not be used on app load/exit and repeated interstitial policy limits are ceilings, not optimization targets.

AdMob app/ad-unit frequency caps apply to interstitial, rewarded and app-open inventory, but they are only backstops: changes can take up to 24 hours and slight server delay can exceed the configured cap. Product-side semantic eligibility and protected-workflow rules remain necessary.

FF0–FF5: `session/value identity → format-semantic integrity → protected-moment integrity → frequency integrity → experience/economics integrity → change-control integrity`.

Preserve: `cap configured ≠ good UX`; `policy maximum ≠ recommended frequency`; `foreground event ≠ app-open eligibility`; `ad loaded ≠ ad should be shown`; `cold start ≠ delay main content for an ad`; `higher full-screen revenue ≠ sustainable monetization`.
