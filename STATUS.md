## Latest validated addition — 115
Canonical principle: **Apple's iOS/iPadOS 27 creative-asset expansion creates new organic Store inventory, not permission to add generic advertising art. Header/search-result assets are intent-specific product evidence and require lifecycle governance.**

Apple now documents creative assets that can appear across the App Store on iOS 27/iPadOS 27 and later, including product-page headers, search results and featuring. The new Asset Library centralizes images, videos, screenshots and app previews; assets can be submitted independently of an app submission and approved ahead of future product-page or Apple Ads use. Apple also introduces product-page preview. Search-result creative should make purpose obvious and show firsthand experience; header creative should communicate one clear idea for a first-time visitor. Apple permits a universal creative asset across header/search, but this is a production-efficiency option rather than evidence that the two surfaces share the same user job.

AG0–AG5 governs this new inventory. AG0 is misleading, stale, privacy-unsafe or unrelated creative. AG1 is decoration without a defined job. AG2 is production-related but incompletely governed. AG3 requires a defined specialist intent, one primary message, shipping-product claim parity, synthetic-data safety, locale parity, source/version and stale trigger, actual preview QA, and an explicit universal-vs-surface-specific decision. AG4 adds valid PPO/route evidence under J0–J5 and downstream useful-return observation. AG5 is a reusable multi-app creative lifecycle system.

For MintTap, search creative should make the YieldMax-tracking job obvious rather than depict generic wealth/high-yield outcomes. Header creative can establish category/promise while screenshots prove workflows. Reverse-split, ROC/tax-adjustment and distribution claims are allowed only when production-valid. For LogMate, pilot-logbook/flight-record purpose should outrank aviation lifestyle imagery; no airline affiliation, regulatory approval, certified-record or sync implication without production evidence. Both apps use synthetic Store data.
