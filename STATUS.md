## Latest validated addition — 222
Aggregate impressions, eCPM and revenue do not show how monetization burden is distributed. Current Google AdMob User Activity reporting distinguishes Active Users, Ad Viewers, ad viewer rate, Ads ARPU, Ads ARPV, Imps/AU, Imps/AV and Imps/Session. These denominators answer different questions: viewer participation is not ad density, and revenue per active user is not revenue per ad viewer. Ad viewer metrics can also be threshold-suppressed, so zero reported AV can coexist with non-zero impressions and must not be interpreted as zero real viewers.

EH0–EH5: `metric identity → viewer-participation integrity → exposure-density integrity → revenue-denominator integrity → sparse/reporting integrity → sustainable specialist-value decision`.

Preserve: `Ads ARPU ≠ Ads ARPV`; `ad viewer rate ≠ ad density`; `Imps/AU ≠ Imps/AV ≠ Imps/Session`; `more impressions ≠ broader monetization participation`; `higher ARPV ≠ better auction yield unless exposure density is controlled`; `zero reported AV ≠ zero real ad viewers when thresholding applies`; `average density ≠ exposure-tail safety`; and `revenue growth ≠ sustainable monetization growth`.

## Progress interpretation
Zero-cost success is not rankings, clicks, followers, subscribers, installs, Store conversion, generic retention, peer percentile, impressions, CTR, dashboard eCPM, ARPV or estimated impression revenue alone. Sustainable growth requires qualified users to reach and repeat promised specialist value through a reliable experience, with monetization that does not corrupt that value. Attribution is not causality; sparse/aggregated/private/unavailable or threshold-suppressed observations remain unknown rather than zero. Advertising decisions retain exact format, placement, geometry, lifecycle, traffic-quality, delivered-content/control state, auction/floor configuration, exposure distribution and revenue semantics. The product does not manufacture friction, accidental interaction, inappropriate creative exposure, concentrate excessive ad burden on frequent specialist users, or sacrifice specialist readability merely to create additional ad inventory or improve eCPM/ARPV.
