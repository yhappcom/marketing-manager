## Current operating model
Store discovery mechanisms remain distinct: `Apple CPP / Google CSL = deterministic intent routing | Apple IAE = truthful time-bounded discovery/reactivation | Google Promotional content = eligibility/category/event-truth gated | external tags = origin measurement | peer benchmark = relative diagnostic | PPO = causal creative experiment only when evidence budget exists`.

Organic/search content uses 082 N0–N5. Search demand alone does not create content eligibility. N3 requires materially distinct specialist utility; N4 adds trust-complete sourcing/authorship/scope/update discipline and is the target for finance, tax-adjacent, aviation and regulatory content; N5 additionally requires qualified downstream evidence.

083 adds D0–D5 distribution eligibility. The canonical N3/N4 article is the maintained evidence object; Reddit/social/community units are native adaptations, not duplicated promotional payloads. D3 requires standalone on-platform value, D4 adds transparent provenance + subordinate CTA + channel permission, and D5 requires qualified downstream behavior without moderation/spam/trust incidents. Search canonicalization handles representative URLs; it does not justify low-value page multiplication. Reddit reposting is a mechanism, not blanket permission.

084 separates owned publishing, owned community, and external-community participation. Owned communities use O0–O5: disclosed ownership is insufficient without governance; O3 requires independent member value; O4 requires a conflict-of-interest firewall; O5 requires sustainable member utility and qualified product discovery without trust degradation. Pinned/distinguished company reach is moderator-amplified reach and must not be reported as organic preference. Reddit's 10% self-promotion convention is community-specific guidance used by some communities, not a universal safe harbor.

085 adds C0–C5 for connected speakers. Employment, ownership, compensation, free/discounted benefits, family/personal ties or affiliate economics can create a material connection. When the connection is not unmistakable from the endorsement itself, C3 requires explicit local disclosure in the audience's language; C4 adds evidence-qualified claims and channel permission; C5 requires auditable separation of connected advocacy from independent member advocacy. Disclosure never cures an unsupported claim.

Advertising remains governed by 069–073: `value block → candidate boundary → exposure quality → interaction geometry → content integrity → format → downstream effect`. Organic/community acquisition recruits product use, never ad interaction. Reviews remain service-recovery/product signals before ASO score optimization.

## Progress interpretation
Do not report progress by file count. The highest-value question remains whether a target specialist can reach personal first value with acceptable effort, understand why to return, and encounter monetization only at legitimate boundaries. Organic content must be useful before SEO amplification, distribution must be useful in the receiving channel before it earns a link or CTA, an owned community must be useful even when a member never installs the app, and connected advocacy must never masquerade as independent specialist demand. Zero-cost marketing compounds by reusing maintained evidence and preserving trust, not by maximizing posting frequency, moderator-controlled reach, or undisclosed company advocacy.
