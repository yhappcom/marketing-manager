## Latest validated addition — 145
Canonical finding: **Reddit promotion is permission-scoped, not quota-entitled.** Reddit currently says promotional content is not inherently spam, while individual communities may prohibit it or use a 10% self-promotion convention. The remembered 10% convention never overrides a community's current rules. Sitewide spam policy focuses on repeated/unsolicited mass engagement, including repetitive mass posting, mass tagging and unsolicited messages.

A company/brand is expressly permitted to moderate a Reddit community under Moderator Code of Conduct Rule 5; the prohibited conduct is exchanging moderation actions for third-party compensation/favors. Rule 2 requires predictable community expectations and non-misleading official/unofficial affiliation. BI0–BI5 therefore separates brand identity, expectation contract, moderation decisions, contribution quality, distribution integrity and downstream growth evidence.

For r/MintTapforYieldMax, operate as a transparent product/community knowledge surface rather than a disguised independent YieldMax forum. Preserve compliant criticism, competitor references, bug reports and non-converting education. External investing communities require a fresh rule/permission check; no campaign obtains a standing right to promote because earlier posts were accepted.

## Progress interpretation
A zero-cost channel is not successful because it generates clicks, followers, karma or installs. A brand-owned community is not an independent forum merely because discussion is user-generated. A consequential financial claim is not trustworthy merely because it comes from an official-looking page. A Store treatment is not successful merely because a platform reports a better conversion number. Missing/censored evidence is not zero. An ad system is not successful because it maximizes impressions, eCPM or short-run revenue; success requires incremental reconciled revenue from natural eligible states without material degradation of core value. Promotion permission is contextual and revocable; useful participation must survive even when no product link is allowed.
