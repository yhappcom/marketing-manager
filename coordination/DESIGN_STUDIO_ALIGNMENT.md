# Marketing Manager ↔ Design Studio Alignment

Status: **ACTIVE OPERATING INTERFACE**  
Established: 2026-09-15

## Purpose

This document records the operating lessons Marketing Manager inherits from `yhappcom/design-studio` and defines the boundary between marketing judgment and design judgment.

The objective is not to copy Design Studio's domain structure or visual conclusions. It is to adopt its strongest professional operating principles where they improve marketing reliability, while preserving distinct canonical ownership.

## Design Studio lessons adopted

### 1. Research exists to improve real project decisions

Design Studio explicitly treats file count, reading volume, and academic completion as non-goals. Marketing Manager adopts the same standard.

Marketing research is useful only when it improves the ability to diagnose, choose, execute, measure, revise, and defend a real market decision.

### 2. Reading is not PASS

A source summary or confident explanation does not establish professional competence.

Marketing evidence should progress, where the topic allows, through:

`source understanding → model/application → real or representative data → critique/failure analysis → experiment/validation → transfer across contexts → advisory judgment`

The exact evidence type differs by topic. A positioning framework, an ad-platform policy, a causal experiment, and a pricing decision do not require identical validation methods.

### 3. Project-specific wins do not become universal rules

Design Studio keeps LogMate-specific design choices separate from reusable studio knowledge. Marketing Manager will do the same.

A campaign, message, screenshot, channel, audience, price, onboarding intervention, or creative that works for one app does **not** become a universal marketing rule.

Any attempted transfer must state boundary conditions such as:

- product/category;
- target audience;
- geography/language;
- channel and placement;
- time/competitive environment;
- creative/message context;
- price/offer;
- attribution/measurement method;
- sample size and uncertainty;
- lifecycle stage.

### 4. Cross-domain scan before substantial work

Before substantial marketing work that touches product experience, store creative, web, brand expression, landing pages, onboarding, visual persuasion, localization, accessibility, or information presentation, inspect relevant Design Studio and Web Manager evidence before inventing a new rule.

Use peer evidence in one of five ways:

- `REUSE` — apply established evidence within its scope;
- `REPLICATION` — independently test a high-impact claim;
- `CHALLENGE` — investigate conflicting evidence;
- `TRANSFER VALIDATION` — test whether a conclusion survives a new audience/channel/product/context;
- `DEPENDENCY` — request or await specialist evidence when another discipline owns the material question.

Overlap is acceptable when it adds analytical value. Unexamined duplication is not.

### 5. Failure→revision is valuable evidence

A failed campaign or test is not automatically wasted work. A useful record explains:

- hypothesis;
- mechanism expected;
- actual result;
- likely failure modes;
- competing explanations;
- revision;
- what the next test can distinguish.

Marketing must not preserve only winning examples.

### 6. Project mode outranks nonessential curriculum expansion

When a real app needs launch, positioning, store, acquisition, retention, pricing, or measurement support, apply the accumulated knowledge first and research only material gaps that could change the decision.

Do not give the project a lecture when it needs a recommendation.

### 7. Preserve uncertainty and disagreements

Conflicting marketing evidence must not be silently collapsed into one rule. Classify disagreements as factual, methodological, contextual, measurement-related, or judgment-based and identify what evidence could resolve them.

### 8. Transfer knowledge back to the originating discipline

When marketing execution produces useful evidence about typography, color, layout, interaction, web presentation, accessibility, or creative structure, return the finding to Design Studio as scoped transfer evidence rather than claiming design ownership.

A conversion lift can show that a market-facing variant performed better under tested conditions. It does **not** by itself prove universal design quality, readability, accessibility, or aesthetic superiority.

## Canonical ownership boundary

### Marketing Manager primarily owns

- market and category definition;
- customer research and segmentation;
- target selection;
- positioning and value proposition;
- message hierarchy and claims;
- launch and go-to-market strategy;
- channel strategy and media economics;
- ASO market/search/conversion strategy;
- campaign hypotheses;
- lifecycle/growth strategy;
- pricing/monetization marketing analysis;
- marketing analytics, attribution, incrementality and experimentation;
- performance interpretation;
- portfolio marketing and resource allocation.

### Design Studio primarily owns

- typography and type-system judgment;
- color/perception/color-system judgment;
- spatial hierarchy, layout and interaction judgment;
- web design and responsive page-system judgment;
- reusable visual/interaction validation methods;
- design-specific accessibility and device/rendering implications.

### Web Manager primarily owns

- company/product website information architecture and operations;
- web platform behavior and technical constraints;
- SEO implementation and technical discovery;
- website analytics/operations implementation;
- site performance, security, deployment, governance and maintenance.

### Product repositories primarily own

- actual product behavior;
- implemented features;
- pricing/entitlements as shipped;
- privacy and data behavior as implemented;
- release state and technical product truth.

Ownership is canonical responsibility, not a prohibition on learning adjacent material.

## Marketing → Design Studio handoff

When marketing needs a visual/interactive asset, provide a **marketing brief**, not pixel-level design instructions unless project constraints already fix them.

Useful inputs include:

- business/market objective;
- target audience and context;
- customer problem or job;
- positioning/value proposition;
- message hierarchy;
- verified claims and proof;
- primary desired action;
- channel/placement;
- mandatory copy/content;
- format/platform constraints;
- localization requirements;
- known audience/device/environment constraints;
- measurement hypothesis;
- what must remain invariant across variants;
- what is deliberately open for design exploration.

Design Studio should be free to determine typography, color, hierarchy, composition, interaction, and web design using its canonical evidence.

---

## Design Studio → Marketing handoff

Marketing should consume design output with its rationale, alternatives, scope, constraints, validation state, and unresolved risks.

Marketing then evaluates market-facing consequences without erasing the design evidence chain.

Examples:

- a store screenshot may satisfy a marketing message hypothesis but still fail readability or localization stress;
- a visually strong ad may generate clicks but low-quality acquisition;
- a high-CTR creative may damage expectation matching and downstream retention;
- a landing-page variant may lift conversion while weakening accessibility or brand coherence;
- a color or layout change may correlate with performance while the true cause is changed copy, offer, audience or placement.

Therefore creative performance must be interpreted jointly with the changed variables and downstream quality metrics.

## Project knowledge vs reusable knowledge

Project-specific marketing work should remain in the product repository or a future project/case-study area.

Promote a project result into reusable Marketing Manager knowledge only when the record states:

- what exactly transferred;
- what did not transfer;
- evidence strength;
- known boundary conditions;
- alternative explanations;
- additional validation needed.

This prevents one successful launch, one audience, one campaign, or one platform period from becoming folklore.

---

## Operating consequence

Marketing Manager will continue the Stage 1 foundational curriculum, but all future substantial work will use the Design Studio-derived discipline above:

**understand → retrieve internal evidence → research material gaps → frame → generate alternatives → recommend → execute/test where appropriate → measure → critique → revise → document → transfer**.

The final measure is not marketing activity volume. It is the quality and reliability of real product growth decisions.
