# Application Readiness Integration Gate

Validated: 2026-09-16
Decision: PASS — V1 may be frozen for live validation.

## Purpose

Test whether the current Marketing Manager operating system can route realistic MintTap and LogMate decisions across Store, evidence, community, owned content, selective social, advertising, and launch without inventing new methodology or fabricating live data.

## Gate criteria

PASS requires all of the following:

1. A candidate can be tied to the canonical growth chain and a current bottleneck.
2. Facts, policy, regulation, permission, and empirical performance are distinguishable evidence classes.
3. Missing live data can remain UNKNOWN without blocking low-risk reversible work unnecessarily.
4. The decision can be routed to ACT+MONITOR, OBSERVE/TRIANGULATE, CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT, RESEARCH FIRST, or DEFER.
5. Sparse traffic can be protected from low-VOI experimentation.
6. Store, content, community, social, and ad records can hand evidence to one another without silently changing metric definitions.
7. Maintenance, trust, constrained labor, and transfer/decay are represented.
8. A harmful or obsolete intervention has a rollback/recheck path.
9. The same operating model works for a live specialist-investor app and a pre-launch professional app without pretending their effect sizes transfer.

## Integration map

`problem/demand evidence`
→ `claim/proposition`
→ `distribution surface and permission`
→ `Store promise`
→ `qualified acquisition`
→ `activation`
→ `natural-cycle retention`
→ `eligible ad-bearing use`
→ `durable revenue`

Supporting audit chain:

`Evidence Registry observation IDs`
→ `Decision Record interpretation`
→ `Decision Queue route`
→ `asset/change event`
→ `native outcome evidence`
→ `adopt / keep / rollback / inconclusive / obsolete`

No layer may manufacture evidence for the next layer.

## Scenario tests

### A. MintTap: YieldMax ROC article → community → Store

Problem: specialist users may need accurate ROC tracking explanations.

Route:
- Community observation may establish audience language/relevance but not tax truth or prevalence.
- Material ROC/tax claims enter the Content Evidence Lifecycle and require authoritative validation.
- Publish owned content under ACT+MONITOR when factual verification and maintenance ownership are satisfied.
- External community distribution remains RESEARCH FIRST until current local permission/disclosure rules are verified.
- Social derivatives inherit source claim IDs and expiry triggers.
- A Store handoff can use a campaign/custom-product-page mechanism only where native measurement semantics support it; otherwise downstream attribution remains UNKNOWN.
- Store asset experimentation is separate from content truth and consumes scarce Store traffic only for a high-VOI contrast.

Result: ROUTABLE. No new method required.

### B. MintTap: new ad placement after a portfolio-analysis task

Problem: increase sustainable ad revenue without degrading precision use.

Route:
- Classify the user state before choosing format. A screen transition is not automatically a natural break.
- If the state is TASK_CRITICAL/TASK_ACTIVE, interruptive inventory is ineligible by default.
- If a genuine NATURAL_BREAK exists, create a Decision Record with quality-adjusted inventory mechanism, revenue metric, latency/abandonment/retention guardrails and rollback conditions.
- Compare durable retained-use economics rather than eCPM alone.
- Log implementation as a baseline-change event so pre/post ad observations are not silently pooled.

Result: ROUTABLE. No new method required.

### C. LogMate: launch proposition — fast manual entry vs import/migration

Problem: choose the first Store promise before meaningful Store traffic exists.

Route:
- Store conversion baseline is legitimately UNKNOWN pre-launch.
- Gather pilot/workflow evidence without converting a small convenience sample into prevalence.
- Use RESEARCH FIRST / OBSERVE-TRIANGULATE until evidence is sufficient for a reversible proposition choice.
- Launch with an explicit proposition and instrument activation before spending scarce launch traffic on multiple variants.
- Once traffic exists, use controlled Store experimentation only if expected resolution and MPME justify it.
- MintTap positioning uplift cannot be transferred as LogMate effect-size evidence.

Result: ROUTABLE. No new method required.

### D. LogMate: regulatory article distributed on professional social

Problem: build professional credibility and qualified discovery.

Route:
- Jurisdictional rule truth requires authoritative regulatory evidence.
- The content claim ledger preserves jurisdiction/version/date scope.
- Social operation requires a declared platform role and target-pilot audience-fit evidence; generic aviation reach is not sufficient.
- Social derivatives inherit correction/expiry triggers.
- Professional-community promotion permission is governed independently from social-account strategy.
- Likes/reach cannot be relabeled as installs; weak attribution follows S0–S5 evidence levels.

Result: ROUTABLE. No new method required.

### E. Company: MintTap Store experiment vs LogMate launch support competing for Design capacity

Problem: one constrained specialist block cannot fund both immediately.

Route:
- Treat each as a decision package rather than allocating time 50/50 by app.
- Compare current bottleneck leverage, VOI, reversibility, time-to-signal, maintenance tail, reusable learning and opportunity cost.
- Mandatory trust/compliance work overrides discretionary growth optimization where necessary.
- Do not create an uncalibrated universal weighted score.

Result: ROUTABLE. No new method required.

## Handoff audit

### Evidence → Decision
PASS. Decision Records cite observation IDs and retain native definitions/missingness.

### Community → Content
PASS. Audience/problem evidence transfers; factual truth does not. Permission remains local and expiring.

### Content → Social
PASS. Derivatives preserve claim provenance and decay triggers rather than becoming detached copies.

### Content/Social/Community → Store
PASS with limitation. Routing can be measured only to the native attribution boundary; unknown downstream contribution remains UNKNOWN.

### Store → Product
PASS conceptually; LIVE VALIDATION REQUIRED. Native Store outcomes do not prove activation or retention. Product telemetry must establish downstream quality.

### Product → Ads
PASS conceptually; LIVE VALIDATION REQUIRED. Ad eligibility is based on workflow state, and revenue optimization requires downstream UX/retention evidence.

### Outcomes → Portfolio allocation
PASS. New evidence can move bottlenecks and reprioritize constrained capability without rewriting historical records.

## Concrete operational gap found and fixed

The system previously had the necessary fields distributed across the Evidence Registry, Decision Record and Content/Social/Ad protocols, but lacked one explicit cross-surface handoff rule. V1 now locks the following:

**Every material distribution asset must identify both its upstream evidence/provenance object and its downstream decision/evidence destination.**

Minimum handoff tuple:

`source evidence/claim IDs → asset/change ID → surface + permission/eligibility state → native measurement ID/definition → linked Decision Record`

If any element is unavailable, record UNKNOWN/NOT_INSTRUMENTED rather than reconstructing attribution after the fact.

This is an integration rule, not a new marketing theory.

## Store-specific revalidation

Apple official documentation was rechecked on 2026-09-16. Product Page Optimization supports randomized tests of up to three treatments for icons/screenshots/previews and uses App Analytics to evaluate estimated conversion lift/confidence. Tests are not available for custom product pages. Apple Custom Product Pages are a separate routing surface: up to 70 pages per app, with distinct screenshots/previews/promotional text/keywords, shareable URLs, localization, search-keyword assignment, and page-level App Analytics metrics. Therefore PPO and CPP must not be represented as one experiment object in company records.

Operational consequence:
- PPO = randomized default-product-page experiment surface.
- CPP = audience/message routing and measurement surface; not PPO treatment inventory.
- A CPP-specific conversion observation does not by itself establish causal lift versus the default page because traffic selection may differ.

## Gate verdict

**PASS. APPLICATION READINESS V1 IS FROZEN.**

The current framework can represent and route the tested decisions. Remaining unknowns are live evidence gaps, not missing general methodology.

Do not add Stage 3 theory by default. New research is justified only when:

1. a live MintTap/LogMate decision cannot be represented or routed;
2. an authoritative platform/policy change invalidates an operating rule;
3. first-party evidence reveals a mechanism the current model does not explain;
4. a future product introduces a materially new business model/channel/regulatory context.

## Next operating mode

Shift from curriculum expansion to evidence-driven application:

1. ingest actual first-party/native baselines when available;
2. open Decision Records only for material decisions;
3. prioritize the current bottleneck rather than channel activity;
4. collect the minimum evidence needed to change a decision;
5. update reusable knowledge only from validated findings;
6. preserve INCONCLUSIVE and UNKNOWN rather than filling gaps with industry averages.
