# Stage 2 Gate Review — Sparse-Niche Decision Science

Last updated: 2026-09-16

## Gate question

Can yhappcom convert scarce specialist traffic, incomplete evidence and limited cross-functional labor into disciplined marketing decisions without false precision, invalid stopping, false transfer, resource churn, or optimization of a non-constraint?

## Verdict

**PASS — Stage 2 theory is sufficient for application/live-validation readiness.**

No material adjacent theory gap was found that justifies continuing to manufacture Stage 2 research topics before real MintTap/LogMate evidence exists.

The integrated system answers five required questions:

1. **Does this decision deserve more evidence?** — 021 VOI/reversibility routes it to ACT+MONITOR, OBSERVE/TRIANGULATE, CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT, RESEARCH FIRST, or DEFER.
2. **How should scarce evidence be interpreted?** — 020 separates validity, effect size, uncertainty and practical significance; MPME prevents trivial wins; INVALID and INCONCLUSIVE remain distinct.
3. **When may evidence collection stop?** — 022 separates health monitoring, fixed-horizon decisions, genuine sequential inference and operational staged commitment; explicit stop states prevent opportunistic peeking or endless extension.
4. **What may be reused elsewhere?** — 023 uses mechanism-first T0–T4 transfer and trigger-based evidence decay. Policy, regulation, factual truth and community permission are never transferred by analogy.
5. **Where does the next constrained labor block go?** — 024 allocates capacity to decision packages at the current bottleneck, considering durable value, evidence/VOI, time-to-signal, downside/reversibility, constrained capability, maintenance tail, reuse value and expiry risk.

## Integration tests

### Case A — MintTap Store positioning
Question: generic portfolio tracker proof vs YieldMax-specific ROC/distribution proof.

- Bottleneck: credible promise / qualified Store conversion, if live evidence confirms this is the constraint.
- Route: controlled experiment only if expected traffic can resolve a predeclared MPME within a useful horizon; otherwise observe/triangulate.
- Sparse design: control + one high-contrast treatment.
- Stop: platform-supported decision state or predeclared horizon; health checks remain separate.
- Transfer: result magnitude is not automatically reusable for LogMate, another locale or another platform.
- Labor allocation: gets scarce Store/Design capacity only if this bottleneck outranks activation, retention, compliance or other higher-leverage packages.

**PASS.** The system produces a bounded decision without inventing traffic or effect size.

### Case B — LogMate proposition before meaningful launch traffic
Question: rapid flight entry vs migration/import as primary promise.

- Direct live evidence: currently absent in canonical status.
- Route: research first / observe-triangulate using pilot evidence; do not consume nonexistent Store traffic in a pseudo-experiment.
- Wrong-decision cost: material because acquisition promise shapes activation expectations.
- Transfer: MintTap niche-app evidence is a hypothesis prior at most, not proof.
- Labor allocation: pilot/problem evidence can outrank social-account expansion because it resolves a more central uncertainty.

**PASS.** The framework refuses false experimental precision when the required traffic does not exist.

### Case C — Additional interstitial advertising
Question: add an ad opportunity to increase revenue.

- Target is not impressions alone; it is sustainable ad-bearing use.
- Active precision-task interruption is presumptively reject/defer.
- A genuinely natural transition may use operational staged exposure with retention/task-completion/latency/accidental-click/policy guardrails.
- HARM may stop rollout before the revenue hypothesis is statistically resolved.
- Placement evidence transfers poorly across task states/apps.
- Engineering/Design time competes with improvements to the actual bottleneck.

**PASS.** Monetization cannot override trust, usability or the growth constraint.

### Case D — Reddit/community promotion
Question: publish product-linked material in an external specialist community.

- Permission/rules are authoritative prerequisites, not conversion variables.
- Unknown or stale permission routes to RESEARCH FIRST.
- Moderator removal/warning is a stop/ledger-update trigger, not an invitation to reword and repost.
- Permission is community-specific and decays when rules/context change.
- Community goodwill is treated as scarce capacity.

**PASS.** The system protects trust while preserving community learning value.

### Case E — Competing MintTap and LogMate work
Question: which app receives the next Marketing/Design/Web/Engineering block?

- Mandatory compliance/trust work is separated from discretionary growth.
- Discretionary work is compared as decision packages, not equal app shares.
- Current bottleneck leverage dominates raw task count.
- Maintenance ownership/tail is included before creating another channel or asset.
- Reprioritization occurs on evidence, bottleneck, dependency, harm, capacity or expiry triggers—not every incoming idea.

**PASS.** The system supports portfolio choice without an invented universal score.

## Platform reality check

The framework remains compatible with current first-party Store experimentation constraints. Apple Product Page Optimization currently uses Bayesian analysis, reports Better/Worse at at least 90% confidence, can mark a test Likely to be Inconclusive, and tests run up to 90 days; adding more treatments can lengthen time to conclusion. This reinforces—not replaces—yhappcom's sparse-traffic rule: reserve Store traffic for high-value, high-contrast questions and accept inconclusive outcomes rather than forcing winners.

Platform-native statistical outputs must remain native evidence. Do not reinterpret Apple's confidence machinery as a generic sequential guarantee for unrelated analytics or other platforms.

## Gate failures deliberately avoided

- File-count completion as a proxy for capability.
- Universal numerical priority weights without calibrated company data.
- A/B testing factual, regulatory, policy or permission truth.
- Treating p>threshold/non-winning status as proof of equivalence.
- Repeated peeking under a fixed-horizon contract.
- Transporting effect sizes across app, platform, locale or professional context without mechanism review.
- Maximizing acquisition while activation/retention is the constraint.
- Maximizing impressions/eCPM while degrading retained use.
- Creating zero-cash channels without accounting for maintenance labor.

## Remaining gaps are validation gaps, not theory gaps

Stage 2 cannot legitimately manufacture the following values: MintTap/LogMate traffic ceilings, activation definitions, natural retention cadence, channel quality, community permissions, empirical MPMEs, harm thresholds, ad long-run value, staff capacity, maintenance demand, or cross-context transfer records. They require live product/market evidence.

## Stage 2 exit rule

Stage 2 is closed for theory expansion unless a live decision exposes a specific failure the current framework cannot route or interpret. New adjacent methodology should not be added merely because it exists.

Next phase: **Application & Live-Validation Readiness** — populate auditable decision/portfolio records from real evidence, establish baseline measurement and evidence provenance, and use the framework to choose the next highest-value learning/action package for each app.
