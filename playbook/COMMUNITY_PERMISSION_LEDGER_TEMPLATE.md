## Action IDs

- P0 read/listen only
- P1 native helpful comment, no product mention
- P2 native helpful post, no product mention
- P3 transparent product/developer mention, no link
- P4 contextual external link after native answer
- P5 explicit app announcement/showcase
- P6 research/usability participant recruitment
- P7 direct-message outreach
- P8 recurring/automated monitoring or outreach

## Gate

No P3–P8 execution from `NOT_STATED`.

For P3–P6, resolve to explicit/conditional permission or moderator approval before material execution. P7/P8 require especially strong explicit permission and must also comply with platform-wide rules; unsolicited or automated outreach is not permitted by company policy.

## Evidence hygiene

Observed competitor/developer posts are precedent only. They do not establish permission. Preserve current written rule/mod evidence and date. A removal or moderator warning immediately triggers re-check and ledger update.
