# Evidence Provenance & Baseline Measurement Protocol

Validated: 2026-09-16
Phase: Application & Live-Validation Readiness

## Purpose

Create a minimum trustworthy evidence layer for MintTap, LogMate, and future niche apps before ranking live marketing decisions. The objective is not maximal tracking. It is to preserve metric meaning, distinguish observed facts from derived estimates, expose missingness, and prevent cross-platform numbers from being silently treated as equivalent.

## Core rule

Never overwrite a platform-native metric definition with an internal convenience definition. Store the native metric as observed, with source/version/context, then derive company metrics separately.

`observed native metric -> provenance record -> optional derived metric -> decision record`

UNKNOWN, SUPPRESSED, NOT_APPLICABLE, NOT_INSTRUMENTED, and ZERO are different states.

## Evidence classes

### A. First-party platform observation
Examples: App Store Connect first-time downloads; unique impressions; Google Play store-listing visitors/clicks/CTR. Preserve the platform's exact definition and reporting scope.

### B. First-party product telemetry
Events generated by the app under an explicit event dictionary. Required for product-specific activation, workflow completion, retained use, and ad-experience effects. Do not call a store download an activated user.

### C. Owned-channel observation
Search Console/blog analytics, first-party website analytics, campaign-link observations, newsletter data where applicable. Preserve URL/campaign/date definitions.

### D. Community/social observation
Post/comment URL or platform identifier, community, permission state, date, content type, disclosure, impressions/clicks if genuinely available, qualitative replies, moderation outcome. Upvotes/likes are not installs or retained users.

### E. Research/qualitative evidence
Interview, observation, support report, moderator response, authoritative policy, competitor observation. Record source and context; do not manufacture quantitative strength.

### F. Derived metric/model
Any ratio, blended funnel, estimated cohort value, attribution model, or cross-source calculation. Must list inputs and formula and inherit limitations from every input.

## Minimum provenance record

Every decision-relevant datum should carry:

- `evidence_id`
- `captured_at`
- `observation_window_start/end`
- `product`
- `platform` (iOS/Android/Web/Community/etc.)
- `market/territory`
- `source_system`
- `source_report_or_surface`
- `metric_name_native`
- `metric_definition_native`
- `value`
- `unit`
- `aggregation` (daily/weekly/cohort/etc.)
- `dimensions/filters`
- `population/eligibility`
- `privacy_or_threshold_limitation`
- `collection_method` (UI/export/API/manual verified capture)
- `derived` boolean
- if derived: `formula` and `input_evidence_ids`
- `transfer_class` T0-T4 when reused outside original context
- `decay_trigger`
- `status` = OBSERVED / UNKNOWN / SUPPRESSED / NOT_INSTRUMENTED / NOT_APPLICABLE / INVALID
- `notes`

## Native-store guardrails

### Apple App Store Connect
Current Apple definitions must be retained literally at the semantic level:

- Impressions: app viewed on Today/Games/Apps/Search for >1 second; includes product-page views.
- Product Page Views: product-page views, including StoreKit-loaded product pages.
- First-Time Downloads: first-time downloads on eligible Apple platforms.
- Total Downloads = first-time downloads + redownloads.
- Apple's Conversion Rate = total downloads and pre-orders / unique-device impressions. This is NOT a generic `product-page-view -> install` conversion rate.
- App Store usage data is based on users who opted to share analytics; privacy/data thresholds can suppress data.
- Source Type, App Referrer, Web Referrer, Campaign, territory and other dimensions have platform definitions and thresholds. Campaigns appear only after >1 day and at least five attributed App Units under current documentation.

Therefore internal reporting must label Apple's metric `ASC conversion rate` rather than silently renaming it `store-page CVR`.

### Google Play Console
Google changed store-listing performance reporting in 2026. Current reporting emphasizes store-listing intent/clicks rather than treating successful acquisitions as the primary listing-performance measure. The current Conversion analysis surface reports visitors, clicks and CTR, filterable by traffic source, listing, country, language, UTM source/campaign and acquisition state.

Store-listing reports cover defined listing surfaces and exclude some other Play surfaces/mechanisms. Consequently:

- do not compare a historical Google acquisition-rate series to the post-2026 click/CTR series as though definitions were unchanged;
- mark a metric-definition break in longitudinal baselines;
- do not label Google Play CTR as equivalent to Apple's ASC conversion rate;
- preserve traffic-source definitions because Google Play Search, Explore, and Ads/Referrals have explicit inclusion/exclusion rules.

## Cross-platform rule

Apple and Google funnels are not forced into one synthetic 'store conversion' KPI. Maintain two layers:

1. **Native layer:** exact Apple/Google metrics for diagnosis within each store.
2. **Company conceptual layer:** discovery -> listing/product-page engagement -> install intent/download -> activation -> retained value, with explicit mapping and missing cells.

A cross-platform comparison is allowed only when numerator, denominator, population, window, and event meaning are sufficiently comparable. Otherwise report side-by-side native metrics.

## Baseline windows

Do not impose a universal 7/28/30-day window on niche apps. For each product establish:

- a recent operational window;
- enough calendar/workflow coverage to represent the product's natural cycle when known;
- version/release boundaries;
- material campaign or store-listing change boundaries.

If traffic is too sparse, widen the observation window but record that the baseline spans more environmental change. Never convert sparse evidence into false precision.

## Baseline hierarchy

Before optimization, seek the smallest set that identifies the current bottleneck:

1. **Store discovery:** native impressions/visitors and source mix where available.
2. **Store engagement/intent:** native page-view/click/CTR/conversion metrics as defined by each platform.
3. **Acquisition:** first-time downloads/new-user acquisition under native definitions.
4. **Activation:** one product-specific meaningful-value event, not merely app open.
5. **Retention:** return to the core job at a cadence justified by the product workflow.
6. **Monetization:** ad-bearing active use, impressions per eligible active user/session, revenue, and UX/harm guardrails only when instrumented.
7. **Owned/community contribution:** qualified traffic or evidence attributable to a specific asset/activity when genuinely observable.

A missing downstream baseline should remain UNKNOWN; it must not be inferred from upstream success.

## Attribution discipline

Attribution answers only what the measurement system can support.

- Native App Store source/referrer/campaign data remains native evidence.
- Google Play UTM/source dimensions remain Google evidence.
- A Reddit post followed by higher downloads is temporal association unless a defensible attribution mechanism exists.
- Search Console impressions/clicks demonstrate search visibility/traffic, not app installs unless the downstream link is measured compatibly.
- Never allocate 'organic' downloads among blog, Reddit, word of mouth, social, and Store search by guesswork.
- When attribution is unavailable, use `UNATTRIBUTED/UNKNOWN`, then triangulate timing, source reports, campaign links, qualitative evidence, and downstream cohorts without upgrading correlation to causation.

## Baseline-change ledger

Any of these can invalidate comparability and must be logged:

- store metadata/screenshot/icon change;
- app version/onboarding/activation-definition change;
- analytics event-definition change;
- platform reporting-definition change;
- campaign-link/UTM convention change;
- market/territory expansion;
- major channel mix shift;
- ad placement/frequency/SDK change;
- consent/privacy implementation change;
- community rule/permission change.

## Decision readiness states

For each decision package:

- `READY`: minimum evidence exists and definitions are trustworthy.
- `READY_WITH_UNCERTAINTY`: enough to act/monitor but important cells remain unknown.
- `BASELINE_FIRST`: missing evidence could reverse the decision and is feasible to obtain.
- `RESEARCH_FIRST`: factual/policy/permission question precedes performance measurement.
- `NOT_MEASURABLE_YET`: no defensible observation path currently exists.

Do not delay reversible low-risk actions merely to complete a dashboard. Baseline collection is subject to the Stage 2 VOI framework.

## MintTap minimum baseline pack

Do not invent unavailable values. Populate when evidence becomes accessible:

- iOS/Android native Store discovery, source mix, listing engagement/intent and first-time/new acquisition;
- current store metadata/version/change dates;
- explicit activation event tied to YieldMax portfolio value (definition to be validated from product telemetry/workflow, not guessed here);
- retention cadence justified by actual investor workflow/distribution usage;
- blog/Search Console baseline by high-intent problem cluster (ROC, distributions, reverse splits, cost basis where content exists);
- YieldMax community permission ledger and post-level evidence;
- ad placement/state/frequency/latency and revenue metrics with task-abandonment/retention guardrails;
- U.S./Korean and iOS/Android context tags rather than pooled assumptions.

## LogMate minimum baseline pack

Pre-launch UNKNOWN is valid. Required readiness evidence includes:

- launch market/platform and target pilot segment;
- store-listing baseline only after a real listing generates data;
- proposition evidence for core job(s), including recording workflow versus migration/import where applicable;
- product-specific activation and natural recurrence cadence once observable;
- pilot interview/observation provenance;
- professional-pilot community permission ledger;
- ad eligibility/placement policy and workflow-interruption guardrails before monetization experiments;
- jurisdiction, airline/workflow, platform and device context on all transferred evidence.

## Operational cadence

At each meaningful product/store/channel change:

1. append the change ledger;
2. preserve pre-change baseline rather than overwriting it;
3. capture native metrics with their definitions;
4. update missingness states;
5. map evidence IDs into the relevant Decision Record;
6. apply evidence-decay/transfer rules;
7. collect only additional evidence whose VOI justifies the labor/traffic cost.

## Unresolved live questions

These are evidence gaps, not invitations to fabricate generic answers:

- MintTap's actual analytics event dictionary and current Store/Search Console/ad baselines.
- LogMate launch state, geography, live listing status and eventual telemetry dictionary.
- Exact company campaign-link/UTM naming convention.
- Whether privacy/consent implementation changes materially alter observable product cohorts.
- Which native exports/APIs will become the canonical ingestion path versus manually verified captures.
