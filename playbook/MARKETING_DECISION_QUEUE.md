# Marketing Decision Queue

Last updated: 2026-09-15

## Purpose

Convert Stage 2 research 020–023 into one auditable operating record. This is not a TODO list. Every material marketing idea must be routed according to evidence value, practical effect, reversibility, stopping logic, and transferability before scarce niche traffic or specialist labor is consumed.

## Canonical record

For each candidate decision record:

1. **Decision** — the actual choice to be made, not an activity label.
2. **Target outcome** — qualified acquisition, activation, retention, sustainable ad-bearing use, or a necessary trust/compliance prerequisite.
3. **Current bottleneck** — which link in `relevant demand → credible promise → qualified acquisition → meaningful activation → repeated core value → sustainable ad-bearing use` currently constrains value.
4. **Mechanism hypothesis** — why the intervention should affect that bottleneck.
5. **Evidence** — direct, observational, authoritative, transferred, or absent.
6. **MPME** — smallest practically meaningful effect when an empirical experiment is appropriate.
7. **Decision cost / wrong-decision cost** — labor, scarce traffic, UX/trust risk, maintenance burden, delay and opportunity cost.
8. **Reversibility** — easy, moderate, difficult/irreversible.
9. **VOI** — could more information plausibly change the decision enough to justify waiting/research?
10. **Route** — `ACT + MONITOR`, `OBSERVE / TRIANGULATE`, `CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT`, `RESEARCH FIRST`, or `DEFER`.
11. **Contract** — fixed horizon, formal sequential if genuinely supported, operational staged commitment, or non-experimental monitoring.
12. **Stop/rollback conditions** — SUCCESS, HARM, INVALID, FUTILITY/LOW-VOI, MAX-HORIZON INCONCLUSIVE, EXTERNAL/OBSOLETE.
13. **Transfer status** — T0–T4 from research 023 plus target context and decay triggers.
14. **Owner/dependency** — Marketing, Web, Design, Engineering, or cross-functional.
15. **Next action** — one concrete next step.

## Representative routing without fabricated performance

These are decision archetypes, not claims about live MintTap or LogMate performance.

### MintTap — authoritative ROC/reverse-split educational article
- Outcome: qualified discovery + trust.
- Mechanism: answer a specialist problem with durable owned content and provide a truthful path to the product.
- Route: **ACT + MONITOR** when factual claims are verified; do not wait for an A/B test to decide whether useful authoritative content may exist.
- Evidence: problem relevance can be triangulated from real questions/search evidence; tax/ROC facts require authoritative verification.
- Reversibility: high.
- Transfer: the content-production principle is T0/T1; exact search demand, wording and conversion magnitude are T2/T3.
- Monitor: qualified search impressions/clicks, useful engagement, Store handoff, maintenance triggers; do not optimize to raw pageviews alone.

### MintTap — first Store screenshot: generic tracker vs YieldMax-specific proof
- Outcome: Store conversion from relevant prospects.
- Mechanism: stronger problem/solution recognition before install.
- Route: **CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT** only if expected traffic can resolve a predeclared MPME within a useful horizon; otherwise OBSERVE/TRIANGULATE and preserve the question as INCONCLUSIVE.
- Reversibility: high, but Store traffic is scarce.
- Preferred design: control + one high-contrast treatment rather than several cosmetic variants.
- Transfer: any empirical uplift is T2 for LogMate and normally T2/T3 across materially different locales/platforms.

### MintTap — posting in an external YieldMax community
- Outcome: qualified discovery + problem evidence.
- Route: **RESEARCH FIRST** until current local rules/permission and disclosure requirements are known. Conversion testing cannot substitute for permission.
- Stop: moderator warning/removal or rule conflict triggers immediate stop and ledger update.
- Transfer: permission is community-specific and time-sensitive; never transfer by analogy.

### LogMate — Store proposition: rapid entry vs migration/import
- Outcome: qualified install and activation.
- Mechanism: align first promise with the pilot's highest-value entry job.
- Route: **RESEARCH FIRST / OBSERVE-TRIANGULATE** before launch if actual pilot evidence is absent; later CONTROLLED EXPERIMENT if traffic can resolve a meaningful contrast.
- Wrong-decision cost: potentially high because it shapes acquisition quality and downstream onboarding expectations.
- Transfer: MintTap positioning evidence is T2 at most; professional niche similarity does not establish mechanism equivalence.

### LogMate — jurisdiction-specific logbook/regulatory content
- Outcome: trust + qualified discovery.
- Route: **RESEARCH FIRST** for factual/regulatory truth, then ACT + MONITOR if useful. Never A/B-test which regulatory claim to present as true.
- Transfer: jurisdictional requirements are T3/T4 across jurisdictions unless directly verified.

### Company — additional interstitial placement in a precision workflow
- Outcome: sustainable ad revenue.
- Mechanism: more eligible impressions may increase revenue.
- Route: generally **DEFER/REJECT** when it interrupts an active precision task; if a genuinely natural transition exists, use staged exposure plus downstream guardrails before any revenue conclusion.
- MPME: must include net retained-user/ad-value improvement, not only eCPM or impression gain.
- HARM stop: task abandonment, accidental-click signal, material latency, complaints, policy risk, or retention degradation.
- Transfer: placement success is context/task-state dependent; empirical magnitude is T2/T3 across apps.

## Queue discipline

- Scarce Store/community traffic is a portfolio resource, not free inventory.
- Do not run a low-value cosmetic experiment while a material positioning uncertainty is waiting for the same traffic.
- `INCONCLUSIVE` is a valid terminal state.
- A policy, factual, regulatory or permission question routes to authoritative research, not conversion experimentation.
- A reversible low-cost decision with low downside can be executed and monitored when further research has low VOI.
- No empirical result becomes a timeless company rule; attach target context and decay triggers.
- Queue priority must be recomputed when the system bottleneck moves.
