## Gate

A material localized claim may ship only when:

`capability verified ∧ semantic scope preserved ∧ jurisdiction checked ∧ proof aligned`

For S3/S5, domain review is mandatory before treating the localization as canonical marketing copy.

## Metrics

Record by market/package where observable:

- qualified Store impressions;
- product-page conversion;
- first-value completion;
- useful return;
- terminology-related support/community corrections;
- localization fallback/coverage gaps.

Do not split sparse traffic into locale-level campaigns unless the resulting data can change a real decision.
