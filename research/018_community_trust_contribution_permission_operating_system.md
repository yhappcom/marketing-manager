# 018 — Community Trust / Contribution / Permission Operating System

Date: 2026-09-15
Status: FOUNDATION / OPERATING SYSTEM

## Purpose

Research 007 established anti-spam principles. This block turns those principles into an operating system for Reddit and specialist communities, where access is governed by local norms and moderator decisions rather than by yhappcom's marketing needs.

Community marketing is not free distribution inventory. It is participation inside another group's governed social environment.

## 1. Core model

`audience relevance → local permission → useful native contribution → disclosed product relationship when relevant → community response/learning → optional product handoff → product/content/store improvement`

Permission is a hard gate. Contribution quality does not override a prohibition on promotion.

## 2. Three layers of governance

Before any community action, distinguish:

1. **Platform policy** — site-wide rules such as spam restrictions.
2. **Community rules** — subreddit/forum-specific rules and post guidance.
3. **Moderator interpretation/permission** — local enforcement and clarification when rules are ambiguous.

The most restrictive applicable layer governs the action.

Current Reddit Help explicitly states that community moderators determine what counts as unwanted/spam content in their communities and advises users to check community-specific rules and contact moderators when uncertain. Reddit also warns against contributions that mainly link to a business the contributor runs/owns/benefits from, repeated irrelevant/link-farmed posting, repeated similar comments, and large-scale unsolicited messages.

Operational consequence: there is no company-wide numerical self-promotion ratio that can substitute for current local rules.

## 3. Permission states

Every target community must have one state before product-related participation:

- `ALLOWED_EXPLICIT` — current rule/mod statement explicitly permits the intended form.
- `ALLOWED_CONDITIONAL` — permitted only under documented conditions (flair, megathread, frequency, disclosure, format, approval, etc.).
- `AMBIGUOUS` — rules do not resolve the intended action; clarification needed.
- `PROHIBITED` — intended promotional/product action is not permitted.
- `UNKNOWN/STALE` — not checked recently enough for an operational decision.

`AMBIGUOUS`, `PROHIBITED`, and `UNKNOWN/STALE` are not publishing permissions.

## 4. Permission ledger contract

For each community record:

- platform;
- community name/URL;
- audience hypothesis;
- intended participation job;
- platform-policy reference/date checked;
- community-rule reference/date checked;
- relevant exact rule summary;
- promotion/link/disclosure constraints;
- moderator clarification reference/date if any;
- permission state;
- expiry/recheck trigger;
- account/owner disclosure convention;
- last action/outcome;
- removal/warning/ban evidence;
- next allowed action.

Do not store private moderator identities or unnecessary personal data.

### Recheck triggers

Revalidate when:

- community rules or post guidance change;
- a moderator gives new guidance;
- a post is removed or warned;
- product relationship/monetization materially changes;
- the intended content format changes;
- the ledger is stale before a campaign.

A historical permission is evidence, not permanent entitlement.

## 5. Contribution jobs

Community work must have a job independent of promotion:

- answer a real specialist question;
- explain a difficult concept with sources;
- correct a material misconception carefully;
- share original analysis/data where permitted;
- ask for product/workflow research without disguising commercial interest;
- collect recurring language, friction and unmet needs;
- publish release/product information only where the community explicitly permits it.

A contribution that becomes useless when the product link is removed is high-risk promotional content.

## 6. Native-value test

Before posting, ask:

1. Does this directly address the thread/community's current problem?
2. Is the useful answer substantially present on-platform rather than withheld behind a click?
3. Are claims supported at the level required by the topic?
4. Would it still be worth reading without the MintTap/LogMate link?
5. Is the product relationship disclosed where it materially affects how readers should interpret the recommendation?
6. Is the exact format/link permitted locally?

Failing #6 blocks publication. Failing #1–5 requires revision.

## 7. Disclosure rule

When recommending or linking a product from which yhappcom benefits, do not present the recommendation as independent user advocacy. Use plain disclosure proportionate to context and local rules.

Disclosure is not a permission bypass. `I made this app` does not make prohibited promotion permissible.

## 8. Link discipline

Links are optional evidence/handoff, not the definition of community marketing.

Preferred sequence when permitted:

`native answer/value → source/reference if useful → product mention/link only when relevant and allowed`

Avoid:

- repetitive cross-community link drops;
- redirect tricks/domain masking;
- duplicate/similar promotional comments;
- unsolicited mass DMs/chats;
- manufacturing threads/questions to create a promotional reply opportunity;
- automation/bots used to scale product promotion.

Current Reddit Help identifies several of these patterns as spam or potential spam.

## 9. Trust accounting

Do not invent a universal `give 9, promote 1` rule. Track evidence instead.

Useful operating signals:

- removals/warnings;
- moderator clarification;
- substantive replies/questions;
- saves/upvotes only as weak contextual signals, not business outcomes;
- recurring problem language;
- qualified store/product handoffs where measurement is permitted;
- downstream activation/retention when evidence exists;
- support burden or misunderstanding generated by the post.

Community karma/reputation is not owned media and is not a guaranteed marketing asset.

## 10. Community learning loop

The strongest strategic value of a small specialist community may be learning rather than reach.

`question/thread → language/problem evidence → product/positioning/content/store hypothesis → change/test → return with better contribution when appropriate`

Examples:

### MintTap

Potential evidence categories:
- confusion around distributions/ROC;
- reverse-split recordkeeping;
- cost-basis/tracking friction;
- terminology investors actually use;
- questions existing tools fail to answer.

Do not infer investment advice demand from general YieldMax discussion. Financial claims require stronger sourcing and careful scope.

### LogMate

Potential evidence categories:
- migration/import friction;
- backup/data ownership concerns;
- workflow terminology;
- search/retrieval needs;
- airline/professional logbook practices.

Do not generalize one pilot community to all professional pilots, airlines, countries or regulatory regimes.

## 11. Owned vs borrowed community

External specialist communities are **borrowed access**: rules, ranking, moderation and account standing are controlled by others.

Owned community/support surfaces are different: yhappcom controls governance but must still prevent the space becoming a one-way announcement feed.

Use external communities for relevant participation/learning under permission. Use owned community for durable support, release context, feedback aggregation and peer discussion where genuine demand exists.

Do not create an owned community merely to claim a marketing channel; an empty community has maintenance cost and weak social proof.

## 12. Measurement hierarchy

Community activity should be evaluated in this order:

1. compliance/permission health;
2. contribution usefulness and quality of discussion;
3. learning captured into product/content/store decisions;
4. qualified handoff where permitted;
5. activation/retention of attributable cohorts when measurable;
6. sustainable ad-bearing usage/revenue only downstream.

Raw impressions, karma and subscriber growth are diagnostic context, not final success metrics.

## 13. Failure and rollback protocol

If a contribution is removed/warned:

1. stop repeating the format;
2. capture rule/mod evidence and timestamp;
3. update permission state;
4. determine whether failure was permission, relevance, disclosure, duplication, link, tone or factual-quality related;
5. do not repost a cosmetic variant to evade moderation;
6. only resume when a compliant path is clear.

A removal is operational evidence, not a challenge to outsmart the filter.

## 14. Minimum live-operation workflow

Before campaign/posting work:

`select community → verify audience → read current platform + local rules → ledger permission → choose contribution job → native-value review → disclosure/link review → publish only if permitted → record outcome → capture learning → update product/content/store backlog`

Retained first-party facts:

- local moderators determine unwanted/spam content and users should inspect local rules/contact moderators when uncertain;
- repeated similar/off-topic/link-farmed content and large unsolicited messaging can be treated as spam;
- Reddit provides community-level moderation/spam controls, reinforcing that local governance is operationally meaningful.

## 16. What is deliberately NOT canonical

- the old folklore `9:1 self-promotion rule` as a universal current Reddit requirement;
- a universal number of non-promotional posts required before product mention;
- assumed permission based on another subreddit/community;
- karma/upvotes as proof of acquisition quality;
- permanent permission inferred from one historical moderator response.
