# 028 — Selective Social Distribution for Specialist Audiences

Validated: 2026-09-16

## Purpose

Define when a social account is worth operating for a narrow professional/investor app, how owned specialist knowledge should be adapted without low-value duplication, and how to continue/stop a social surface when attribution is weak.

## Core conclusion

Social is not a mandatory channel layer. For a narrow app, a social surface earns ongoing labor only if it performs a distinct job that Store, search/owned content, and permission-governed communities do not perform as efficiently.

`platform presence ≠ distribution strategy`

The decision unit is:

`audience concentration × platform job × content-source fit × credible native adaptation × evidence value ÷ production + moderation + maintenance tail`

Do not create accounts merely to reserve broad reach or because a platform is large.

## 1. Platform-role gate

Before recurring publication, specify one primary role:

- DISCOVERY: reach relevant non-followers with specialist material.
- CREDIBILITY: make expertise, product reasoning, changelog/proof, or founder/operator identity inspectable.
- RETURN: give existing users/followers a reason to re-engage with useful updates.
- LISTENING: observe language/questions/signals that improve product/content hypotheses.
- ROUTING: move qualified users to a deeper owned/community/Store surface where that transition is native and measurable enough to justify it.

If the account has no distinct role, DEFER it.

A platform can perform more than one role, but vague multi-role claims are not evidence of value.

## 2. Specialist-audience fit precedes format optimization

Do not begin with generic advice such as post X times per week, use Reels, or publish carousels. First establish that the target audience or a strategically useful adjacent audience is present and reachable in the relevant context.

Evidence can include:

- first-party referral/source observations;
- naturalistic discussion/search evidence;
- platform-native audience/engagement evidence;
- credible professional/community usage evidence;
- repeated qualified interactions from the target segment.

Raw platform MAU is not audience-fit evidence.

## 3. Atomization is semantic adaptation, not duplication

One validated specialist source asset may produce several social artifacts, but each artifact must preserve the underlying claim provenance while being rebuilt for the platform job.

Source object examples:

- MintTap: verified ROC/split/distribution tracking explanation, app change, worked tracking example.
- LogMate: verified logging rule distinction, workflow/import explanation, device/offline design rationale.

Transformation record:

`source asset/claim IDs → platform → audience job → selected claim → native artifact → CTA/routing → expiry trigger`

Rules:

1. Preserve factual scope and source authority.
2. Do not turn uncertainty into certainty to make a stronger hook.
3. Do not strip jurisdiction/date qualifiers from financial, tax, regulatory, or professional claims.
4. Add platform-native explanatory value; do not mechanically cross-post identical text/media everywhere.
5. A social derivative inherits the source claim's decay/refresh trigger.
6. If the source claim is corrected or withdrawn, derivatives become correction/withdrawal candidates too.

This is particularly important because current platform direction can explicitly penalize recycled/low-value material. LinkedIn announced on 2026-03-12 that its feed is reducing generic, recycled, engagement-bait content and prioritizing relevant, authentic professional insight. Meta stated on 2026-03-13 that Facebook is prioritizing original content and deprioritizing duplicate/minimally edited content; Meta also reported increased recommendation share for original content on Instagram in late 2025/early 2026.

## 4. Do not build an algorithm-chasing production system

Platform ranking changes are evidence-decay triggers, not instructions to rebuild the company around transient hacks.

Prefer durable production characteristics:

- specialist specificity;
- original analysis or first-party product insight;
- credible evidence/provenance;
- clear usefulness without requiring a click;
- native presentation appropriate to the surface;
- non-deceptive routing to deeper assets.

Avoid:

- engagement pods or automated fake conversation;
- generic engagement bait;
- near-identical repost farms;
- publishing volume targets unsupported by company capacity or qualified outcomes.

LinkedIn's 2026 official feed update explicitly says it is acting against automated comments/inauthentic engagement and reducing generic/recycled/engagement-bait posts. Treat third-party claims about exact optimal lengths, posting frequency, link counts, or algorithm formulas as lower-tier evidence requiring product-specific validation, not company policy.

## 5. Maintenance economics

Every recurring account has a tail:

`research + drafting + visual adaptation + factual review + posting + replies/moderation + analytics + correction/refresh + policy/platform rechecks`

Therefore measure both creation labor and maintenance labor. A zero-cash account can still be economically negative.

Account-level record:

- platform;
- target segment;
- primary role;
- owner;
- recurring labor estimate/observed time;
- factual-review burden;
- moderation burden;
- reusable-source ratio;
- qualified evidence generated;
- downstream evidence available/unknown;
- next review trigger;
- CONTINUE / OBSERVE / PAUSE / STOP.

Do not invent a universal posting cadence. Cadence is constrained by useful source material, audience response, factual-review burden, and available labor.

## 6. Weak attribution: use an evidence ladder, not fake ROAS

When deterministic install attribution is unavailable, do not assign installs to a social post from temporal coincidence.

Evidence ladder:

S0 — OUTPUT: artifact published correctly.
S1 — RELEVANT EXPOSURE: target-relevant reach/impressions where the platform can credibly indicate it.
S2 — QUALIFIED RESPONSE: target-segment saves, replies, profile actions, substantive questions, or other behavior consistent with the intended role.
S3 — ROUTING: measurable qualified click/referral/campaign transition where technically valid.
S4 — DOWNSTREAM: attributable Store/product activation or retained use where instrumentation supports it.
S5 — TRIANGULATED INCREMENT: repeated pattern across native/referral/product evidence sufficient to update the decision, without pretending deterministic attribution.

Missing S3/S4 is UNKNOWN, not zero. S1/S2 must not be relabeled as installs or revenue.

For LISTENING/CREDIBILITY roles, S2 may be decision-useful even without direct installs. For an account whose declared role is ROUTING/acquisition, persistent inability to produce or measure qualified downstream movement lowers its portfolio priority.

## 7. Continue/stop rule

Continue recurring operation only while at least one declared role produces decision-relevant or durable value commensurate with its labor/maintenance tail.

Review or pause when:

- audience fit remains unverified after a reasonable evidence window;
- responses are broad but not target-qualified;
- content is mostly duplicated from stronger owned/community surfaces;
- maintenance consumes constrained Design/Web/Marketing capacity with little reusable output;
- factual-review burden is disproportionate to value;
- the platform creates weak signals that do not change decisions;
- community/owned/search channels dominate the same job with lower trust or labor cost.

Stop is not failure. It is portfolio reallocation.

No universal numeric stop threshold is set before live baselines exist.

## 8. MintTap implications

Candidate social work should originate from verified YieldMax-specific problems and owned content, not generic investing commentary. High-fit artifact families may include concise explanations of tracking mechanics, annotated product changes, and corrections/updates where the app has real specialist competence.

Do not convert community anecdotes into market prevalence claims or financial/tax facts. Social claims about ROC/tax/distributions inherit the Content Evidence Lifecycle.

Reddit remains governed by the Community Permission Ledger rather than this social-account model.

## 9. LogMate implications

Professional credibility is potentially more important than broad entertainment reach. Candidate artifacts should come from verified pilot/logbook workflow knowledge, product design reasoning, import/offline/device constraints, and jurisdiction-scoped authoritative material.

Do not publish broad aviation lifestyle content merely to inflate reach if it does not attract the intended pilot segment or strengthen product credibility. Regulatory derivatives retain jurisdiction/version qualifiers and claim refresh triggers.

## 10. Cross-product reuse

Transfer T0/T1:

- platform-role gate;
- maintenance economics;
- weak-attribution evidence ladder;
- provenance-preserving atomization;
- stop/continue governance.

Do not transfer as effect-size evidence:

- platform performance;
- format performance;
- cadence;
- CTR/engagement rates;
- audience concentration;
- MintTap social outcomes to LogMate or vice versa.

## 11. Decision template

Before opening or materially expanding an account, record:

1. Target specialist segment.
2. Distinct platform role.
3. Evidence of audience/platform fit.
4. Source assets available for native adaptation.
5. Required recurring labor/capabilities.
6. Factual/moderation/maintenance tail.
7. Evidence ladder level realistically measurable.
8. Minimum practical value the account must create.
9. Review/decay trigger.
10. Portfolio alternative that would receive the same labor if the account is not operated.

Then route through the existing Decision Queue: ACT+MONITOR / OBSERVE / EXPERIMENT / RESEARCH FIRST / DEFER.
