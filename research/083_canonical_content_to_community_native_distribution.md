# 083 — Canonical Content → Community-Native Distribution

Date: 2026-09-18

## Why this exists

082 established that specialist content must earn the query before SEO amplification. This note addresses the next operational problem: how one high-trust specialist asset can compound across owned blog, Reddit, social, and Store routes without becoming duplicate low-value promotion or spam.

## Evidence checked

### Google Search canonicalization
Google Search Central documents canonicalization as selection of a representative URL among duplicate/near-duplicate URLs. Duplicate content itself is normal and not automatically a spam-policy violation. Canonical hints are signals, not absolute directives. For regional same-language variants, Google recommends canonicalization together with `hreflang` where appropriate.

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/canonicalization

### Reddit reposting and spam
Reddit's current help guidance says reposting can legitimately extend a community's reach while preserving the original source/community, but not every community permits reposts. Reposts must be relevant to the target community and comply with its rules. Reddit's spam policy prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement and mass-posting repetitive content for exposure or financial gain. Reddit also states promotional content is not inherently spam, while individual communities may prohibit it or apply stricter self-promotion norms.

Sources:
- https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/15484385684628-How-to-repost-fka-crosspost-your-community-s-content-to-other-communities
- https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam
- https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/28012014962580-How-do-I-keep-spam-out-of-my-community

## Canonical principle

> **One evidence asset, multiple native expressions; never one promotional payload sprayed across channels.**

A canonical specialist article is the durable evidence object. Distribution units are adaptations to the receiving context, not copies whose main purpose is to manufacture links or impressions.

## D0–D5 distribution eligibility

| Class | State | Growth inventory? |
|---|---|---|
| D0 | Topic/asset does not meet 082 specialist-content eligibility | No |
| D1 | Canonical asset exists, but target-channel permission/relevance unknown | No |
| D2 | Permission is known, but proposed distribution is mostly duplicated CTA/link payload | No |
| D3 | Community-native standalone value exists; reader can benefit without clicking | Conditional |
| D4 | D3 + source/provenance is transparent + CTA is subordinate + channel rules/format are satisfied | Yes |
| D5 | D4 + observed qualified downstream behavior without moderation/spam/trust incidents | Repeatable pattern |

The reusable unit is therefore not `article → same post everywhere`. It is:

`canonical evidence asset → channel permission → native value extraction → optional provenance/deep link → qualified response → first value/useful return`

## Channel contract

### Owned blog
The blog hosts the canonical, maintained N3/N4 evidence asset. It carries full sourcing, scope, limitations, update trigger, and product relationship disclosure. Do not create near-identical owned pages solely to match every community or social phrasing.

### Reddit
The Reddit post/comment must answer a real community job on-platform. A user should receive meaningful value without leaving Reddit. A canonical article/app link is optional and subordinate. Reposting is used only when the target community allows it and the original post is genuinely relevant. Do not mass-repost the same asset across adjacent finance/pilot communities.

### Social
Extract one useful claim, visual, calculation, checklist, or finding per unit. Social distribution is a compression layer, not a teaser-only link farm. Multiple posts from one canonical asset must expose materially different useful facets rather than repeat the same CTA.

### Store
Store routing is not the canonical evidence host. CPP/CSL can align a verified specialist intent to an earned product promise, but should not inherit article claims that the product cannot deliver at first value.

## MintTap application

Good canonical candidates are mechanics where MintTap has first-hand implementation knowledge and authoritative support: reverse-split tracking effects, distribution/ROC bookkeeping semantics, reinvestment interactions, and tax-adjustment recordkeeping boundaries.

A distribution sequence can be:
1. N4 blog article explaining the mechanic and limitations.
2. A Reddit-native worked example or concise explanation that is useful without leaving Reddit.
3. A social visual/checklist containing one independently useful insight.
4. Optional Store route only when the app's corresponding first-value path is evidence-qualified.

Do not publish `CONY version`, `TSLY version`, `MSTY version` of essentially the same article/post unless the underlying mechanic or user job materially differs.

## LogMate application

Do not distribute regulatory or logbook-compliance content until ownership/update triggers and primary-source discipline are established. When eligible, community posts should solve a pilot/logbook question directly rather than use regulatory anxiety as a click driver. App claims remain narrower than the evidence when jurisdiction/company variation exists.

## Measurement

Per canonical asset, record:

`asset_id | N-class | channel | community/account | permission state | D-class | native value unit | link/no-link | source_family | intent_family | qualified response | Store acquisition | first value | useful return | moderation/removal/spam incident | maintenance trigger`

Do not rank adaptations by raw impressions. A D5 pattern requires qualified downstream behavior and no evidence that distribution damaged community permission/trust.

## Operational rules

1. Canonical first, adaptation second.
2. Permission before tracking or reposting.
3. Every community/social adaptation must stand alone.
4. Provenance is explicit; do not disguise ownership or source.
5. CTA is optional and subordinate to the useful content.
6. Repetition is bounded by distinct user value, not by available channels.
7. Search canonicalization solves duplicate-URL representation; it does not legitimize low-value content multiplication.
8. Community repost support is a distribution mechanism, not blanket permission.
9. Stop a pattern on moderation/removal/spam signals even if clicks are high.
10. Promote D4 to D5 only with downstream first-value/useful-return evidence.

## New decision rule

**Distribution efficiency is not posts per article. It is qualified specialist value delivered per maintained evidence asset without exhausting trust.**

This makes zero-cost marketing compound through knowledge reuse while preserving the scarcity of niche-community attention.
