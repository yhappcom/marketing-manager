# 084 — Owned Community Governance Before Growth

Date: 2026-09-18
Status: CANONICAL

## Why this fills a real gap

083 established how a maintained evidence asset can become a community-native distribution unit. That is insufficient for a company-operated subreddit or future owned professional community because the company simultaneously acts as publisher, beneficiary, and moderator. Growth incentives can therefore corrupt moderation incentives unless governance is explicit before scale.

## Authoritative findings

### Reddit communities are locally governed
Reddit's current moderator documentation says each community has its own focus and rules, and moderators can remove content that does not fit those rules. Reddit's Moderator Code of Conduct also requires moderators to set appropriate and reasonable expectations and to clearly identify whether a community is official or unofficial when that distinction matters.

Operational implication: a company-operated community must not rely on sitewide policy alone. Its own scope, promotion rules, disclosure expectations, and moderation behavior must be understandable before members contribute.

### Promotion is not automatically spam, but repeated self-benefiting distribution is risky
Reddit's current spam guidance says promotional content is not inherently spam. It also states that individual communities may prohibit it or impose their own ratios; Reddit's moderator guidance mentions that some communities use a 10% self-promotion convention. This is not a universal Reddit entitlement or universal sitewide rule.

Reddit's sitewide spam policy prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement and lists repetitive mass-posting for exposure or financial gain as behavior that may violate policy.

Operational implication: "90/10" must never be treated as a safe-harbor formula. A company cannot manufacture legitimacy by posting nine low-value items around one promotional item.

### Moderation must remain active, not merely protective of the brand
Reddit's current moderator guidance describes active moderation as checking queues/modmail, acting on reports, and engaging with the community. Moderators can distinguish official moderator submissions, but that capability does not convert brand claims into neutral community consensus.

Operational implication: an owned subreddit is a community product with moderation obligations, not a free advertising surface.

## Canonical principle

**Govern the community before harvesting the audience.**

Owned-community growth is eligible only when the community can remain useful even when a member never installs the app.

## O0–O5 owned-community maturity gate

- **O0 — Brand shell:** subreddit/community exists but purpose, ownership, rules, and moderation expectations are incomplete.
- **O1 — Disclosed ownership:** official/unofficial status and commercial relationship are clear, but member-value and promotion rules are incomplete.
- **O2 — Governance ready:** scope, allowed/disallowed promotion, disclosure, moderation, conflict handling, and report/modmail expectations are documented.
- **O3 — Independent member value:** useful discussion, questions, evidence, and member contributions are supported even when they do not benefit the app. Company posts meet the same topical/value standards expected of members.
- **O4 — Trust-preserving operation:** moderation decisions are logged where material; criticism and competing tools are not removed merely because they disadvantage the company; company/promotional posts are transparently identified; D4 distribution rules apply.
- **O5 — Sustainable community asset:** repeated evidence shows useful member-to-member activity, acceptable moderation load, no material spam/trust incidents, and qualified product discovery occurs as a by-product rather than the community's sole purpose.

O5 is not defined by subscriber count.

## Conflict-of-interest firewall

For company-operated communities:

1. **Moderator power cannot manufacture marketing evidence.** Pinned/stickied/distinguished company content must be tagged separately in performance analysis; its reach is not organic community preference.
2. **Criticism is not a moderation violation.** Remove content for published rules, safety, spam, privacy, illegality, or scope—not because it is unfavorable to MintTap/LogMate.
3. **Competitor mentions are not automatically off-topic.** Comparative discussion can create specialist value. Any restriction must be content-neutral and written in advance.
4. **Company identity is explicit.** Do not simulate independent users, testimonials, grassroots enthusiasm, or neutral moderator endorsement.
5. **Member data is not a prospecting list.** Do not use unsolicited mass chat/DM/community invitations as an acquisition tactic.
6. **Ad interaction is never recruited.** 074 remains controlling: recruit product use and discussion, never ad views/clicks.

## Measurement separation

Maintain separate fields for:

`content_origin (member/company) → moderator_amplification (none/pin/distinguish/megathread) → D-class → useful on-platform response → outbound click → Store acquisition → first value → useful return → moderation/trust incident`

Do not report pinned company-post reach as equivalent to member-generated reach. Do not treat subscriber growth as qualified acquisition without downstream evidence.

## MintTap application

r/MintTapforYieldMax should be evaluated first as an owned-community governance system, not as another Reddit acquisition channel. Before scaling promotion:

- verify that official ownership/affiliation is explicit;
- publish a narrow YieldMax/MintTap-relevant scope and promotion/disclosure rules;
- ensure useful YieldMax tracking discussion can exist without requiring MintTap installation;
- permit product criticism and relevant alternatives under the same topical rules;
- separate company-originated posts from member-originated evidence;
- avoid ticker-by-ticker repetitive posting and unsolicited invitations;
- apply 082 N-class to source assets and 083 D-class to every reused distribution unit.

The community should not be called successful merely because it sends installs. O5 requires community utility plus qualified downstream product behavior without trust degradation.

## LogMate application

Do not create an owned pilot community merely to secure a launch audience. A pilot community has high trust requirements because operational/regulatory claims can be consequential. Creation should wait until there is a defensible specialist scope, moderation capacity, claim/evidence discipline, and enough production product truth to support useful discussion without promotional substitution.

## Reusable company rule

For future niche apps, choose among three different assets deliberately:

- **Owned publishing surface:** company controls the message; discussion is secondary.
- **Owned community:** company controls moderation infrastructure but must preserve member utility and governance legitimacy.
- **External community participation:** company has no governance authority and must follow the receiving community's rules.

Never treat these as interchangeable distribution channels.

## Stop conditions

Pause growth/promotion and repair governance when any of the following appears:

- repeated removals or complaints tied to company self-promotion;
- members reasonably cannot tell company speech from independent speech;
- criticism is being moderated differently from praise;
- moderator amplification is required for most engagement;
- useful member-to-member activity remains absent while promotional posting rises;
- moderation workload exceeds available capacity;
- downstream installs rise while trust/moderation incidents worsen.
