# 085 — Employee, Founder & Affiliate Disclosure Contract

Date: 2026-09-18
Status: CANONICAL

## Why this exists

083 established that one evidence asset should become multiple channel-native expressions, and 084 established governance for company-operated communities. A remaining trust gap exists when founders, employees, contractors, community moderators, testers, affiliates, or other connected people discuss MintTap, LogMate, or future company apps in spaces where the relationship is not obvious.

Zero-cost distribution is not permission to make company-originated advocacy look independent.

## Canonical principle

**Relationship truth travels with the endorsement.**

A profile biography, subreddit ownership statement, employer page, or earlier disclosure does not reliably disclose the relationship for a particular post seen out of context. When a reasonable reader may not understand the connection, disclose it in or immediately adjacent to the relevant endorsement.

## First-party basis

FTC Endorsement Guides guidance states that material connections that may affect the weight or credibility of an endorsement should be disclosed. Connections are broader than payment: employment, family/personal relationships, free or discounted products, and other benefits can qualify. FTC guidance specifically says employees promoting their employer's products should disclose that relationship; merely identifying an employer on a profile is not necessarily sufficient because users may encounter an individual post without that context.

FTC guidance also emphasizes clear and conspicuous placement. Disclosure should be difficult to miss, close to the endorsement, and not hidden after a 'more' expansion, buried in hashtags, or expressed in ambiguous shorthand. Disclosure should use the language of the endorsement. Platform disclosure tools do not automatically cure an unclear message.

Affiliate relationships similarly require clear disclosure when the publisher earns a commission from a recommendation. A convenience link with no compensation does not create that financial connection.

## C0–C5 connection-disclosure gate

- **C0 — Hidden connection:** a connected person endorses the app while appearing independent.
- **C1 — Remote disclosure only:** connection exists in profile/about/community rules but not near the endorsement.
- **C2 — Present but ambiguous:** shorthand or placement can reasonably be missed or misunderstood.
- **C3 — Local explicit disclosure:** relationship is plainly disclosed with the endorsement in the audience's language.
- **C4 — Claim-safe connected advocacy:** C3 plus claims are evidence-qualified, channel-permitted, and no connected actor is presented as independent user proof.
- **C5 — Auditable program:** C4 plus connected-origin content is tagged in the distribution ledger, downstream performance is separated from independent advocacy, and incidents/corrections are tracked.

Only C3+ is eligible for intentional company amplification. C4 is the operating target. C5 is required before connected advocacy is treated as a repeatable growth program.

## MintTap / LogMate rules

1. Founder/owner/employee posts recommending the app in external communities disclose the relationship locally when it is not unmistakable from the post itself.
2. Company moderators do not use personal-looking accounts to manufacture member enthusiasm.
3. Beta testers, contractors, advisors, friends/family, or creators who receive meaningful benefits are not classified as independent advocacy until the material-connection question has been resolved.
4. Uncompensated genuine users remain independent; do not force unnecessary disclosure merely because they participate in an owned community.
5. If future affiliate/referral monetization is introduced, commission-bearing recommendations receive explicit local disclosure. Affiliate revenue must not silently change editorial ranking or product comparisons.
6. A connected person's testimonial is never evidence that the wider specialist audience has validated first value or useful return.
7. Disclosure does not cure a false, exaggerated, unsupported, or out-of-scope claim.

## Measurement contract

Extend the distribution ledger with:

`actor_origin → material_connection → disclosure_class → channel_permission → claim_class → moderator_amplification → qualified response → Store acquisition → first value → useful return → trust/compliance incident`

Connected advocacy and independent member advocacy must be reported separately. A founder post that performs well can validate message resonance in that context; it cannot be reported as organic word-of-mouth.

## Interaction with 082–084

- 082 N-class determines whether the underlying evidence asset deserves distribution.
- 083 D-class determines whether the channel-native expression has standalone value and permission.
- 084 O-class governs owned-community conflicts and moderator amplification.
- 085 C-class governs whether the speaker's relationship to the product is locally understandable.

A unit can therefore be N4/D4/O4 but still fail if the actor is C0–C2.

## Operational checklist

Before intentional amplification of a connected person's post, verify: relationship classification; whether the relationship is obvious in the post context; local disclosure wording and placement; disclosure language; claim evidence; community/platform permission; whether the actor is being counted separately from independent users; and whether any incentive could distort review/testimonial behavior.

## Stop conditions

Stop or correct distribution if disclosure is missing/ambiguous, an account is made to look independent when it is controlled by the company, a benefit is introduced after publication without updating disclosure, a testimonial claim exceeds evidence, or connected reach is being reported as independent community advocacy.
