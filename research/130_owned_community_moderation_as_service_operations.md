# 130 — Owned-community moderation as service operations

Validated: 2026-09-20

## Why this is new
Research 129 established the governance boundary for a company-operated subreddit. This note closes the next operational gap: governance principles are insufficient unless support, moderation and product-learning work have explicit queues, response states and escalation ownership.

## Authoritative platform evidence
Reddit's Moderator Code of Conduct Rule 2 requires communities to set clear and reasonable expectations, including an accurate official/unofficial label and explicit community rules. Rule 4 requires moderators to remain active and engaged; Reddit specifically identifies regular review of mod queue and modmail, action on reports, community engagement, and adding moderators when workload requires it. Rule 3 prohibits using an owned community to coordinate interference in other communities. Reddit also provides Brand Affiliate labeling for posts/comments with commercial intent or incentivized relationships, while disclosure compliance remains the contributor's responsibility.

## Canonical principle
**An owned niche community is a service surface before it is a distribution surface.**

A company subreddit should not be judged primarily by subscriber growth, post volume, outbound Store clicks or announcement reach. Its first operating obligation is predictable handling of user questions, bug reports, criticism, product feedback and rule violations without converting every interaction into acquisition pressure.

## AV0–AV5 Owned-Community Service-Operations Gate

### AV0 — Promo feed
Announcements and Store links dominate. No visible support/feedback workflow, response ownership or moderation routine.

### AV1 — Reactive moderation
Moderators respond opportunistically, but queue/modmail/reports have no stable review routine and user-facing expectations are unclear.

### AV2 — Basic service taxonomy
Community rules and affiliation are clear; posts can be classified as support, bug, feedback, education, announcement or community discussion. Handling is still person-dependent and product-learning continuity is weak.

### AV3 — Operable service surface
Required minimum:
- explicit official affiliation and stable community rules;
- routine review of mod queue, reports and modmail;
- stable post taxonomy: `support | bug | feedback | education | announcement | discussion`;
- state model for actionable reports: `new → acknowledged → needs evidence → product handoff → resolved/closed`;
- moderator removal decisions separated from product criticism decisions;
- legitimate criticism remains visible unless it violates a stated rule;
- product announcements and commercially intended contributions are identifiable and disclosed where required;
- no requirement to click a Store/site link to receive an answer that can reasonably be given on Reddit;
- recurring questions feed FAQ/documentation/product learning rather than repeated promotional replies;
- escalation owner exists for security/privacy/data-loss or materially harmful product defects;
- workload is reviewed so moderator capacity grows when the community requires it.

### AV4 — Evidence-backed service loop
Repeated evidence shows that the operating loop reduces unresolved recurring questions, converts valid feedback/bugs into product or documentation actions, maintains predictable moderation, and preserves genuine member-to-member participation without increasing promotional pressure.

### AV5 — Reusable niche-community operating system
The service taxonomy, escalation rules, disclosure standard, queue review, learning loop and health metrics are reusable for future specialist apps while preserving community-specific rules and norms.

## Metrics: what to measure and what not to optimize
Use operational denominators rather than vanity growth:
- actionable support/bug/feedback threads received;
- acknowledged and resolved/closed share;
- unresolved aging by severity;
- recurring-question clusters converted into FAQ/docs/product work;
- legitimate criticism removed for stated-rule reasons vs merely negative sentiment;
- member-to-member answers and discussions that require no company intervention;
- announcement/share of total company-authored activity;
- Store-link use only where contextually useful.

Do **not** set subscriber count, post frequency, Store CTR or positive-sentiment ratio as primary moderation KPIs. A moderation system that suppresses criticism can make those numbers look better while destroying specialist trust.

The first evidence inventory should capture affiliation wording, rules, current flairs/taxonomy, modmail/report handling, recurring questions, bug/feedback handoffs, announcement share, Store-link frequency, criticism handling and unresolved aging. Distribution attribution (AS) remains downstream of AU governance and AV service fitness.

Recommended flow:
`specialist/member need → AU governance boundary → AV service handling → product/docs learning → optional AS-attributable destination when useful`.

## LogMate application
Do not create an owned pilot community merely to reserve a channel. If product/support demand later justifies one, define AV3 operations before launch. Pilot trust and operational-record integrity make unanswered support or ambiguous company identity disproportionately costly.

## Company-wide reusable rule
A future niche app may open an owned community only when there is both (1) a real recurring community/support job and (2) an owner capable of sustaining AV3 operations. Channel ownership without service capacity is not an asset.
