# 131 — Social search discovery as a zero-cost second surface

Validated: 2026-09-20

## Why this is new
Research 127–130 established attribution and community operating boundaries. This note closes a different zero-cost gap: social/video posts can now be measured as Google Search discovery surfaces rather than being judged only by in-platform reach or outbound Store clicks.

## Authoritative platform evidence
Google Search Console platform properties are globally available for Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube as of 2026-07-29. Each account/channel is verified as its own property. Search Console reports Google Search/Discover/News impressions and clicks, average CTR/position, top posts and query groups. Google explicitly recommends using query themes for topic/caption/hashtag decisions, the 24-hour view for spikes, cross-platform exports for comparison, and annotations to assess changes such as rewritten titles/captions. Platform-property data measures discovery on Google; it does not measure impressions inside the social platform itself.

Google also supports Search profiles that can aggregate a creator/publisher's website and social presences. Following a Search profile can make linked content more likely to appear to that audience in Discover. This is a distribution option, not evidence that every app business needs every social platform.

## Canonical principle
**A social account can be a searchable specialist knowledge surface even when it produces little direct social-platform reach.**

For a zero-budget niche app, social content should therefore be evaluated on two independent discovery planes:
1. native-platform discovery/engagement; and
2. Google Search/Discover discovery measured through Search Console platform properties.

Do not collapse these into one `social traffic` KPI. A post can have modest native engagement yet answer a durable specialist query and continue receiving Google discovery.

## AW0–AW5 Social-Search Discovery Gate

### AW0 — Presence without evidence
Accounts exist because brands are expected to have them. Posting cadence and follower counts dominate; no search-discovery measurement exists.

### AW1 — Native-only reporting
Views, likes, followers and outbound clicks are measured, but Google discovery of social/video content is invisible.

### AW2 — Search visibility connected
Eligible Instagram/TikTok/X/YouTube accounts are verified as Search Console platform properties where the feature is available. Data is collected, but query/topic learning and downstream product utility are not yet connected.

### AW3 — Operable dual-surface system
Required minimum:
- each active supported account/channel has its own verified platform property;
- native-platform metrics and Google Search/Discover metrics remain separate;
- query groups and post-level search performance are reviewed for specialist intent, not merely traffic volume;
- durable query-led content is distinguished from short-lived trend content;
- content changes such as title/caption revisions are annotated before interpreting later movement;
- cross-platform comparisons use comparable intent/topic windows rather than raw follower-normalized vanity rankings;
- Google clicks are not assumed to be Store visits or installs; AS attribution remains downstream where an attributable Store path is actually present;
- no new social account is created merely because Search Console can measure it.

### AW4 — Evidence-backed discovery portfolio
Repeated observations identify which topic × format × platform combinations create qualified specialist discovery and useful downstream behavior. Older content that regains search demand is refreshed, pinned, expanded or reused only when the underlying intent remains valid.

### AW5 — Reusable niche social-search operating system
The company maintains a reusable topic registry, platform-property setup, query-cluster review, annotation/change log, cross-platform comparison method, content-refresh rule and downstream attribution contract for future specialist apps.

## Measurement contract
Use the chain:
`specialist query/theme → social/video result impression on Google → Google click/view → platform content utility → optional attributable destination → first value → useful return`.

Keep at least these fields distinct: `platform`, `account`, `content_id`, `topic_cluster`, `format`, `publish_date`, `Google surface`, `query/query-group`, `impressions`, `clicks`, `CTR`, `position where available`, `native-platform metrics`, `destination`, `campaign/referrer identity where applicable`, `first-value evidence`, `useful-return evidence`.

A Google Search click can open content inside a Google viewer and still count as a Search Console click. Therefore Search Console clicks must not be equated with outbound website/Store sessions.

## MintTap application
Prioritize searchable, durable YieldMax problems rather than generic finance posting: distribution-history interpretation, ROC/tax-adjustment workflow, reverse-split continuity and portfolio tracking mechanics. Use observed query groups to decide whether a topic deserves a deeper blog/article, revised social explanation or Store-page hypothesis. Do not create ticker-by-ticker near-duplicate posts solely to capture query variants.

The practical audit is now: determine which official MintTap Instagram/TikTok/X/YouTube accounts actually exist and are actively owned; connect only legitimate active accounts as platform properties; establish a baseline before changing titles/captions; then compare search-discovery topics with Reddit/community questions and Store-intent evidence.

## LogMate application
Do not build broad lifestyle social accounts merely to accumulate reach. When launch evidence warrants content, prioritize durable pilot jobs such as logbook import, duplicate handling, previous totals, offline/PWA continuity and workflow explanations. A pilot query that repeatedly discovers a precise explanatory video/post is more valuable than broad aviation entertainment reach if it produces qualified product understanding.

## Company-wide reusable rule
Channel selection precedes measurement. Search Console support for four social/video platforms is not a mandate to operate four accounts. Create/maintain a channel only when the target specialist audience and content format justify it; once active, treat Google search discovery as a second measurable surface.
