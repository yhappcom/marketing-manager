# 147 — Founder/employee social disclosure and authentic advocacy contract

Validated: 2026-09-20

## Why this is a distinct problem
MintTap and future niche apps rely on zero-cost participation in Reddit, blogs and social media. Permission to post is only one layer. When a founder, employee, contractor, family member, compensated advocate or other materially connected person praises or recommends the product, the relationship itself can change how a reasonable reader weighs the endorsement. Community permission therefore does not substitute for relationship disclosure.

## Authoritative findings
The FTC's revised Endorsement Guides (revised June 2023) preserve the core rule that endorsements must be truthful and not misleading and that unexpected material connections affecting credibility should be disclosed. FTC guidance identifies employment, financial, personal and family relationships as material connections. The FTC also warns not to assume followers already know the relationship.

Disclosure must be clear and conspicuous in the endorsement itself. Profile/about-page disclosure alone is weak because readers may never see it; disclosures hidden after a `more` expansion, buried among hashtags/links, or expressed through vague shorthand may also fail. Platform disclosure tools are not automatically sufficient. FTC Q&A specifically says plain language such as `my company’s` or `I work for XYZ` can be clearer than ambiguous employee hashtags.

The FTC's Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule adds a separate review-specific concern: insiders can review a business only with clear and conspicuous relationship disclosure, and even disclosed insider reviews can create deception if they materially distort an aggregate star rating. Therefore company-controlled accounts, employees, family or advocates must never be mobilized to manufacture Store/review/social proof.

Reddit adds a separate platform/community layer. Reddit says promotional content is not inherently spam, but individual communities can prohibit it or impose their own limits. Repeated unwanted/unsolicited mass engagement remains spam. The Moderator Code requires official/unofficial brand affiliation to be accurately denoted and prohibits moderation actions in exchange for compensation or favors. Thus `allowed by subreddit rules`, `not spam`, `relationship disclosed`, and `truthful/substantiated claim` are four separate gates.

## BK0–BK5 Connected-Advocacy Disclosure Gate

### BK0 — identity unknown
Do not distribute a product endorsement if the relationship between speaker and product is not recorded.

### BK1 — relationship classified
Record at least: founder/owner, employee, contractor/agency, family/personal connection, compensated/incentivized advocate, uncompensated independent user, or unknown. Unknown does not inherit `independent` status.

### BK2 — venue permission established
Check current platform and community rules independently of disclosure. Disclosure cannot make a prohibited promotional post permissible.

### BK3 — disclosure attached to the endorsement
Where a material connection exists, use plain-language relationship disclosure that is visible with the endorsement and does not require profile navigation or expansion to understand. Preserve language/context and evidence date. Do not rely solely on account biography, flair, platform disclosure controls, vague hashtags, or presumed audience knowledge.

### BK4 — claim and social-proof integrity
The connected person may state genuine experience/opinion but may not make objective claims the company cannot substantiate. Do not coordinate connected people to inflate ratings, reviews, votes, karma, comments or apparent independent consensus. Connected feedback is useful research but must remain labeled as connected evidence.

### BK5 — auditable advocacy program
For repeated company-led advocacy, maintain a small registry of actor relationship, permitted venues, required disclosure form, claims allowed, post evidence and remediation. Periodically sample live posts rather than assuming policy delivery equals compliance.

## MintTap operating implications
1. A founder posting `I built MintTap` is materially better evidence than merely using the product name from an apparently ordinary investor account. The relationship statement belongs in the post/comment where the recommendation occurs.
2. `No external link` does not remove promotional or endorsement character. Likewise, an explicit relationship does not override a subreddit ban on promotion.
3. Company-operated r/MintTapforYieldMax must disclose official affiliation at community level under Reddit governance, while individual product recommendations still need context-appropriate speaker disclosure when the relationship would otherwise be unclear.
4. Do not ask employees, family, moderators or community regulars to leave Store reviews, Reddit praise or social posts in a way that creates apparent independent consensus. Never condition perks, moderation treatment or access on favorable advocacy.
5. User quotes/testimonials selected for Store, website or social reuse require provenance and truthfulness review; do not edit them so the resulting claim exceeds what the user actually said or what the product can substantiate.

## Reusable company contract
`actor relationship → current venue permission → clear in-message disclosure when material → truthful/substantiated claim → no manufactured consensus → evidence capture → qualified downstream outcome`

This sits alongside AT/BI/BJ rather than replacing them. AT answers whether the venue permits the activity; BI answers how an owned community is governed; BJ routes community learning; BK answers whether connected advocacy is transparently represented.

## Anti-patterns
- `Founder` only in profile bio while promotional comments appear independent.
- Assuming a branded username makes every relationship obvious.
- Treating #partner/#employee or a platform badge as universally sufficient.
- Asking staff/family to seed positive App Store/Google Play reviews or upvote company posts.
- Offering moderator privileges, flair, pinning or access in exchange for advocacy.
- Reusing a genuine user quote after materially changing its meaning.
- Treating disclosure as permission to make unsupported performance, investment or aviation claims.
