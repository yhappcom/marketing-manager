# Research 249 — Rating/Review Recovery Integrity

Date: 2026-09-25
Status: validated

## New findings
Apple summary ratings are territory-specific. Apple recommends requesting ratings after genuine satisfaction or a completed task without interrupting activity. Developers can reply to reviews; reviewers are notified and can update reviews. Apple explicitly recommends replying to older reviews after a relevant fix. A summary-rating reset can accompany a new version, but it is irreversible after release and does not remove written reviews.

Google Play’s public rating is weighted toward recent ratings and is distinct from lifetime average. Ratings do not reset when a new version ships. New ratings and reviews are generally held about 24 hours for abuse detection. Play Console exposes review themes, benchmarks and updated-rating evidence, including updates after developer replies. Google prohibits incentives and forced or deceptive prompts.

Google confirmed a system-wide In-App Review issue reduced rating/review volume from 2026-04-21 through 2026-05-06. Historical analysis spanning that period must not attribute the drop to product or prompt changes without correction.

## Operating framework
The review program is: completed specialist value → honest native prompt → classify negative feedback → fix material defects → factual reply → observe voluntary updates → feed recurring themes back into product, Store promise and support.

MintTap must not trigger review requests from favorable market moves, distributions or investment outcomes. Candidate triggers follow genuine product-value completion. LogMate candidates follow successful professional-work completion and never interrupt logging, recovery, validation, sync or compliance/recency interpretation.

## FI0–FI5
FI0 value identity → FI1 prompt integrity → FI2 evidence identity → FI3 recovery integrity → FI4 measurement integrity → FI5 trust decision.

Preserve these distinctions: high rating is not retention; more prompts are not sustainable trust; low review volume is not weak sparse-niche product; a developer reply does not prove it caused a rating change; Apple rating reset does not delete written reviews; a new Google Play version does not reset ratings.
