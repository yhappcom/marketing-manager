# Research 255 — Sparse-Niche Channel Marginal Allocation

Validated: 2026-09-25

## Core finding
For a niche professional app, zero-cost marketing is constrained by operator time. Allocate the next unit of work by evidence-adjusted contribution to repeated specialist value, not by channel vanity metrics.

## FV0–FV7 gate
1. Specialist-demand eligibility.
2. Truthful channel → Store → product promise continuity.
3. Define one bounded, comparable work increment.
4. Measure native channel evidence before cross-channel comparison.
5. Preserve attribution limitations and unknowns.
6. Prefer qualified activation and repeated core value over clicks or installs.
7. Include operator time, reusable asset/learning value, maintenance risk and opportunity cost.
8. Decide ALLOCATE / MAINTAIN / PAUSE / LEARN.

## Evidence ladder
E0 assumption; E1 audience/problem evidence; E2 qualified engagement/discovery; E3 attributable Store/site transition or download; E4 qualified activation; E5 repeated core value; E6 replicated marginal effect.

## Measurement constraints
Apple App Store Connect separates search, browse, app-referrer, web-referrer and campaign acquisition and can connect the recorded download source to later usage. On iOS, Safari website referrals are Web Referrers, while non-Safari browser referrals can appear as browser App Referrers. Privacy thresholds can suppress sparse detail. Missing attribution therefore does not prove zero effect.

Google Analytics attribution is model-dependent; attribution credit is not causal incrementality. Search Console discovery metrics, including the new multimodal reporting, are demand signals rather than proof of app acquisition.

## Portfolio application
MintTap: prioritize durable YieldMax investor jobs such as distribution/ROC interpretation, split-adjusted history and portfolio tracking; do not allocate by ticker popularity or duplicate ticker pages. Distribution-cycle traffic spikes are confounders.

LogMate: pre-launch work should favor pilot-language/problem evidence and reusable launch assets because retention evidence does not yet exist. After launch, upgrade channel confidence only with Store/referral and activation evidence.

## Ledger
app | channel | specialist job | bounded increment | operator time | native evidence | attribution caveat | Store transition | activation | repeated value | reusable asset/learning | E0-E6 | decision | next test

## Prohibited inferences
Zero media spend = zero cost; followers/karma/page views = acquisition; attributed download = incremental download; missing referrer = zero effect; Store conversion = retained value; one viral post = repeatable channel; every niche app needs every channel.
