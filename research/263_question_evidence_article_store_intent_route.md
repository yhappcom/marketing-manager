# Research 263 — Question → Evidence Article → Store Intent Route

Validated: 2026-09-26

## Decision

Owned web is not a publication-volume channel. For sparse professional apps, its highest-value zero-cost role is to convert recurring community questions into durable, searchable evidence assets and then route only product-relevant intent to the correct Store destination.

Canonical loop:

`recurring specialist question → evidence-qualified article → crawlable owned-web asset → contextual product bridge → platform-specific Store attribution → qualified activation → repeated specialist value → article refresh / product learning`

Do not publish an article merely because a keyword exists. The question must be relevant to the product's intended specialist audience and the article must remain useful if every product CTA is removed.

## GJ0–GJ8 Question-to-Evidence Gate

### GJ0 — Demand provenance
Record where the question came from: repeated community questions, support, Store-search evidence, product analytics, or an authoritative rule/change. One isolated post is qualitative evidence, not proof of broad demand.

### GJ1 — Audience/product fit
The question must belong to the audience the company actually serves. Reject adjacent high-volume topics that do not improve MintTap, LogMate, or a future niche app's specialist utility.

### GJ2 — Independent answer value
The page must solve the user's question before asking for an install. Product mention is optional and subordinate.

### GJ3 — Evidence integrity
Separate official facts, calculations, product behavior, practitioner interpretation, and unresolved uncertainty. High-stakes finance/aviation claims require authoritative sourcing and visible update discipline.

### GJ4 — Search/index integrity
Use a stable descriptive URL, unique descriptive title/meta description, semantic HTML, crawlable internal links, sitemap inclusion, and indexability checks. Important information must exist as text, not only screenshots/canvas/JS UI.

### GJ5 — Structured-data truth
Use Article/BlogPosting markup only when it accurately describes the page; include applicable author/date/headline/image properties. Structured data is an understanding/eligibility aid, not a ranking guarantee. Never add QAPage merely because an article contains an FAQ section; use schema only when the page actually matches Google's supported content type.

### GJ6 — Product bridge integrity
A CTA appears only where the answered problem naturally creates product intent. The bridge must state what the app actually does; no fake urgency, forced interstitial, gated answer, or misleading regulatory/financial promise.

### GJ7 — Attribution proportionality
Preserve the article URL as the owned-web acquisition source. Use platform-specific Store attribution where available. Apple campaign links can connect a campaign token to product-page views, downloads, usage, sales and subscriptions, but sparse rows can be withheld/combined and dashboard metrics require privacy thresholds. Missing data is not zero.

### GJ8 — Downstream value
Judge the asset by qualified acquisition/activation and repeated specialist value when observable, not pageviews or Store clicks alone. Low-volume pages can remain valuable when they repeatedly resolve a high-intent specialist problem.

## MintTap application

Priority article families should arise from real YieldMax-owner problems, not ticker-page multiplication. Strong candidates include:
- how distributions, return of capital and tax adjustments alter portfolio interpretation;
- reverse-split effects on historical holdings and cost/quantity continuity;
- how reinvestments affect portfolio tracking;
- distinctions between distribution cash flow and investment return.

Do not mass-produce one near-identical SEO page per YieldMax ticker. A ticker-specific page is justified only when the underlying problem/evidence materially differs. Finance content must distinguish educational tracking explanations from investment advice and avoid implying expected returns.

A useful MintTap article should answer the accounting/tracking question fully, then optionally bridge to the relevant MintTap capability. Community posts should link to it only when community rules permit and the article genuinely expands the native answer.

## LogMate application

Priority families should come from actual pilot/logbook workflows:
- importing an existing electronic logbook without losing previous totals;
- duplicate detection and reconciliation during migration;
- what fields different source exports contain and how mappings differ;
- device/offline/PWA continuity;
- jurisdiction-specific logging requirements only when authoritative sources and scope are explicit.

Avoid generic aviation-news publishing. LogMate's owned web should become a narrow technical reference library for pilot logbook problems.

## Article contract

Each durable evidence article records:
- question/problem statement;
- audience and jurisdiction/scope;
- provenance/evidence strength;
- authoritative sources and checked date;
- concise answer;
- explanation/examples;
- uncertainty or exceptions;
- product relevance and CTA eligibility;
- canonical URL;
- title/meta description;
- internal links;
- structured-data type if truthful;
- publication/last substantive review date;
- Store destination and attribution token/route;
- Search Console evidence;
- Store acquisition evidence;
- qualified activation/repeated-value evidence;
- KEEP / REFRESH / MERGE / RETIRE decision.

Do not change dates merely to manufacture freshness. Update the date when the substance has actually changed.

## Portfolio operating rule

Community, owned web and Store are different surfaces:
- Community: native problem-solving and demand discovery.
- Owned web: durable evidence and search capture.
- Store: conversion from product-relevant intent.
- App: delivery of specialist value.

Do not force every community interaction through the website. Direct Store routing can be appropriate when the user already has explicit install intent; an owned-web detour is justified when explanation/evidence materially helps the decision.

## Technical baseline

Google's current Search guidance supports this architecture: prioritize helpful, reliable, people-first content; use audience language in prominent locations; make links crawlable; keep important information textually visible; use descriptive titles/meta descriptions and semantic HTML; submit sitemaps; and use structured data when it truthfully represents the page. Search visibility is free, but compliance does not guarantee crawling, indexing, or ranking.

For Article/BlogPosting pages, applicable Article structured data can help Google understand title, image, date and author information. It is not mandatory for ordinary indexing and does not guarantee a rich result.

## Anti-patterns

BLOCK:
- scaled keyword/ticker/location pages with little distinct value;
- articles that merely summarize other sites;
- fabricated community questions;
- hiding the answer behind an install CTA;
- rewriting titles/dates solely for search traffic;
- unsupported finance/aviation claims;
- duplicate near-identical pages;
- generic social reposting counted as evidence;
- treating Search impressions, clicks or rankings as qualified growth.
