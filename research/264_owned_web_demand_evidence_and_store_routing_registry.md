# Research 264 — Owned-Web Demand Evidence and Store Routing Registry

Date: 2026-09-26

## Decision
Owned web is not a page-production target. It is a durable evidence layer between recurring specialist questions and a truthful Store destination.

Canonical chain:
`recurring specialist problem → evidence-worthy query cluster → independently useful article → Search discovery evidence → optional product bridge → attributed Store destination → qualified activation → repeated specialist value`

## GK0–GK8 gate
0. **Demand provenance** — a topic must come from recurring community/support/Search/product evidence, not keyword-volume speculation alone.
1. **Problem-cluster integrity** — consolidate equivalent questions around one specialist job; do not create ticker/model/jurisdiction permutations merely to multiply URLs.
2. **Independent utility** — the page must solve the stated problem even if all MintTap/LogMate CTAs are removed.
3. **Evidence integrity** — factual/regulatory/tax/technical claims require suitable primary or authoritative sources and a review date.
4. **Search integrity** — unique title/URL, crawlable semantic text, useful internal links, indexability and canonical hygiene. No scaled low-value page generation.
5. **Destination integrity** — add a Store/product bridge only when the article creates legitimate product intent. Direct-install intent should not be forced through an article.
6. **Attribution proportionality** — use campaign/source evidence at a granularity traffic can support. Privacy-threshold absence is UNKNOWN, not zero.
7. **Downstream-value validation** — clicks/downloads are intermediate; prefer qualified activation and repeated specialist value when observable.
8. **Maintenance/retirement** — refresh claims whose truth changes; consolidate cannibalizing pages; retire pages whose premise is obsolete rather than preserving them for traffic.

## Article registry
Minimum fields:
- app
- problem_cluster_id
- audience/job
- provenance (community/support/Search/product)
- evidence examples and dates
- article URL/status
- authoritative sources + reviewed_at
- search-intent class: learn / compare / solve / install
- product bridge rationale
- Store destination
- Apple campaign token or Android attribution key where justified
- Search impressions/clicks/query families
- Store page views/downloads where observable
- qualified activation
- repeated specialist value
- maintenance trigger
- decision: KEEP / UPDATE / CONSOLIDATE / RETIRE / NO-PUBLISH

## Portfolio application
### MintTap
Prefer durable problem clusters such as ROC accounting, reverse-split history, distribution-vs-return interpretation, reinvestment and tax-adjustment tracking. Do not manufacture near-duplicate TSLY/MSTY/CONY pages unless the underlying question, evidence, and answer materially differ.

### LogMate
Prefer pilot jobs such as migration/import mapping, previous totals, duplicate reconciliation, offline/PWA continuity, and jurisdiction-specific logging questions only where authoritative evidence is available. Do not broaden into generic aviation news for traffic.

## Attribution constraints
Apple campaign links can associate campaign tokens with App Store impressions, product-page views, downloads, usage, sales and subscriptions. Dashboard metrics appear only after minimum privacy thresholds; detailed small cohorts may be withheld or combined. Therefore sparse niche article-level attribution must not interpret missing campaign rows as zero performance.

## Anti-patterns
- page count as KPI
- programmatic ticker pages with substantially identical answers
- rewriting community posts into SEO pages without added evidence
- CTA-first articles
- publishing outside the product's specialist competence to chase volume
- treating rank/click as business success
- retaining obsolete pages solely because they have impressions
- fragmenting attribution until privacy thresholds make the evidence unusable

## Operating rule
A new article is justified only when it improves one of: durable specialist answer coverage, trustworthy product-intent routing, or reusable demand evidence. Otherwise NO-PUBLISH is the correct decision.
