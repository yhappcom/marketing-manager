# Research 273 — Community Saturation & Marginal-Value Stop Rule

Validated: 2026-09-27

## Purpose
Stop zero-cost community marketing from becoming an unbounded posting obligation. Once a specialist problem is sufficiently understood, the scarce resource is operator attention. Additional community actions must earn their cost by adding independent evidence, qualified/repeated product value, or a durable reusable asset.

## Core distinction
**Problem saturation is not market saturation.** A job can be well understood while many potential users remain unreached. Saturation means additional observations/actions are no longer materially changing the decision, not that the addressable audience has been exhausted.

Also separate:
- **evidence saturation** — new observations mostly repeat an established job/language/capability conclusion;
- **distribution saturation** — another contribution on the same surface produces little incremental qualified value;
- **asset maturity** — a durable owned answer now handles the recurring explanation better than repeated manual replies;
- **permission pressure** — repeated distribution increases moderation/spam risk even when the underlying information remains useful.

## FS0–FS8 gate
**FS0 Normalize the job.** Measure marginal value by specialist job, not ticker, airline, vendor, subreddit, post count, follower count, or content format.

**FS1 Preserve lineage.** Same-thread agreement, reposts, quotes, cross-posts and derivative summaries can increase salience but do not reset the evidence counter.

**FS2 Define the decision being learned.** Every community action must be capable of changing at least one decision: problem definition, native wording, capability priority, destination, Store intent, support artifact, or channel allocation.

**FS3 Measure marginal evidence.** A new observation is decision-useful only if it adds an independent origin, materially different context, new stable wording, previously unknown severity/workflow consequence, or contradictory evidence requiring revision.

**FS4 Measure marginal value.** A distribution action is useful when it produces observable qualified specialist engagement, first/repeated core value where attribution permits, or a durable answer that reduces future operator work. Raw impressions, karma, likes, followers and clicks are not sufficient.

**FS5 Detect asset crossover.** When the same verified question repeatedly requires substantially the same answer, stop rewriting it manually. Promote the explanation to a versioned FAQ/article/calculator/reference, then use community-native answers only when they add context. The owned asset is a maintenance target, not a license for repeated link dropping.

**FS6 Respect permission pressure.** Reddit's current guidance treats repeated same/similar comments across threads or communities as spam-risk behavior, and sitewide policy prohibits mass repetitive posting for exposure or financial gain. Declining marginal value therefore strengthens the case to reduce distribution rather than increase frequency.

**FS7 Apply stop/reallocation state.** Use KEEP, REDUCE, MAINTAIN_ASSET, REOPEN, or STOP. Never increase posting cadence merely because a job is validated.

**FS8 Reopen only on novelty.** A saturated job re-enters active research when there is a material product/platform change, new independent context, contradictory evidence, changed community permission, or evidence that the existing owned artifact no longer resolves the job.

## Operational ledger
Per normalized job and surface retain:
`job_id | evidence_lineage_id | independent_origin_delta | context_novelty | wording_novelty | contradiction | decision_changed | qualified_value_delta | repeated_value_delta | durable_asset_delta | operator_minutes | moderation_minutes | permission_state | asset_state | marginal_state | decision | reopen_trigger`

Do not collapse these into a universal score. Sparse-niche volumes are too small for pseudo-precise thresholds to be portable.

## Practical stop rule
After each action ask:
1. Did this add independent evidence that could change a product/marketing decision?
2. Did it produce observable qualified or repeated specialist value?
3. Did it create or materially improve a durable reusable asset?

If **none** is true, classify the action as zero marginal output. One zero-output action is not proof of saturation; repeated zero-output actions on the same job/surface are a REDUCE signal. If the answer has also stabilized and an owned artifact exists, move to MAINTAIN_ASSET. If permission risk/removal or operator burden is material, STOP that distribution pattern immediately.

No fixed universal count (for example, “three posts” or “ten comments”) is canonical. The stop condition is decision stability plus declining marginal output, not an arbitrary activity quota.

## Community-to-owned crossover
Use community participation for discovery, clarification and context. Use owned content for stable, reference-heavy or repeatedly requested explanations. The crossover is justified when:
- independent recurrence is already established;
- native wording has stabilized;
- the product capability/destination is truthful;
- repeated manual answers are substantially duplicative;
- the answer benefits from versioning, calculations, screenshots, citations or change tracking.

Continue native community participation after crossover only when a fresh user context requires interpretation. Do not syndicate the owned URL as a growth loop.

## Store escalation boundary
Saturation does not automatically imply Store experimentation. A stable community job becomes a Store-route candidate only when it also has truthful capability, matching destination and enough traffic/evidence to justify fragmentation.

Apple currently allows up to 70 Custom Product Pages, but each page's analytics appears only after at least five first-time downloads. Apple CPP analytics includes conversion plus downstream engagement/proceeds. Therefore sparse traffic should be conserved for materially distinct validated intents, not consumed by every saturated community topic.

## MintTap
Treat ticker-specific repetition as potentially one saturated business-level job. If TSLY, CONY and MSTY discussions repeatedly reduce to the same distribution/ROC interpretation or corporate-action continuity question, do not create endless ticker-specific posts. Once language and explanation stabilize, maintain one authoritative artifact and answer only context-specific deltas. Reopen when tax treatment, fund mechanics, corporate actions, product capability, or independent investor context materially changes.

## LogMate
Do not equate different airlines, aircraft or roster vendors with independent marketing jobs. If repeated discussions reduce to import/migration, Previous Total continuity, duplicate reconciliation, record/export integrity or offline/device continuity, move stable explanations into durable pilot-facing artifacts. Reopen when a source-system format, regulatory/logbook requirement, import parser, platform capability, or pilot workflow materially changes.

## Evidence basis
- Reddit Help, “How do I keep spam out of my community?”, updated 2026-04-02: repeated same/similar comments across a thread, community or multiple communities are examples of spam-risk behavior; promotional content may also be restricted by individual communities.
- Reddit Help, “Spam”, updated 2026-05-19: repeated/unsolicited mass engagement and mass repetitive content for exposure or financial gain are prohibited.
- Apple Developer, Custom Product Pages / App Store Connect Analytics, current 2026 documentation: up to 70 CPPs; page-specific analytics begins after five first-time downloads and can include downstream engagement and proceeds.

## Reusable principle
`discover until the decision stabilizes → distribute only while marginal qualified value exists → convert repeated explanation into a durable asset → maintain instead of syndicating → reopen on material novelty`

“Zero-cost” means zero media spend, not zero opportunity cost.

Integrate community evidence, Store intent, destination continuity, analytics readiness and a no-paid-media launch sequence.
