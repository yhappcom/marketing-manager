# Research 274 — Sparse-Niche Launch Evidence Minimum

Validated: 2026-09-27

## Decision
A zero-paid-media launch is an evidence gate, not a calendar event. Do not manufacture launch volume. Release when the specialist promise, destination, measurement path, and first-value experience are coherent enough that scarce niche traffic can teach us something.

## FT0–FT8 launch gate
1. FT0 Problem truth — at least one normalized specialist job is independently evidenced; echoes/reposts do not count as independent demand.
2. FT1 Promise truth — audience-native wording states the job/outcome without unsupported superiority, urgency, or breadth.
3. FT2 Capability truth — the shipping build actually performs the promised job.
4. FT3 Destination continuity — acquisition promise maps to the relevant Store page and then to a matching in-app destination/onboarding path.
5. FT4 Store readiness — default listing is truthful and complete; intent-specific pages are used only for materially distinct validated intents.
6. FT5 Measurement readiness — source/campaign routing can distinguish meaningful acquisition sources and connect them, where privacy permits, to first core value and repeated value. Vanity reach is insufficient.
7. FT6 Native-channel permission — community participation follows current community rules and affiliation/link requirements; launch does not justify mass cross-posting.
8. FT7 First-value integrity — launch traffic is not sent into broken import, accounting, onboarding, sync, or other critical workflows. Critical defects override launch-date pressure.
9. FT8 Learning/reallocation rule — after launch, KEEP/REDUCE/STOP decisions use independent evidence, qualified first value, repeated value, maintenance burden, and policy risk—not raw impressions/install counts.

## Evidence classes
Keep demand recurrence/independence, source diversity, language stability, capability fit, Store/message continuity, measurement coverage, first/repeated core value, operational burden, and permission/policy risk separate rather than collapsing them into one arbitrary score.

No universal minimum follower, preorder, install, review, or community-count threshold is canonical for sparse professional apps. A numerical threshold is valid only when tied to a specific decision and observable traffic.

## Pre-order is optional
Apple currently allows an unreleased app to be offered for pre-order in eligible territories, with first-release dates 2–180 days ahead. Pre-order users are notified and the app can automatically download at release. App Analytics can measure pre-orders and later associated downloads/sales. This is useful when qualified pre-release demand already exists; it is not evidence that demand exists.

## Attribution before amplification
Apple App Store Connect acquisition analytics exposes unique impressions, unique product-page views, downloads, conversion rate, and source types such as App Store search/browse, web referrer and app referrer. Campaign links can attribute product-page views, downloads, usage, sales and subscriptions to a campaign token. Zero-cost launch links should therefore be distinguishable before distribution; otherwise scarce traffic is consumed without reusable channel evidence.

## Sparse-niche launch sequence
validated job → truthful shipping capability → default Store page → instrumented source link/routing → one or few permission-verified native communities/owned surfaces → first-value observation → repeated-value observation → only then broaden distribution

Do not open every available channel on launch day. Sequential release preserves causal information and operator capacity.

## MintTap application
Launch/growth bursts should organize around business-level YieldMax jobs such as distribution/ROC interpretation and portfolio continuity, not ticker-count amplification.

## LogMate application
Pilot acquisition should center on real logbook jobs such as import/migration, Previous Total continuity, duplicate reconciliation, and record/export integrity. Critical record-integrity/import defects block amplification even if Store assets are ready.

## Reusable launch contract
A future niche app may declare LAUNCH-READY only when FT0–FT7 pass. FT8 begins immediately after traffic arrives. If demand is weak but capability is ready, launch can still occur as a controlled learning release; it must not be reported internally as validated demand.
