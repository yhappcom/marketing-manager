# Research 285 — Community Trust Before Distribution Contract

Validated: 2026-09-28
Scope: zero-cost Reddit/community marketing for MintTap, LogMate, and future specialist apps.

## Decision
Community marketing is not a free distribution channel. It is a permissioned trust surface. The unit of work is a useful contribution that would still deserve to exist if it produced zero installs.

Reddit's current spam guidance defines spam around repeated or unsolicited behavior that harms users/communities, and explicitly says promotional content is not inherently spam. However, individual communities may prohibit promotion entirely; some use a 10% self-promotion convention, and moderators retain final authority. Reddit also warns against repeated cross-community content, link farming, bulk unsolicited messages, and accounts whose activity is mainly links to a business they benefit from.

Therefore a portfolio-wide numeric “10% rule” is not a safe operating permission. Community-specific rules and moderator judgment are the binding constraint.

## GL0–GL8
1. **GL0 Community fit** — participate only where the app's specialist problem is genuinely on-topic.
2. **GL1 Rule audit** — read current subreddit/community rules before each promotional or link-bearing format; do not infer permission from Reddit-wide policy.
3. **GL2 Native-value test** — the post/comment must solve, explain, compare, document, or clarify something useful without requiring an app click.
4. **GL3 Interest disclosure** — when the product is relevant, disclose the creator/operator relationship plainly; do not simulate independent advocacy.
5. **GL4 Link necessity** — add a product/site link only when it materially helps the reader complete the job. Prefer a native answer when the link adds no value.
6. **GL5 Repetition control** — do not mass-repost substantially similar product content across threads/communities and do not use unsolicited bulk chat/DM outreach.
7. **GL6 Trust ledger** — track community, rule state, contribution type, problem language learned, moderator/user response, removal reason, and whether a link was actually necessary. Do not optimize for karma alone.
8. **GL7 Distribution gate** — expand only when useful non-promotional participation and community response show that the contribution format is accepted. A post surviving moderation is not proof of product-market fit.
9. **GL8 Re-entry trigger** — after removal/rejection, stop repeating the format; re-enter only after rules, moderator guidance, product relevance, or contribution format materially changes.

## Important correction: 10% is not the strategy
Reddit Help currently notes that some communities use a 10% self-promotion rule, while others prohibit promotional content. This is descriptive community guidance, not a platform-wide entitlement to make every tenth action promotional.

Operationally:
community rules/mod judgment > native usefulness > disclosure > link necessity > frequency.

A marketer who mechanically stays below 10% can still create spam through repetitive, irrelevant, unsolicited, or cross-community promotion.

## MintTap application
MintTap's highest-information community work is not “announce MintTap repeatedly.” It is to contribute to real YieldMax investor problems: distribution interpretation, ROC/tax-adjustment concepts, reverse-split/accounting edge cases, reinvestment/return interpretation, and transparent methodology.

When a MintTap feature directly solves the discussed job and the community permits it, disclose ownership and link only as a completion aid. Maintain r/MintTapforYieldMax as an owned support/reference surface, but do not use participation in unrelated communities merely to funnel users into it.

Decision evidence to collect:
- recurring investor language and misunderstood calculations;
- which explanations generate substantive follow-up questions;
- which owned reference assets are repeatedly useful;
- whether product references are welcomed, ignored, removed, or challenged;
- Store/product promise gaps revealed by discussion.

## LogMate application
Pilot communities have higher professional-context sensitivity. Lead with workflow evidence: logbook migration/import, duplicate handling, previous-total continuity, device/PWA workflow, export integrity, and jurisdiction-specific recordkeeping only where validated.

Do not impersonate pilot consensus, manufacture testimonials, or repeatedly seed product links. A credible technical answer without a LogMate link is still successful marketing if it builds domain trust or reveals product requirements.

## Reusable zero-cost operating loop
listen → classify specialist problem → answer natively → capture language/evidence → build durable owned reference if recurring → disclose/link only when permitted and useful → observe trust/product evidence → update Store/product promise.

Community evidence should feed ASO and product work, not exist as a separate “social content calendar.”

## Minimum ledger
community | current rule checked date | specialist problem | native contribution | ownership disclosed? | link used? | link necessity | response quality | removal/mod signal | language learned | product/Store implication | next allowed action

## Stop rules
Stop or hold when:
- current community rules prohibit the format;
- substantially similar posts are being repeated;
- contribution requires a click to have value;
- product mention is doing more work than the answer;
- removals/moderator objections repeat;
- the same unresolved product weakness keeps surfacing;
- operator time is producing impressions/karma but no new specialist evidence or durable asset.
