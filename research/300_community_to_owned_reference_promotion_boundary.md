# Research 300 — Community-to-Owned Reference Promotion Boundary

Validated: 2026-09-29

## Why this is new
Research 299 established durable owned references. This addition defines when those references may be introduced into external communities without turning useful expertise into repetitive self-promotion.

## Authoritative evidence
Reddit's current spam policy (updated 2026-05-19) prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement, including mass-posting repetitive content for exposure or financial gain. It advises authentic participation and warns that business-link-heavy contribution frequency matters; individual community rules and moderator judgment remain controlling.

Reddit's current moderator guidance (updated 2026-03-28) states that promotional content is not inherently spam, but communities may prohibit it or use their own conventions. The commonly cited 10% rule is therefore not a sitewide safe harbor or entitlement.

## HB0–HB9 Community Reference Boundary
1. PROBLEM — Start from a real question/problem already present in the community.
2. NATIVE ANSWER — Answer sufficiently in-platform; the external link cannot be required to understand the answer.
3. RULE CHECK — Re-check current community rules before every promotional/reference use.
4. DISCLOSURE — Disclose material affiliation when the referenced asset/app is owned or benefits the operator.
5. LINK NECESSITY — Link only when the owned reference adds material depth, evidence, methodology, calculator/data, or maintained detail that is impractical to reproduce natively.
6. REPETITION CONTROL — Do not paste the same or near-identical link/comment across threads or communities; repeated distribution is a risk signal even when each destination is useful.
7. NO FORCED FUNNEL — Do not require install/signup/email capture to access the evidence needed to answer the community question.
8. VALUE LEDGER — Record problem, native contribution, rule state, disclosure, link/no-link decision, qualitative response, product/reference insight, and any downstream specialist value.
9. RE-ENTRY TRIGGER — Re-enter only for a materially new question, corrected evidence, changed methodology/product state, or explicit request—not because a publishing calendar says to post.
10. DECISION — KEEP / NATIVE-ONLY / LINK-PERMITTED / REPAIR / RETIRE.

## Operating implications
- Owned references are support infrastructure for community participation, not a syndication inventory.
- A reference can be strategically valuable even if it is rarely linked; it improves answer quality, internal consistency, support, Store claims, and future retrieval.
- Community posts should not be rewritten into teaser copy whose sole purpose is to drive clicks.
- Never treat a numeric self-promotion ratio as permission when local rules or moderator norms are stricter.
- If the native answer is complete and the link adds no material evidence, omit the link.
- If a community forbids promotion, use the research internally to improve the product/reference; do not route around the restriction.

## MintTap application
Use recurring YieldMax problems—ROC state/provenance, ticker/date reconstruction, split/reinvestment reconstruction, total-return methodology—as the unit of contribution. A MintTap reference may be linked only when it materially adds maintained evidence/methodology beyond the native Reddit answer. Ticker variants alone do not justify repetitive posts.

## LogMate application
For pilot communities, start with professional workflow questions such as migration/import continuity, duplicate reconciliation, Previous Total, export integrity, and offline/PWA boundaries. Product links are subordinate to community rules and a complete native answer. Professional credibility is more valuable than short-term referral volume.

## Measurement
Do not rank community activity by posts, karma, clicks, or installs alone. Track whether the contribution generated: recurring-problem evidence, corrected terminology, product defect/requirement evidence, reference improvements, qualified Store routing, first specialist value, or repeated specialist value. Mark unavailable downstream joins UNKNOWN rather than substituting clicks.

## Invalidation triggers
Revalidate this contract when Reddit sitewide spam policy materially changes, target-community rules change, an account receives moderation/enforcement feedback, or the owned reference becomes stale under the Claim Registry.
