# Research 313 — Community Cold-Start Reputation & Deliverability Contract

Validated: 2026-09-29

## Why this adds new knowledge

Research 285/300 established permissioned, native-first community participation and anti-spam boundaries. This module addresses a separate operational failure mode: a contribution can be relevant and policy-compliant yet still have weak deliverability because communities can filter accounts using reputation/quality signals. Therefore community launch planning must distinguish **permission** from **deliverability**.

## Current authoritative evidence

Reddit Help (2026):
- Reddit's sitewide spam rule prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement, including repetitive mass posting for exposure or financial gain.
- Reddit advises authentic participation in communities where the account has a genuine interest; business-link-heavy participation should be frequency-conscious and community rules/moderator judgment control what is unwanted.
- Promotional content is not inherently spam, but individual communities may prohibit it or adopt their own conventions.
- Reddit's Reputation Filter is an optional moderator safety control informed by Contributor Quality Score. Reddit states that this score uses karma, verification, and other account signals to classify behavior and can filter content from accounts that may be potential spammers, likely to have content removed, or are unestablished.
- Community rules explicitly set local expectations and report reasons.

## Core finding

**Permission != deliverability != trust.**

A compliant post is not guaranteed distribution. Conversely, attempting to manufacture account history, karma, engagement, or cross-community exposure to defeat filtering is not an acceptable growth tactic. The correct cold-start strategy is genuine specialist participation whose value remains positive even when no link or install occurs.

For sparse professional audiences, account reputation is therefore an operating constraint, not a KPI to game.

## HO0–HO9 — Cold-start community contract

HO0 — Identity and interest legitimacy  
Use an account that can participate authentically in the subject. Do not create a persona solely to simulate independent enthusiasm.

HO1 — Local rule gate  
Read current community rules before participation. Record explicit promotion/link/disclosure requirements and moderator-specific conventions.

HO2 — Native-value baseline  
First contributions must stand alone as useful specialist answers/discussion. They must not depend on an app link to make sense.

HO3 — No reputation manufacturing  
Do not farm karma, coordinate votes, mass-comment, recycle content across communities, or create artificial engagement to accelerate account standing.

HO4 — Deliverability observation  
Track whether a contribution is visible/retained and whether moderation/removal occurs. Do not infer suppression from low engagement alone.

HO5 — Moderator clarification gate  
When a material rule or removal reason is ambiguous and future participation depends on it, use the community's permitted clarification route. Do not repeatedly repost around a removal.

HO6 — Link eligibility after value  
A product/owned-reference link is eligible only when current local rules permit it and it adds material depth beyond the native contribution. Account age/karma is not permission.

HO7 — Trust evidence  
Prefer substantive replies, saves/bookmarks where observable, follow-up questions, corrections accepted, recurring recognition, and unresolved-problem evidence over karma totals.

HO8 — Escalation by evidence  
Increase contribution cadence or introduce maintained references only after repeated evidence of topic fit and accepted participation. Never escalate merely because an account crosses a karma threshold.

HO9 — Decision  
KEEP / NATIVE-ONLY / CLARIFY / SLOW / RETIRE / UNKNOWN.

## MintTap application

YieldMax/income-investor communities are a high-fit but promotion-sensitive environment. The cold-start objective is not “build enough karma to post MintTap.” It is to become a useful participant around independently recurring investor problems such as:
- estimated vs final ROC/provenance;
- issuer/broker reporting mismatch;
- split/reinvestment reconstruction;
- total-return/recovery methodology.

A MintTap or owned-reference link appears only when the community permits it and the destination materially extends the answer with maintained evidence/methodology. Ticker-cloned posts and repetitive product mentions are prohibited by this operating contract.

## LogMate application

Pilot communities have stronger professional-credibility requirements. Cold-start participation should center on real workflow knowledge—migration/import, duplicate reconciliation, Previous Totals, export integrity, offline/PWA boundaries—without turning aviation groups into launch-distribution inventory. Professional correctness and useful corrections are stronger trust evidence than raw engagement.

## Reusable launch method for future niche apps

Before launch, create a Community Cold-Start Ledger:

community | audience/problem fit | rules checked date | promotion/link rule | disclosure expectation | account state | native contribution | visible/retained? | moderation outcome | substantive response | link eligible? | operator minutes | next action

This ledger separates:
1. **permission** — is the behavior allowed?
2. **deliverability** — does the contribution survive normal community filtering/moderation?
3. **trust** — does the specialist audience find it useful?
4. **acquisition** — only then, does an appropriate transition to owned/Store/product value occur?

## Measurement guardrails

Do not optimize:
- karma as an acquisition target;
- account age as a proxy for trust;
- post volume;
- cross-post count;
- raw impressions without audience/problem fit.

Measure:
- accepted native contributions;
- substantive specialist interaction;
- recurring problem evidence;
- moderation/removal reasons;
- permitted qualified transitions;
- first/repeated specialist value downstream;
- operator time per useful learning.
