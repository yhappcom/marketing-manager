# Research 314 — Owned Reference Discoverability for AI and Multimodal Search

Date: 2026-09-30
Status: VALIDATED

## New validated findings
Google's 2026 guidance says generative-AI Search visibility continues to depend on ordinary Search fundamentals: crawlability, indexability, useful human-first content, and accurate technical implementation. Google specifically recommends unique, expert-led, non-commodity content rather than summaries of common information.

Google also says special AEO/GEO markup, AI-specific rewrites, forced content chunking, and llms.txt are not required for Google Search. Creating many pages for minor query variations is not a supported strategy. For this portfolio, durable owned references should therefore stay organized around materially different specialist problems rather than ticker, airline, aircraft, or keyword clones.

Search Console's dedicated Generative AI performance reporting completed global rollout on 2026-08-31. On 2026-09-24 Google also introduced web-multimodal reporting for discovery through Lens, Circle to Search, image uploads, and Chrome image search. Relevant diagrams, screenshots, and other explanatory visuals can now be treated as measurable discovery assets when they genuinely improve the reference.

## HP0–HP9
recurring specialist problem → non-commodity evidence → canonical human-first answer → crawl/index/text availability → useful visual evidence → claim/provenance linkage → avoid unsupported AI-search tactics → Search Console observation → downstream specialist value → KEEP/REPAIR/ENRICH/MERGE/RETIRE/UNKNOWN

## MintTap
Prioritize maintained references for estimated-vs-final ROC provenance, split/reinvestment reconstruction, ticker/date reconstruction, and total-return/recovery methodology. Do not clone articles merely by ticker. Add annotated visual examples only when they improve understanding and remain consistent with verified claims.

## LogMate
Prioritize import/migration continuity, duplicate reconciliation, Previous Totals, export/certificate integrity, and offline/PWA/device boundaries. Prefer workflow/data-integrity visuals over generic aviation imagery. Do not multiply pages by airline or aircraft unless the workflow or evidence materially differs.

## Measurement
Track canonical URL, specialist problem, classic Search performance, generative-AI visibility, multimodal visibility, qualified visits, first specialist value, repeated specialist value, and update trigger. Search visibility is evidence of discovery, not the final growth objective.
