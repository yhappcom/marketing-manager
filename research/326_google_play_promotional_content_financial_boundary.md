# Research 326 — Google Play Promotional-Content Eligibility & Financial-App Boundary

Validated: 2026-09-30

## Decision problem
When should a niche non-game app use Google Play Promotional content as a zero-cost acquisition/re-engagement surface, and when should it deliberately not use it?

## Newly validated platform facts
Google Play Promotional content is for fresh/timely offers, limited-time events, and major updates. For eligible apps it can appear on the store listing and, subject to quality/featuring eligibility, across additional Play surfaces including search and high-visibility discovery surfaces. Deep links can route users to the precise in-app destination.

Google requires promotional content to be new, noteworthy, user-facing, available in-app during the scheduled period, easy to find after opening the app, internally consistent, and not evergreen/routine. Google advises submission at least four days before start for review; featuring requests have stricter lead time and limited quarterly allotments.

Critically for MintTap, Google currently says Promotional content is not allowed for certain financial products/services and explicitly lists themes including investment/financial advice, investment resources/management, stocks, funds, shares, and financial utility services. This is not a wording loophole: do not try to evade the restriction by euphemistic event copy.

## IB0–IB9 contract
IB0 product-event reality — A Store promotion must correspond to a real, user-facing product moment.
IB1 tool eligibility — Confirm the app/account has access to Promotional content/Premium growth tooling before planning around it.
IB2 category/restriction gate — Check current restricted-content guidance before asset production. A restricted theme is a STOP, not a copy-edit task.
IB3 freshness/materiality — Routine recurring data, evergreen capabilities, ordinary reminders, and generic awareness are not event inventory.
IB4 destination continuity — The card promise must resolve to a relevant, functioning in-app destination; use deep linking where eligible.
IB5 discoverability-in-app — The promoted item must be readily findable after app open during its scheduled period.
IB6 asset/claim consistency — Tagline, description, imagery, timing, eligibility and actual product behavior must agree.
IB7 featuring scarcity — Treat featuring requests as scarce options, not a publishing quota. Spend them only on high-value product moments.
IB8 downstream-value measurement — Separate Store exposure/open/reinstall/acquisition from first specialist value and repeated specialist value.
IB9 decision — ELIGIBLE-RUN / LISTING-ONLY / HOLD / REPAIR / RESTRICTED-DO-NOT-SUBMIT / UNKNOWN.

## MintTap
Default decision: RESTRICTED-DO-NOT-SUBMIT for Promotional content tied to YieldMax investing, ETFs/funds/shares, investment tracking/analysis, or financial-utility themes under the current Google guidance.

Do not manufacture a “major update” or rename an investment event to bypass the restriction. Continue to use compliant default/Custom Store Listings, owned references, native community participation, release notes, and product-quality improvements as the primary zero-cost surfaces.

Re-check the official restriction before any future Promotional-content plan because platform eligibility can change.

## LogMate
LogMate is not inherently caught by the cited financial restriction. Promotional content may become useful after launch for a genuinely major, user-facing update—for example, a materially new import/migration capability or other bounded workflow improvement—provided the current eligibility and quality gates are met.

Do not promote routine flight logging, recurring roster cycles, generic reminders, or ordinary maintenance releases as events. The event must be discoverable in-app and its Store promise must resolve to the relevant workflow.

## Reusable operating ledger
app | product moment | event type | access eligible? | restricted-theme check | freshness/materiality | user-facing? | in-app discoverability | deep-link destination | asset/claim consistency | featuring requested? | Store outcome | first specialist value | repeated specialist value | operator minutes | decision

## Operational consequence
Google Play and Apple event-like surfaces are not interchangeable. Maintain platform-specific eligibility/restriction gates before reusing an event concept cross-store. For regulated or sensitive niche apps, platform eligibility must precede creative production.
