# Research 330 — Marginal Ad Pressure & Specialist-Workflow Protection Contract

Validated: 2026-09-30

## Core finding
For a niche utility, maximize sustainable revenue rather than impressions. Evaluate each additional ad exposure as:

incremental verified ad revenue − specialist-value loss − retention/trust loss − latency/layout cost − policy/traffic-quality risk − operating cost.

Google Play prohibits unexpected full-screen interstitials. AdMob guidance places interstitials at natural transitions, supports frequency caps for interstitial/rewarded/app-open inventory, and recommends delaying the first app-open ad until users have used the app a few times and showing it while they are already waiting. Impression-level ad revenue provides per-impression value plus currency and precision; precision can be UNKNOWN, ESTIMATED, PUBLISHER_PROVIDED, or PRECISE, so revenue observations do not all have identical evidentiary quality.

## Application
**MintTap:** protect transaction entry/editing, split/reinvestment reconstruction, distribution/ROC interpretation, tax adjustment, and portfolio/recovery analysis. Audit current production inventory and ILAR before increasing pressure. Compare revenue against activated/repeated-value cohorts, not eCPM alone.

**LogMate:** keep Home ad-free. Protect Add Flight, Previous Totals, import/migration, duplicate reconciliation, backup/restore, export/certificate, and record-integrity workflows. Hold app-open/full-screen launch inventory; evaluate secondary/non-critical surfaces only after first specialist value is established.

## Failure modes
Do not optimize eCPM alone; do not show an ad merely because it is loaded; do not treat estimated and precise ILAR equally; do not test pressure before first value; do not crowd core evidence with banners; do not declare a revenue winner while product-harm evidence is underpowered.
