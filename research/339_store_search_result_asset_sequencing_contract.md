# Research 339 — Store Search-Result Asset Sequencing Contract

Validated: 2026-10-01

## Decision
For sparse specialist apps, Store creative optimization begins with the assets that can be consumed before a user reaches the full product page. Treat search-result-visible media as a scarce evidence surface, not as a miniature brand campaign.

## Authoritative platform findings
Apple currently permits 1–10 screenshots per product page. Depending on orientation, up to three screenshots can appear in search results. App previews precede screenshots on the product page, can autoplay in search results, and Apple permits up to three previews per supported device size/language. Apple explicitly recommends that screenshots show the app in use, lead with the strongest features/benefits, and tell a cohesive story. It also advises a single clear idea for header/search assets and says unverifiable claims should not be included.

Apple Custom Product Pages (CPPs) can vary screenshots, promotional text and previews; assigned keywords should match the intent of the CPP. Thus the search-result asset sequence must be evaluated per destination/intent rather than assumed to be one global creative sequence.

## IO0–IO9
IO0 search-intent evidence → IO1 pre-click asset inventory → IO2 first-visible proof job → IO3 claim/evidence gate → IO4 sequence coherence → IO5 real-UI legibility → IO6 localization parity → IO7 default-vs-CPP routing → IO8 downstream specialist-value measurement → IO9 KEEP / REORDER / REPLACE-PROOF / ROUTE-TO-CPP / HOLD / RETIRE / UNKNOWN.

## Operating rules
1. Optimize the first search-visible evidence before polishing late screenshots that many users may never inspect.
2. One frame = one primary specialist promise. Avoid dense feature collages.
3. Use actual product UI as proof; decorative branding cannot substitute for workflow evidence.
4. Sequence by decision value, not by internal feature taxonomy.
5. Do not infer that a screenshot is effective from installs alone. Connect destination-level Store evidence, where available, to first and repeated specialist value.
6. Sparse traffic is not a license for cosmetic micro-testing. Reorder only when the hypothesis is materially different and measurable.
7. Localization includes the visual claim, not merely translated Store text.
8. CPP search routing creates separate intent-specific sequences; do not copy the default sequence mechanically.

## MintTap application
Candidate first-visible proof jobs are recurring specialist problems: distribution/ROC provenance/state, split/reinvestment reconstruction, and portfolio recovery/total-return understanding. Do not lead with ticker-count breadth or generic finance imagery when the acquisition intent is a reconstruction/provenance problem. Any tax/return language remains Claim-Registry gated.

## LogMate application
Pre-launch creative should prove professional workflow value: fast multi-leg logging, import/migration continuity, duplicate/Previous Total continuity, or export/backup integrity. Generic aviation lifestyle imagery is secondary to actual workflow evidence. Do not create airline/aircraft/jurisdiction variants without evidence of distinct intent.

## Reusable niche-app rule
The scarce unit is not screenshot count; it is user attention before commitment. Allocate the earliest visible assets to the smallest set of truthful, high-decision-value proofs.
