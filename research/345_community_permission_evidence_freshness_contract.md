## Authoritative/current evidence

- Reddit Help, “Spam”, updated 2026-05-19: repeated or unsolicited mass engagement is prohibited; mass-posting repetitive content for exposure or financial gain can violate policy. Reddit recommends authentic participation and checking community-specific rules.
- Reddit Help, “How do I keep spam out of my community?”, updated 2026-03-28: promotional content is not inherently spam sitewide, but communities may prohibit it or use their own conventions; moderators decide what works for their community.
- r/dividends moderator/AutoModerator removals observed 2026-02-21 and 2026-05-03 explicitly state that Rule 2 prohibits promotion of web content, products, services, companies, or anything personally owned, including non-monetized material, and directs users to modmail for clarification.
- The older detailed Rule 2 explanation is therefore corroborative history rather than the sole basis for the current decision.

## Key operating conclusion

Permission evidence has two independent properties: authority and freshness. A historically authoritative rule can become stale; a current enforcement artifact can refresh the operational state even when a clean rules page is not retrievable.

Use evidence classes:
- A1 CURRENT-RULE: current first-party community rules/mod announcement.
- A2 CURRENT-ENFORCEMENT: current moderator/AutoModerator removal or enforcement language that identifies the applicable rule.
- B PLATFORM: current Reddit-wide policy/guidance.
- C HISTORICAL-COMMUNITY: older community rule/mod evidence.
- U UNVERIFIED: no adequate current evidence.

A2 is sufficient for a conservative restriction decision when the enforcement text is explicit, but not for inferring permissions that the artifact does not mention.

## MintTap decision: r/dividends

Current state: PROMOTION HOLD.

Evidence: A2 + corroborating C + B.

Do not:
- post MintTap/App Store/Play Store links;
- post MintTap-owned web references as self-promotion;
- treat non-monetized educational material as exempt;

Allowed state is not inferred for native product mentions merely because no link is present. If a MintTap-affiliated response is operationally necessary, use modmail first.

The community remains valuable as a problem-intelligence surface for dividend/ROC/broker-reporting language and recurring questions, subject to normal Reddit participation rules.

## Freshness/expiry contract

Every permission record carries:
community | contribution class | decision | authority grade | evidence date | evidence URL/reference | expiry trigger | recheck date/status.

Revalidate when:
1. a rule/mod announcement changes;
2. current enforcement contradicts the recorded state;
3. moderators provide direct clarification;
4. a new contribution class is proposed;
5. the prior evidence becomes materially old for an active distribution plan.

Do not convert silence, non-removal, or another user's surviving promotional post into permission evidence.

## Reusable rule

Community research and community distribution are separate systems. Restrictive current enforcement can close a distribution path while leaving the community highly valuable for zero-cost language/problem research.
