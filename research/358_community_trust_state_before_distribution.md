# Research 358 — Community Trust-State Before Distribution

Validated: 2026-10-01

## Decision

For zero-cost niche-app community marketing, rule compliance is necessary but not sufficient. A contribution can be truthful, useful, and permitted yet still fail distribution because the account has not established enough community trust. Treat community trust-state as an operational eligibility layer before any promotional or owned-reference distribution.

## Current authoritative evidence

Reddit's current moderator guidance says promotional content is not inherently spam, but individual communities may prohibit it or apply their own promotion rules. Moderator judgment remains decisive.

Reddit also exposes two moderation systems that matter to legitimate new contributors:

- Reputation Filter can filter content from redditors who may be potential spammers based on sitewide signals.
- Crowd Control can collapse or filter posts/comments from redditors who have not yet established trust within a community.

Reddit's current Spam rule separately prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement, mass repetitive posting for exposure or financial gain, and large volumes of unsolicited chat/private messages. Reddit advises authentic participation in communities where the account has a genuine interest and checking community-specific rules.

Therefore, “the rules allow links” does not imply “a new app-affiliated account should post a link now.”

## HC0–HC9 — Community trust-state gate

HC0 — Problem fit  
Is there a real specialist problem already being discussed?

HC1 — Current rule fit  
Read the current community rules and post requirements before contributing.

HC2 — Account/community trust-state  
Classify the account as UNKNOWN / NEW / ESTABLISHING / ESTABLISHED for that specific community. Do not infer community trust from account age or success elsewhere.

HC3 — Native contribution test  
Can the user receive a complete useful answer without leaving Reddit? If not, improve the native answer first.

HC4 — Affiliation disclosure  
Where material, disclose the relationship to the app/project plainly. Disclosure does not create permission to promote.

HC5 — Link necessity  
Add an owned-reference link only when it contributes evidence, methodology, maintained data, or depth that materially exceeds the native answer.

HC6 — Distribution restraint  
No cross-community copy/paste campaign, unsolicited DMs, coordinated voting, or repeated reposting to manufacture exposure.

HC7 — Visibility diagnosis  
If a contribution gets little/no distribution, do not immediately repost. Separate low demand, moderator removal, automated filtering, account trust-state, and weak contribution quality.

HC8 — Trust ledger  
Record community, rule snapshot/date, contribution type, disclosure, link/no-link, moderation outcome, visible discussion quality, recurring problem evidence, and any product/reference insight. Do not optimize for karma alone.

HC9 — Decision  
NATIVE-FIRST / LINK-ELIGIBLE / ESTABLISH-TRUST / REPAIR / STOP / UNKNOWN.

## Important operational consequence

The first objective in a new specialist community is not referral traffic. It is to establish a history of useful, topic-native participation and learn the community's actual norms. This is especially important for brand/project-affiliated accounts because legitimate contributions can still encounter automated or moderator filtering when community trust is not established.

A filtered or low-reach post is not evidence that the topic has no demand. It is censored evidence until the distribution state is known.

## MintTap application

For external YieldMax/income-investor communities:

1. Enter through independently recurring problems such as ROC provenance, broker/substitute-payment mismatch, split/reinvestment reconstruction, or return methodology.
2. Answer natively first.
3. Do not use ticker variants as a reason to repeat substantially identical posts across communities.
4. Keep the MintTap link out until both community permission and account/community trust-state are adequate.
5. If an owned reference is eventually linked, it must add maintained evidence or methodology beyond the Reddit answer.
6. Measure useful discussion, recurring-question confirmation, corrections, terminology, and product/reference defects before referral volume.

The owned r/MintTapforYieldMax community should not be used to coordinate voting, commenting, or interference in external communities.

## LogMate application

For pilot communities, establish credibility around professional workflow questions—migration/import fidelity, duplicate reconciliation, Previous Total continuity, export integrity, and offline/PWA/device boundaries—before launch promotion. A pilot community's trust threshold is likely to be higher than a generic consumer-app community because incorrect workflow claims carry professional cost.

## Reusable niche-app rule

Community marketing sequence:

problem evidence → rule eligibility → trust-state → native answer → disclosure → optional evidence link → discussion learning → product/reference repair → measured re-entry

Never:

app exists → find many communities → paste launch/link post → interpret removals or silence as market evidence

## Measurement

Primary learning metrics:
- independently recurring specialist problems
- substantive replies/corrections
- moderator acceptance/removal
- contribution visibility state
- reusable language/evidence gained
- owned-reference defects found
- product/support defects found
- qualified downstream visits only after link eligibility

Karma, impressions, referral clicks, and installs are secondary diagnostics, not the objective.

## Sources

- Reddit Help, “Spam” (updated 2026-05-19): repeated/unsolicited mass engagement, repetitive posting for exposure/financial gain, and unsolicited bulk messages are prohibited; authentic participation and community-specific rule checks are advised.
- Reddit Help, “Disrupting Communities” (updated 2026-05-19): vote manipulation and coordinated interference are prohibited.
- Reddit Help, Moderator Code of Conduct Rule 3 (updated 2026-03-05): communities must not direct or coordinate interference in other communities.
