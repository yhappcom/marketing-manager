# Research 365 — Regulated-Category Discovery Eligibility Before Channel Tactics

Validated: 2026-10-02

## Why this matters

A niche app can be perfectly eligible for ordinary Store distribution while being ineligible for a particular discovery surface. Marketing planning must therefore distinguish **app eligibility**, **Store-listing eligibility**, and **optional growth-surface eligibility**.

This is material for MintTap because Google Play's Promotional content surface currently restricts certain finance/investing themes even though those apps may otherwise be distributed on Google Play.

## Authoritative findings

### Google Play Promotional content is a separate growth surface

Google describes Promotional content as a self-service Play growth tool for fresh, timely items such as major updates, offers and limited-time events. It can surface in places including Apps/Games tabs, search results and the Store listing. It is available to games and apps meeting Premium growth-tool eligibility.

Google's current content-quality guidance also says Promotional content must be new, noteworthy and user-facing; evergreen service descriptions and routine events are not valid submissions.

### Finance restriction materially changes MintTap channel planning

Google currently states that Promotional content is **not allowed for certain financial products/services** and explicitly names investing/equity-related tools and terms including investment/financial advice, investment resources, investment management, stocks, funds, shares and financial utility services.

MintTap is a YieldMax ETF portfolio/distribution tracker. Its subject matter therefore overlaps the expressly restricted finance vocabulary. Do not treat Google Play Promotional content as an assumed MintTap acquisition/reactivation channel. The exact product classification in Play Console remains an evidence item; do not invent an eligibility determination beyond Google's stated restriction.

This restriction is about the **Promotional content feature**, not a blanket statement that finance apps cannot have ordinary Play Store listings. Keep those policy surfaces separate.

### Store metadata remains a truthful-product contract

Apple's current App Review Guidelines require complete and accurate metadata and prohibit irrelevant terms, unverifiable product claims, misleading names/metadata and Store-experience manipulation. This reinforces the existing Claim Registry: regulated-category constraints do not justify euphemistic or misleading Store copy intended to bypass a growth-surface rule.

Google's Promotional content rules likewise require text to comply with Store-listing/promotion policies and require the promoted experience to exist in-app at the scheduled time.

## New framework — IB0–IB9: channel-eligibility firewall

IB0 product/category classification
→ IB1 jurisdiction/policy scope
→ IB2 ordinary Store-distribution eligibility
→ IB3 optional discovery-surface eligibility
→ IB4 content/term restriction
→ IB5 claim-registry consistency
→ IB6 in-product truth/availability
→ IB7 measurable surface-specific outcome
→ IB8 downstream first/repeated specialist value
→ IB9 USE / ROUTE-ELSEWHERE / HOLD-CLASSIFICATION / STOP.

### Decision rules

1. Never infer optional growth-surface eligibility from Store availability.
2. Never rewrite a finance/investing claim into vague wording merely to evade a surface restriction.
3. If a surface is restricted, route effort to eligible zero-cost surfaces rather than repeatedly resubmitting variants.
4. Keep policy evidence surface-specific: ordinary Store listing, custom listing/page, Promotional content, community, owned web and social are different contracts.
5. A channel unavailable to one app is not automatically unavailable to another. LogMate's aviation niche must be evaluated independently rather than inheriting MintTap's finance restrictions.
6. Channel portfolio planning should include an explicit **ineligible/not worth pursuing** state; zero-cost does not mean zero opportunity cost.

## MintTap operational consequence

Do not allocate launch/update effort to Google Play Promotional content until current Play Console eligibility and finance-category treatment are verified. Default state: **HOLD-CLASSIFICATION / likely ROUTE-ELSEWHERE for investing-themed Promotional content**.

Prefer eligible surfaces already supported by the operating system:
- truthful ordinary Store listing and intent routing;
- evidence-backed owned references;
- permissioned/native community participation;
- review/support repair loops;
- measurable social distribution where a real specialist problem is being answered.

Do not substitute ticker/update spam for the unavailable Play surface.

## LogMate operational consequence

Do not generalize MintTap's finance restriction to LogMate. Apply IB0–IB9 independently. If LogMate later qualifies for Google Play Premium growth tools, a genuinely major, user-facing release may be evaluated for Promotional content, but routine releases and evergreen product descriptions remain poor/invalid candidates under Google's quality rules.

## Reusable niche-app lesson

Before building a launch calendar around any platform feature, establish a **channel eligibility matrix**:
app × jurisdiction × surface × category restriction × content restriction × evidence owner × current status × recheck trigger.

This prevents teams from optimizing creative for a surface the product cannot use and from confusing Store distribution with editorial/discovery eligibility.
