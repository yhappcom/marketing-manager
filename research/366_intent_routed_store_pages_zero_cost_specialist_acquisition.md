# Research 366 — Intent-Routed Store Pages as Zero-Cost Specialist Acquisition

Validated: 2026-10-02

## Why this matters
Sparse-niche apps should not treat the default Store page as the only organic acquisition surface. Apple now permits Custom Product Pages (CPPs) to be associated with search keywords, so a specialist app can route materially different search intents to materially different truthful product-page narratives without buying traffic.

## Authoritative findings
Apple currently allows up to 70 CPPs per app. A CPP can vary screenshots, app previews and promotional text, can be shared by unique URL, and can be assigned search keywords. When an approved/visible CPP is assigned a keyword, customers searching that keyword can see that CPP instead of the default product page. Apple says keyword combinations should be unique to one CPP and match that page's intent.

CPPs can also carry an app deep link. On iOS/iPadOS 18+, opening from that CPP can take the user to a specific in-app destination. The deep link must be reviewed and should be tested; Apple recommends universal links and avoiding unnecessary redirect/shortener layers.

CPP measurement is page-specific in App Analytics: impressions, downloads and conversion rate, plus downstream retention and proceeds comparisons. Page metrics appear only after at least five first-time downloads. This means low-volume niche traffic must not be fragmented into many pages merely because 70 are available.

CPP metadata and deep links require review. Existing approved apps can submit a CPP independently of an app-version update.

## New operating principle
A CPP is not merely a creative variant. It can be an **intent-routing contract**:

search/community intent → truthful specialist promise → intent-specific Store evidence → install → relevant first-value destination → repeated specialist value.

For sparse niches, create a CPP only when the intent represents a materially different specialist job, the product actually satisfies that job, and enough traffic exists to observe a business-material outcome. Cosmetic keyword/screenshot variants remain ineligible.

## MintTap
Potential CPP families should be problem-led, not ticker-led: distribution/ROC reconstruction; split + reinvestment continuity; after-tax/recovery workflow. Do not create TSLY/CONY/MSTY/NVDY clones unless observed intent and product workflow materially differ. Finance-category policy eligibility for other Play discovery surfaces remains a separate question.

## LogMate
Potential future intent families are migration/import continuity, fast multi-leg logging, and export/backup/offline continuity. Pre-launch, these remain hypotheses until pilot-language and Store-search evidence exists. Deep-link routing is useful only if the destination is stable and the promised workflow is immediately available.

## Reusable rule
For future niche apps, optimize the number of **meaningfully different jobs served**, not the number of Store pages published. One default page plus one well-supported intent route can outperform a large fragmented inventory because sparse traffic preserves learning power.
