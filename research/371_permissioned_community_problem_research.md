# Research 371 — Community Marketing as Permissioned Problem Research

Validated: 2026-10-02

## Why this extends the system

The repository already treats Reddit as a permissioned trust surface (Research 285/300) and recognizes visibility censoring (359). The missing operational layer is how to learn from a specialist community without turning research into disguised promotion or mistaking posting volume for progress.

## Authoritative findings

Reddit's current spam policy prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement, including mass-posting repetitive content for exposure or financial gain. Reddit advises authentic participation in communities of genuine interest and says users whose contributions mainly link to a business they benefit from should be thoughtful about frequency and check each community's rules.

Reddit's current moderator guidance separately states that promotional content is not inherently spam, but communities may prohibit it or apply their own conventions. Moderator judgment and community-specific rules therefore outrank any numeric self-promotion heuristic.

This means a zero-cost niche-app program should treat external communities first as problem-language and evidence environments, not as distribution inventory.

## IJ0–IJ9 — Permissioned Problem-Research Loop

IJ0 recurring specialist problem: observe a recurring, independently expressed job/problem.

IJ1 rule state: record current community rules, link/promotion restrictions, disclosure expectations, and whether moderator clarification is needed.

IJ2 native answer: produce a complete useful answer that does not require a product click.

IJ3 affiliation: disclose material product affiliation when relevant; do not simulate independent advocacy.

IJ4 evidence capture: record exact recurring concepts, objections, terminology, workflow constraints, and unresolved factual questions without collecting unnecessary personal data.

IJ5 repetition gate: do not repost the same answer/link across threads or communities merely for exposure.

IJ6 owned-reference gate: create or update an owned reference only when the recurring problem warrants a durable, evidence-maintained answer.

IJ7 product-feedback gate: route recurring workflow gaps to product/Claim Registry review rather than rewriting marketing claims around missing capability.

IJ8 downstream value: when measurable, connect permitted community/referral activity to first specialist value and repeated specialist value; attribution is not incrementality.

IJ9 decision: ANSWER-NATIVE / RESEARCH / UPDATE-REFERENCE / PRODUCT-HANDOFF / LINK-PERMITTED / HOLD / STOP.

## MintTap

The useful unit is a recurring investor problem, not a ticker or posting opportunity. Candidate research objects already supported by the repository include ROC provenance/state, broker/substitute-payment mismatch, ticker/date reconstruction, split/reinvestment continuity, and total-return methodology.

Do not mass-seed MintTap links into YieldMax/income communities. A high-quality native answer that produces no install can still be successful if it validates terminology, exposes a product gap, improves an owned reference, or identifies an authoritative evidence need.

MintTap's own subreddit may support maintained references and support loops, but ownership does not make repetitive promotional posting a useful growth metric.

## LogMate

Before launch, pilot communities should be used to validate professional jobs and terminology around import/migration, duplicate handling, Previous Total continuity, multi-leg entry, export integrity, offline/PWA/device boundaries, and backup/recovery. Do not manufacture launch buzz with repeated product links.

Professional credibility is the scarce asset: a community answer should remain useful if the LogMate name and link are removed.

## Reusable operating ledger

Minimum fields:
- community / thread context
- observed date
- current rule state and source
- recurring specialist problem
- native-answer completeness
- material affiliation disclosed? 
- external link necessary/permitted?
- repeated-problem evidence count
- terminology/objection/workflow evidence
- authoritative evidence gap
- owned-reference action
- product/Claim Registry handoff
- visible/removed/filtered/unknown
- downstream first/repeated specialist value if observable
- decision and re-entry trigger

## Decision rules

1. No content calendar quota. Publish because a real problem warrants an answer.
2. No cross-community copy-paste distribution.
3. No numeric self-promotion ratio as a safe harbor.
4. No link when the native answer is sufficient.
5. No conversion conclusion when visibility is filtered/unknown.
6. No product claim upgrade from anecdote alone.
7. Repeated independent problem evidence can justify an owned reference or product investigation even with zero installs.
8. Community attribution, when available, is observational unless an incremental design supports a causal claim.
