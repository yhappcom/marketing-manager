# Research 372 — Owned Niche Community Governance Is a Trust Product, Not a Marketing Megaphone

Validated: 2026-10-02

## Decision
When the app operator also runs a niche community, treat community governance as a separate trust product. Ownership creates additional duties; it does not create permission to turn the community into an app-distribution feed.

## Authoritative evidence
Reddit's Spam policy (updated May 19, 2026) prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement and mass-posting repetitive content for exposure or financial gain. Reddit also warns that business-link-heavy participation should be frequency-controlled and community rules checked.

Reddit's Moderator Code of Conduct requires moderators to create and maintain a stable community, set appropriate and reasonable expectations, respect neighboring communities, remain active and engaged, and moderate with integrity. Reddit specifically says communities should have clear expectations/rules; moderators should regularly review queues and modmail; communities must not coordinate interference in other communities; and moderation actions cannot be exchanged for compensation, gifts, favors, or other consideration.

## New operating principle
An operator-owned subreddit must pass a stronger test than external-community participation:

1. Would the community remain useful if the app disappeared tomorrow?
2. Can members discuss competing tools, contrary evidence, and product defects without structural disadvantage?
3. Are app-affiliated posts visibly distinguishable from neutral community information?
4. Are moderation decisions separable from marketing outcomes?
5. Is cross-community growth non-coordinated and permission-respecting?

If the answer is no, the community is functioning as owned promotional inventory rather than a durable specialist community.

## IK0–IK9 — Owned-community governance contract
IK0 specialist-community purpose independent of the app
→ IK1 explicit scope and current rules
→ IK2 operator/app affiliation disclosure
→ IK3 moderation/marketing role separation
→ IK4 competitor and criticism neutrality
→ IK5 native knowledge value independent of app links
→ IK6 anti-spam/repetition and cross-community non-interference
→ IK7 active queue/modmail/community maintenance
→ IK8 trust/product-learning ledger
→ IK9 HEALTHY / REPAIR-GOVERNANCE / HOLD-PROMOTION / STOP.

## MintTap application
r/MintTapforYieldMax should not be optimized primarily for MintTap installs. Its durable value proposition should be YieldMax/income-investor problem solving: provenance, distribution/ROC interpretation, reconstruction methodology, broker-reporting discrepancies, split/reinvestment continuity, and related specialist questions.

MintTap-affiliated material should be disclosed as such. Product defects, alternative tools, contrary evidence, and corrections should not receive adverse moderation merely because they reduce conversion. Moderator actions must never be used as a growth lever against competing products or dissent.

A useful success ledger therefore includes: unresolved questions answered, corrections accepted, evidence gaps found, durable references improved, recurring terminology/problems learned, moderation burden, rule violations, member-originated useful contributions, and product handoffs. Installs/referrals are downstream outcomes, not moderation KPIs.

## LogMate / future niche apps
Do not create an operator-owned pilot community merely to manufacture launch distribution. Create one only if it has an independent professional-community purpose and can sustain neutral discussion of workflows, competing logbooks, migration failures, regulatory/recordkeeping questions, and product defects. The same governance contract is reusable for future niche apps.

## Operational implication
Owned community is neither ordinary owned media nor ordinary earned media. It is a governed trust surface. The operator controls infrastructure but should not control conclusions. Marketing may learn from community evidence, but moderation decisions must be based on published community rules and platform policy rather than acquisition objectives.

## Next evidence target
Audit r/MintTapforYieldMax itself: description, rules, disclosure, pinned material, moderator practices, competitor/criticism treatment, repeated-link behavior, and whether the community has a useful purpose independent of MintTap installation. Do not change the subreddit until the audit identifies a concrete governance gap.
