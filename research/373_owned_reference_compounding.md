# Research 373 — Owned Reference Compounding

Date: 2026-10-02
Status: VALIDATED

## Purpose
Define how a zero-budget niche app converts recurring community questions into durable owned references without treating communities as link-distribution channels or the blog as a publishing quota.

## Authoritative basis
- Google Search Central — Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content:
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
  Google recommends content created primarily to help an intended audience, with original information/analysis, clear sourcing and authorship, and warns against producing large amounts of search-first content.
- Google Search Central — Canonicalization:
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/canonicalization
  Google selects representative canonical URLs among duplicate or near-duplicate pages.
- Reddit Help — Spam (updated 2026-05-19):
  https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam
  Repeated or unsolicited mass engagement and repetitive mass posting for exposure are prohibited; community-specific rules and moderator judgment remain controlling.

## New synthesis
Do not operate community and search as separate content calendars.

Use one compounding loop:

recurring specialist problem → complete native answer → evidence and terminology capture → canonical owned reference → search discovery → product/claim feedback → maintained reference → community re-entry only when materially useful

Community discussion supplies real wording, edge cases and evidence gaps. The owned reference preserves the complete sourced answer at one maintainable URL. Search provides durable discovery without repeated community promotion. Product/support evidence creates maintenance triggers.

## IL0–IL9
IL0 Problem recurrence — require independent evidence that the specialist problem recurs.
IL1 Native sufficiency — community answers must remain useful without a click.
IL2 Evidence bundle — record authoritative sources, uncertainty, scope and date.
IL3 Original contribution — add synthesis, method, worked examples, maintained data or other substantive value.
IL4 Canonical discipline — one primary URL per materially identical problem; split only for genuinely different intent/workflow/evidence.
IL5 Authorship/provenance — make creator/reviewer, evidence process and purpose reasonably clear.
IL6 Claim/product linkage — connect relevant claims and product gaps to the Claim Registry/backlog.
IL7 Search observability — measure query/page discovery and qualified downstream behavior without treating ranking as user value.
IL8 Maintenance trigger — revalidate when source rules/data, product behavior or material community evidence changes.
IL9 Decision — KEEP / UPDATE / MERGE / SPLIT / RETIRE / HOLD-EVIDENCE.

## App implications
MintTap references should be problem-centered rather than mechanically ticker-centered. Candidate areas include distribution/ROC provenance, broker-reporting discrepancies, split/reinvestment reconstruction, ticker/date reconstruction, total-return methodology and bounded Tax Adjustment mechanics. Do not clone pages by ticker when the underlying problem and method are materially identical.

LogMate pre-launch references should center on real pilot jobs: import/migration continuity, duplicate reconciliation, Previous Total continuity, multi-leg entry, export/backup integrity and offline/PWA/device boundaries. Avoid generic pilot-productivity publishing merely to expand search surface.

## Measurement contract
Keep six layers separate:
1. recurring-problem evidence;
2. reference quality/freshness;
3. search discovery;
4. acquisition;
5. first/repeated specialist value;
6. sustainable non-intrusive monetization.

Search attribution is not incrementality. Sparse or censored evidence remains UNKNOWN.

## Stop rules
Merge, hold or stop when pages differ mainly by keyword/ticker, a page exists mainly because a keyword has volume, the page merely rewrites sources, the native answer is intentionally incomplete to force a click, or updating one canonical reference would serve users better.

## Reusable rule
Publish fewer maintained problem-solvers. Distribute answers, not links. Let recurring specialist evidence decide what deserves a durable URL.

## Next operational target
Audit MintTap's existing owned-reference inventory against IL0–IL9: recurring problem, canonical URL, evidence owner, last verification date, Claim Registry linkage, invalidation trigger, community evidence, search observability, downstream specialist value and decision.
