# Research 387 — Apple 2026 Creative Asset Surface Contract

Date: 2026-10-03

Apple's 2026 App Store changes expand creative inventory beyond the traditional icon, screenshots, and app previews. Apple says new creative assets can support product-page headers and search-result presentation, while current submission guidance now identifies product-page headers and search-result assets as built-in discoverability features.

## Strategic conclusion
Treat Store creative as a surface portfolio, not a screenshot set. More available surfaces do not justify more messages. Each surface should express a verified specialist promise and have a distinct role.

## JC0–JC9
specialist intent → verified atomic claim → surface eligibility → surface role → truthful product evidence → visual/copy continuity → localization/device validity → preview/review check → acquisition plus first/repeated specialist value → KEEP / REPAIR / RETIRE / HOLD-NOT-AVAILABLE.

Surface roles:
- Search result: immediate specialist relevance.
- Product-page header: brand/value framing.
- Screenshots: workflow/product proof.
- App preview: dynamic proof of real interaction.
- Custom product page: routing for materially distinct specialist intent.

## MintTap
First audit which new 2026 surfaces are actually available in App Store Connect. Map every live or proposed asset to the existing Claim Registry. Do not create separate creative systems merely for ticker variants.

## LogMate
Use new surfaces only after product evidence is launch-ready. Headers/search assets may establish professional identity; screenshots/previews should prove verified workflows rather than imply unsupported operational capability.

## Reusable rule
Admit a new Store surface only when it improves specialist comprehension or routing without creating unsupported claims or disproportionate maintenance cost.
