# Research 421 — Community Permission Is a Per-Community Contract, Not a Global Self-Promotion Ratio

Validated: 2026-10-04

## Decision

For zero-cost Reddit/community marketing, treat permission as a per-community, per-contribution contract. Do not use a platform-wide posting quota as a safe harbor and do not optimize for link volume.

## Current authoritative findings

Reddit's current spam policy prohibits repeated or unsolicited mass engagement, including repetitive mass posting for exposure or financial gain. It explicitly recommends genuine participation and checking each community's rules when business-linked activity could be perceived as spam.

Reddit's current moderator guidance says promotional content is not inherently spam, but individual communities may prohibit it entirely or use their own conventions (including a 10% convention). Community-specific rules and moderator judgment therefore outrank any numeric heuristic.

Reddit also recommends asking moderators of related communities before promoting a new community and mentioning it only when naturally relevant. This supports a permission-first operating model rather than cross-post distribution.

## IC0–IC9 Community Permission Contract

IC0 recurring specialist problem
→ IC1 current community-rule audit
→ IC2 native answer that is complete without a product link
→ IC3 material affiliation disclosure where relevant
→ IC4 explicit moderator permission when rules are ambiguous/restrictive
→ IC5 link-necessity test
→ IC6 repetition/cross-community similarity check
→ IC7 visibility state (visible / removed / filtered / unknown)
→ IC8 specialist-value evidence
→ IC9 re-entry decision.

States: NATIVE-ONLY / LINK-PERMITTED / ASK-MODS / HOLD-CENSORED / REPAIR / RETIRE.

## Operational implications

A contribution that produces no install can still be valuable if it yields specialist language, a validated problem, correction of a product assumption, or a durable owned-reference requirement. Conversely, installs do not excuse rule-breaking or repetitive promotion.

Do not copy substantially identical promotional posts across multiple communities. Do not use unsolicited DMs or chats as a substitute for community permission. Do not repost after filtering/removal merely to recover reach.

## MintTap

Use recurring YieldMax investor problems as the unit of participation: ROC provenance/state, broker-reporting mismatches, ticker/date reconstruction, and return methodology. Answer natively first. Link MintTap or an owned reference only when rules permit and the destination adds material evidence/workflow depth beyond the native answer.

The existing r/MintTapforYieldMax owned community is not evidence that external investing communities permit MintTap promotion. Maintain separate external-community rule/permission records.

## LogMate

Before launch, map pilot communities separately. Professional credibility around roster/import migration, duplicate handling, Previous Total continuity, offline/PWA constraints, and export integrity takes priority over referral volume. Product links should follow demonstrated relevance and community permission, not precede them.

## Reusable niche-app rule

The scalable asset is not a posting calendar. It is a permission ledger:

community | rule snapshot/date | promotional policy | disclosure expectation | moderator permission | recurring problem | native-answer status | link status | visibility | specialist evidence | next eligible re-entry

This converts community marketing from broadcast activity into governed specialist participation.
