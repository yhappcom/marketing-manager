# Research Index

This directory stores integrated, reusable Marketing Manager knowledge checkpoints.

## Artifact rule

A research file should represent a meaningful professional topic, not one isolated term or source. Prefer synthesis over accumulation.

Each substantial study should normally contain:

1. **Question / capability target** — what decision or diagnostic ability this research should improve;
2. **Definitions and mechanisms** — what the topic is and how it works;
3. **Historical / theoretical context** — why the model or practice exists when that context explains present behavior;
4. **Evidence** — authoritative sources, original research, platform documentation, or high-quality synthesis;
5. **Limitations / counterexamples** — boundary conditions and common misuse;
6. **Cross-domain connections** — product, design, web, economics, analytics, privacy, operations;
7. **Marketing judgment** — reusable decision principles supported by the evidence;
8. **Project implications** — only when a real product assignment exists;
9. **Open / validation / change-watch items** — what remains uncertain or time-sensitive.

## Evidence labels

- `SOURCE`
- `SYNTHESIS`
- `MARKETING JUDGMENT`
- `PROJECT DECISION`
- `OPEN`
- `DEPENDENCY`
- `VALIDATION`
- `CHANGE WATCH`

## Source recording

For time-sensitive material, record source owner, document/page title, URL, and access/review date. Prefer primary sources and original research. Community and practitioner evidence may be recorded when useful but should not silently become established fact.
